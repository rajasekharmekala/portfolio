11. Forensic interpretation before the references

The difference between a business description and an underwriting conclusion is measurement. Faze Three has a recognisable product set, an export channel and a description of vertical integration. Those facts explain how revenue can be generated, but they do not answer whether the company earns more than its cost of capital. The verified ROCE of 10.1% is the answer supplied by the current record, and it is below the 15% floor. That is why the dossier does not use the product catalogue as a reason to soften the verdict.

The sales series also needs careful reading. Sales rose to ₹923 Cr in FY26 and ₹940 Cr TTM, while PAT was ₹34 Cr in FY26 and ₹30 Cr TTM. OPM was 10% in FY26 and 8% TTM. A reader might reasonably ask whether the weak result is temporary. That is a valid research question, but it is not a verified recovery. The report therefore marks forward EPS, consensus target and three-year median P/E as N/A. A future report can replace those N/A values only after it retrieves an auditable estimate and states the period, source and assumptions.

Working capital is another place where a single number can mislead. Working-capital days improved to 20 in FY26, which suggests less cash is tied up in the operating cycle. Yet FCF was −₹62 Cr and borrowings were ₹275 Cr. The combined reading is WATCH, not PASS: the cycle improved, but the company still needs to demonstrate that investment spending and financing can be funded by durable operations. This is particularly relevant for an export manufacturer, where inventory and receivables can expand ahead of shipments.

The market regime is Downtrend as of 2026-10-09, which reinforces the stock-specific technical rejection. The technical record is not being used as a prediction engine. SMA means simple moving average, RSI means the relative-strength index, and P/E means price-to-earnings. SMA20, SMA50 and SMA200 are reference lines that show where price sits relative to its recent trend. Price at ₹408.30 is below all three, and the 50-day distance of −3.21 ATR makes the move materially more than a small pullback. RSI 35.66 says selling pressure has been persistent; it does not say a reversal is due. Volume at 1.48x adds participation information, but it does not turn distribution into accumulation. The framework therefore names SUPPORT_BROKEN and refuses a buy zone.

Definitions that matter

ROCE is operating profit relative to the capital employed in the business. It is useful here because the central question is whether factories, inventory and receivables earn enough. OPM is operating profit divided by sales; its decline from 18% in FY23 to 10% in FY26 shows that the extra sales did not carry the earlier margin. CFO is cash generated by operations, while FCF subtracts investment. The negative FY26 FCF means reported profit did not translate into freely distributable cash after investment. ATR is the average daily price range used as a volatility ruler, so −3.21 ATR below the 50-day average is a structural distance rather than a small fluctuation.

Stage 4 is the framework’s label for a markdown phase. It is not a statement that the company will fail. It is a statement that the price trend is not eligible for new tactical capital. That distinction matters because a weak share price can precede a recovery, but the framework requires evidence before allocating. The current evidence instead shows price below all three averages, negative relative strength and above-average volume on the run’s verified session. The report uses those facts to reject the timing, not to forecast the next daily bar.

The dossier also separates “not verified” from “negative.” Export concentration is N/A, not low or high. Debt-to-equity is N/A, not safe or unsafe. CFO/PAT is N/A, not zero. These labels make the report auditable: another reviewer can retrieve the missing filing and replace the boundary with a number. Until then, the hard failures already present remain sufficient for rejection.
