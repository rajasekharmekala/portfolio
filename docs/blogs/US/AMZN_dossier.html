2. Corporate Overview, Line of Business Architecture & Moat

Amazon operates three reporting segments: North America, International, and Amazon Web Services (AWS)[4]. The company's business model has transformed from a low-margin retail volume distributor into a high-margin, software- and infrastructure-driven economic machine powered by AWS cloud computing, digital advertising, 3P marketplace merchant services, and Prime membership subscriptions[5].

Amazon's unassailable multi-layered economic moat comprises:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Scale (AWS): The global leading cloud provider with massive developer mindshare, custom silicon integration (Trainium2, Inferentia2, Graviton4), and native AI foundation model hosting via Amazon Bedrock[6].
  • High-Margin Advertising Network: With billions of high-intent purchase searches per month, Amazon captures unmatched closed-loop ad conversion data, making its advertising unit a profit powerhouse[7].
  • Fulfillment & Delivery Logistics Barrier: A regionalized North American fulfillment network that delivers same-day or next-day packages to over 80% of US metropolitan areas at lower unit economics than any competitor[8].

3.1 AWS Cloud & Generative AI Architecture

Amazon Web Services (AWS) remains the world's most comprehensive and broadly adopted cloud platform. AWS's competitive advantage in generative AI is built on a 3-tier technology stack:

  • Bottom Layer (Compute & Infrastructure): Purpose-built custom silicon including Trainium2 chips (designed for training ultra-large foundation models with 4x faster training speed than Trainium1) and Inferentia2 chips (delivering up to 40% lower cost per inference). Major AI model builders including Anthropic utilize Trainium2 clusters comprising hundreds of thousands of interconnected chips across AWS UltraClusters.
  • Middle Layer (Foundation Models & Tools): Amazon Bedrock provides enterprise-grade serverless access to high-performing foundation models from AI21 Labs, Anthropic (Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Opus), Cohere, Meta (Llama 3), Mistral AI, and Amazon's proprietary Titan and Olympus models, wrapped with native guardrails, RAG integration, and enterprise security compliance.
  • Top Layer (Native AI Applications): Amazon Q, the generative AI-powered assistant for work and software development, capable of code generation, application modernization, business intelligence, and enterprise internal search integration.

3.2 Retail Inbound Logistics & Regionalization Economics

In 2023–2026, Amazon executed the most consequential structural overhaul of its North American fulfillment network since its inception, transitioning from a single national fulfillment network to eight self-contained geographic regions. Each regional hub operates with dedicated inventory placement, local sortation, and last-mile delivery fleets.

3.3 Digital Advertising Network & Prime Video Monetization

Amazon Advertising has transformed into the world's third-largest digital advertising network behind Alphabet and Meta. Unlike traditional search or social advertising, Amazon sits directly at the point of purchase with deterministic buyer intent data.

The monetization of Prime Video via default ad-supported tiers across 150+ million US viewers has unlocked over $7.5 billion in annual high-margin programmatic CTV advertising revenue, commanding premium CPMs ($32 to $40) due to Amazon's proprietary closed-loop audience attribution capabilities.
