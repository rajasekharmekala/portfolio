Executive Summary & Investment Verdict

The GraniteShares 2x Long QCOM Daily ETF (NASDAQ: QCML) is a single-stock leveraged exchange-traded fund seeking to deliver 200% of the daily percentage performance of Qualcomm Incorporated (NASDAQ: QCOM)[1]. Qualcomm itself is an elite global semiconductor powerhouse leading the mobile application processor market, automotive digital chassis growth, and AI-enabled Windows Copilot+ PC architectures[2]. However, utilizing QCML as a multi-month holding vehicle introduces substantial structural friction: a high 1.50% annual expense ratio, swap financing borrowing costs, and compounding path-dependency that causes returns to lag pure 2X Qualcomm equity performance over extended holding periods[3].

While QCML has experienced less severe volatility decay than extreme crypto-proxy ETFs like MSTX (due to Qualcomm's moderate 28%–35% historical annualized volatility), its trade geometry offers an Asymmetry Ratio of only 2.83:1 (Target: $28.50 vs Stop: $19.50), failing the mandatory 3.5:1 hurdle required for high-risk tactical leveraged satellites[4]. Institutional capital is substantially better deployed either directly in spot QCOM common stock (which trades without fee drag and pays an attractive dividend yield) or in defined-risk Bull Call options spreads.

Under our institutional 3-Layer Underwriting Framework, QCML is assigned a 🔴 REPLACE_UNDERPERFORMER verdict. Active Robinhood holdings should be liquidated and redeployed into spot Qualcomm common shares or allocated toward top-ranked institutional compounders (ANET / VRT) per Swap Matchup 18.

1. Fund History & Commercial Purpose

QCML was launched by GraniteShares on February 13, 2025, to provide institutional and retail traders with an amplified vehicle to trade semiconductor earnings cycles, AI PC product announcements, and mobile handset replacement inflection points[5]. The fund utilizes daily rebalanced swap agreements backed by short-term cash collateral.

2. Derivative Architecture & Swap Counterparties

QCML secures its 2X daily leverage primarily through over-the-counter swap contracts with Cowen and Nomura Securities, collateralized by U.S. Treasury Bills and custodial cash deposits[6].
