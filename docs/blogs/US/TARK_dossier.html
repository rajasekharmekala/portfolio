1 Overview and Structural History

The Tradr 2X Long Innovation ETF (NASDAQ: TARK), originally launched as the AXS 2X Innovation ETF, is a leveraged exchange-traded fund issued by Investment Managers Series Trust II and managed by AXS Investments / Tradr ETFs.[1] Incepted on May 2, 2022, TARK seeks daily investment results, before fees and operating expenses, that correspond to 200% (2.0x) of the single-day performance of the ARK Innovation ETF (NYSE Arca: ARKK), the flagship actively-managed thematic technology fund founded by Cathie Wood.[2]

TARK is engineered to magnify the daily price swings of disruptive innovation themes, including genomics, artificial intelligence, autonomous mobility, next-generation internet, and financial technology.[3] The fund achieves its 2.0x target through bilateral OTC swap agreements referencing ARKK rather than individual common stocks.[10] However, because ARKK itself is an actively-managed fund that trades with high portfolio turnover, speculative valuations, and high constituent beta, layering a 2X daily reset on top of an already volatile underlying fund creates extreme path-dependent compounding decay.[11]

As of September 2026, TARK manages a modest $22.34 million in net assets, with thin daily trading volume (average volume ~16,200 shares) and wide bid-ask spreads (0.35%–0.60%).[5] The fund carries an exorbitant gross expense ratio of 1.48% (net 1.15%), representing one of the costliest management fee burdens in the US ETF ecosystem.[6]

2 Derivative Architecture & Active Thematic Volatility

TARK’s structural mechanics involve daily swap agreements entered into with major global broker-dealers.[12] Unlike broad index ETFs where the underlying benchmark remains static between quarterly rebalances, ARKK's portfolio manager actively trades constituent equities daily, frequently selling winning compounders (such as NVIDIA) and concentrating capital into underperforming, unprofitable turnaround names.[13]

This dynamic creates severe analytical friction for institutional allocators. When combined with 200% daily leverage, any sharp drawdown in ARKK's speculative constituents (which routinely experience 35%–50% annualized volatility) triggers catastrophic volatility drag.[14] Over the trailing 12-month period, TARK declined -23.40%, and over the trailing 5-year cycle, the fund has destroyed -33.03% of investor capital despite broad equity bull markets.[15]
