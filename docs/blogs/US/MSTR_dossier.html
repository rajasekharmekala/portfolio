1 Executive Summary & Investment Thesis

MicroStrategy Incorporated (NASDAQ: MSTR) is the world's premier corporate Bitcoin development company and enterprise analytics software vendor, pioneering an institutional treasury reserve strategy that leverages capital markets arbitrage to accumulate Bitcoin accretively for shareholders. Led by Executive Chairman Michael Saylor and CEO Phong Le, MicroStrategy holds over 500,000 Bitcoins valued in excess of $45 billion, transforming the equity into an institutional-grade, leveraged proxy on digital asset adoption.

Primary Investment Thesis: MSTR trades at a persistent premium to its net asset value (mNAV ~1.25–1.40x), enabling management to issue at-the-market (ATM) equity and low-coupon (0%–0.875%) convertible senior notes to purchase spot Bitcoin. This reflexive capital markets flywheel generates positive "BTC Yield"—delivering double-digit percentage increases in Bitcoin holdings per diluted share without proportional shareholder dilution.

2 Bitcoin Treasury Flywheel & Capital Markets Model

The core economic mechanism governing MSTR operates via institutional volatility monetization:

  • Volatility Arbitrage: Convertible debt hedge funds purchase MSTR convertible notes with deeply negative yields-to-maturity or ultra-low coupons in order to delta-hedge the underlying equity's immense implied volatility (IV > 80%).
  • Reflexive mNAV Accretion: When MSTR shares trade at a premium to the underlying Bitcoin spot value, issuing equity through the ATM facility to purchase spot BTC directly increases the NAV per share for existing holders.
  • Long-Duration Capital Structure: The company's $6.77 billion in convertible notes carry extended maturities (ranging from 2027 to 2032) with no maintenance debt covenants and zero forced-liquidation margin calls regardless of Bitcoin price drawdowns.
