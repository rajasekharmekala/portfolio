2. What the Company Does: ETF Wrapper Mechanics, Operating Segments & Real-World Examples

Because NBIL is a single-stock leveraged exchange-traded fund, understanding "what the company does" requires mapping two completely different operational machines. At the top layer, GraniteShares 2x Long NBIS Daily ETF (NBIL) operates as a regulated investment vehicle holding just 4 portfolio positions as of November 2026 [1]. Rather than holding physical shares of Nebius Group N.V. in a 2-to-1 margin account, NBIL enters into over-the-counter Total Return Swaps (TRS) with institutional prime brokers and swap dealers (such as Clear Street), backed by cash and U.S. Treasury equivalents posted as collateral, with FLEX or listed options available as backup liquidity instruments [2][18]. Meanwhile, NBIL pays the counterparty a floating financing rate (tied to SOFR plus a spread) and absorbs the fund's 1.41% net (1.50% gross cap) operating expense ratio [1][2][19].

Alongside two major unconsolidated equity-method or minority stakes in Toloka AI and ClickHouse [4][15]. In reality, Nebius Group has rapidly converged into a single-engine AI infrastructure business: by Q3 2026, the core Nebius AI Cloud segment generated $973.8 million out of $980.8 million in total consolidated revenue.
