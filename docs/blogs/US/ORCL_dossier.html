1 Executive Summary & Investment Thesis

Oracle Corporation (NYSE/NASDAQ: ORCL) is a premier market participant operating in the Cloud, Enterprise Software & Cybersecurity domain, specifically focused on AI Infrastructure & Hyperscale Cloud[1]. The company exhibits high returns on invested capital with an audited ROIC of ~9.0% and robust cash flow conversion generating an FCF margin of −33.9%[2].

Primary Investment Thesis: OCI Gen2 growth (+77% FY2026; +93% Q4), RPO backlog of $638B at FY2026 year-end.[3]. The company possesses an enduring economic moat characterized by: Mission-critical enterprise database lock-in with massive multi-cloud OCI Gen2 AI datacenter contracts (OpenAI, Microsoft, NVIDIA).[4].
