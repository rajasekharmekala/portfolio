1 Overview and Fund Identity

The ProShares UltraPro Dow30 (NYSE Arca: UDOW) is an exchange-traded fund issued by ProShares Trust and managed by ProShare Advisors LLC.[1] Incepted on February 11, 2010, UDOW is the only US-listed ETF engineered to provide magnified 300% (3.0x) daily investment results tracking the world-renowned Dow Jones Industrial Average (DJIA; Bloomberg Ticker: INDU).[2] The underlying benchmark serves as the historic barometer of American enterprise, consisting of 30 prominent, dividend-paying blue-chip industrial, consumer, financial, and technology stalwarts.[3]

Operating as an open-end management investment company registered under the Investment Company Act of 1940, UDOW achieves its investment objective by deploying daily rebalanced OTC total return swap contracts with major Wall Street banking institutions alongside physical investments in Dow constituent common stocks and DJIA futures.[10] Unlike broad market cap-weighted indices dominated purely by mega-cap technology, the Dow Jones Industrial Average maintains significant exposure to traditional industrial manufacturing, defense aerospace, healthcare, and retail titans.[11]

As of September 2026, UDOW oversees approximately $864.50 million in net assets with an average daily trading volume of $95 million.[5] It maintains a net annual expense ratio of 0.95% and offers an annual dividend distribution yield of 1.07%.[6]

2 Price-Weighted Index Mechanics & Derivative Structure

A distinctive feature of UDOW is its underlying benchmark's price-weighted calculation methodology. Unlike market-cap weighted indices (such as the S&P 500 or Nasdaq-100), the Dow Jones Industrial Average weights its 30 constituents based on their absolute nominal share price divided by the proprietary Dow Divisor (approximately 0.1517).[12] Consequently, higher-priced stocks (such as UnitedHealth Group, Goldman Sachs, and Microsoft) exert a significantly larger influence on daily index points than lower-priced members (such as Intel or Coca-Cola).[13]

ProShare Advisors achieves 3.0x daily exposure by establishing OTC swap contracts with primary dealers including Barclays, BNP Paribas, Citibank, Goldman Sachs, and Morgan Stanley.[14] The fund pays floating SOFR financing costs and receives the 3X gross total return of the DJIA. Because the Dow Jones Industrial Average exhibits lower annualized historical volatility (15%–18%) compared to tech-heavy benchmarks (Nasdaq-100 volatility of 22%–28%), UDOW experiences comparatively less volatility decay during moderate choppy market environments.[15]
