From a Somerset Public Library to the Cloud Control Plane

Dr. Tianyi "TJ" Jiang and Xunkai "Kai" Gong founded AvePoint, Inc. in July 2001 in Somerset, New Jersey [18], [19]. They wrote their early enterprise infrastructure code out of a public library during the dot-com downturn [18], [19]. Most startups of that era chased consumer internet traffic. Jiang and Gong instead targeted an unglamorous enterprise bottleneck inside Microsoft SharePoint. As large corporations adopted SharePoint for document collaboration, native admin tools fell short. IT teams lacked granular backup, item-level recovery, content migration, and permissions auditing. AvePoint's flagship on-premises suite, DocAve, solved those exact operational failures. That focus let the company compound profitably for nearly two decades before tapping public equity markets [19].

Between 2001 and 2020, AvePoint survived three technology and capital cycles without losing founder control [19]. Kai Gong served as CEO until 2009, when he and TJ Jiang became Co-CEOs. In 2020, Jiang became sole CEO while Gong moved to Executive Chairman [19]. Both co-founders still hold those roles today. Along the way, AvePoint took minority growth capital from Summit Partners, Goldman Sachs, and TPG Sixth Street Partners. That capital funded expansion across North America, EMEA, and APAC. It also anchored Singapore as AvePoint's Asia headquarters and a major international R&D hub [13], [19]. When Microsoft shifted from on-premises SharePoint to Office 365, AvePoint rebuilt DocAve for multi-tenant cloud APIs early enough to capture the enterprise migration wave [19]. That early cloud rewrite turned a legacy SharePoint toolmaker into a global SaaS platform [19].
