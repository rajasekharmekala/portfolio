1. Executive Summary & Investment Pitch

American Express operates a closed-loop payments and lending system that combines issuer, network and merchant data. The company earns discount revenue when cardmembers spend, net card fees from premium products, interest income on revolving balances and service fees from travel, loyalty and corporate programs. Because the same platform sees authorization, cardmember and merchant information, it can personalize rewards, manage credit lines and identify fraud. The trade-off is that AXP retains credit, funding, regulatory and acceptance risks that Visa and Mastercard largely pass to issuing banks.[1]
