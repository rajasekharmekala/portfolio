Identity and operating model

Apollo Pipes sells a family of pipes, fittings, tanks, solvent products and newer adjacent building-material lines. Its own overview describes eight plants, 2,26,000 tonnes of capacity, more than 1,000 channel partners, more than 10,000 customer touch points and over 2,600 SKUs[16]. That footprint matters because polymer pipes are bulky: distribution density can reduce freight friction and improve availability. It is not, however, a moat by itself; the return on the capital required to operate that footprint is the test used later.
