1. Company Profile & Infrastructure Capabilities

KNR Constructions Limited (NSE: KNRCON, BSE: 539150) is an infrastructure construction, development, and management company based in Agra, Uttar Pradesh[1]. The company specializes in the execution of large-scale highway, expressway, bridge, flyover, and airport runway projects on Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) and Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM) / Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) bases[3]. In recent years, PNC diversified into rural drinking water supply projects under the Government of India's Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) in Uttar Pradesh[11].

2. Fatal Flaw: The CBI Bribery Case & 3-Year NHAI Debarment

In June 2024, the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) registered a criminal case and conducted nationwide raids involving senior officials of the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) and KNR Constructions executives regarding alleged bribery to obtain completion certificates for the Jhansi-Jhansi highway project without penal deductions[12].

Following the CBI chargesheet, the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) and NHAI issued an official order on October 18, 2024, disqualifying and debarring KNR Constructions Limited and its subsidiaries from participating in any tenders invited by MoRTH, NHAI, or other road-sector agencies for a period of three full years (from October 18, 2024 to October 17, 2027)[10]. While the Delhi High Court granted interim relief allowing execution of ongoing projects, PNC is legally barred from bidding on fresh NHAI tenders—which historically constituted 70% to 80% of its order book additions[13].

⛔ Governance Fatal Flaw Disqualification: Under Section 2.1 of the Institutional Underwriting Mandate, active criminal charges involving bribery of regulatory officials coupled with formal central government procurement debarment represent an absolute binary disqualifier, regardless of headline P/E or optical asset discounts.

3. Asset Monetization & HAM Project Portfolio

In an attempt to reduce consolidated debt and unlock equity capital, KNR Constructions executed a definitive share purchase agreement with Highways Infrastructure Trust (HIT, backed by KKR) to monetize 12 operational road projects (11 HAM and 1 BOT-Annuity asset) for an enterprise value of ~₹9,005 Crore and equity value of ~₹2,900 Crore[14]. However, the regulatory debarment and ongoing CBI proceedings have introduced significant friction and extended delays in obtaining statutory NOCs from NHAI and lender consortia for concluding the divestment tranches[15].
