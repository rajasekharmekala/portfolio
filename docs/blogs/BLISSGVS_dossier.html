What the company does

Bliss GVS Pharma makes medicines in forms that are not swallowed. Its core products are suppositories and pessaries, which are inserted into the body and dissolve to release the drug. The company describes itself as engaged in manufacturing, marketing, trading and export of pharmaceutical formulations in the form of suppositories, pessaries, capsules, tablets and syrups, and it was incorporated in 1984[1]. A company can be a good business and a poor holding at a particular price, and the framework is built to say so.

Glossary

ATR (average true range) is the average daily price swing over 14 sessions, in rupees. One ATR is a normal day's movement, and it measures how far a price is from a level. Asymmetry is the ratio of reward to risk, measured against a stop. CFO (cash from operations) is the cash a business generates from its trading before spending on assets. CFO / PAT divides that cash by profit after tax. Debt to equity divides borrowings by owners' funds. Pledge is shares given as security for a loan, which the lender can sell if the loan is not repaid. Stage 2 is a rising markup phase in a stock's price cycle.
