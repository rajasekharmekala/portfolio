1 Executive Summary & Investment Thesis

iShares China Large-Cap ETF (NYSE Arca: FXI) is the premier institutional benchmark vehicle providing targeted exposure to the 50 largest and most liquid Chinese equities trading on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (SEHK). Managed by BlackRock Fund Advisors, the fund holds approximately $4.06 billion in net assets and offers direct participation in the structural monetary and fiscal reflation cycle orchestrated by the People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the Ministry of Finance.

Primary Investment Thesis: Chinese large-cap equities trade at historic valuation discounts (P/E 10.25x vs 22.5x S&P 500) while offering superior dividend yields (2.01%). Aggressive PBOC liquidity injections, Central Huijin equity purchases, and targeted fiscal stimulus provide a strong macroeconomic floor. The ETF's high concentration in mega-cap technology champions (Tencent, Alibaba, Meituan) and Tier-1 state banks (CCB, ICBC) creates an asymmetric vehicle to capture Chinese equity multiple re-rating without single-stock idiosyncratic regulatory risk.

2 Fund Architecture & Index Methodology

FXI tracks the market-cap weighted FTSE China 50 Index, applying strict liquidity, free-float, and foreign ownership eligibility screening:

  • Constituent Universe: 50 largest Chinese companies listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange as H-Shares, Red Chips, and P-Chips.
  • Capping Methodology: Quarterly rebalancing applies a 9% single-stock capping rule to prevent excessive concentration while preserving capital efficiency.
  • Creation/Redemption Efficiency: Average daily trading volume exceeding 27.5 million shares ensures tight bid-ask spreads (0.01%–0.02%) and negligible tracking error relative to NAV.
