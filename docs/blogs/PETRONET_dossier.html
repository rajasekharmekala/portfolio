Accounting definitions and reading guide

Financial statements use different lenses for the same enterprise. Sales includes the value of LNG that passes through the terminal, while operating profit is closer to the fee-and-spread cushion left after operating costs. Cash from operations adds back non-cash expenses and adjusts working capital, so it is the best bridge between the income statement and money available to fund expansion or dividends. The figures in this report come from the consolidated Screener series and the company's published financial-results archive; they are not blended with a different standalone basis unless explicitly labelled. This distinction matters because a high-volume terminal can report a large revenue number without earning a commodity producer's margin.

ROCE is computed by the source as a return on capital employed, and ROE relates profit to shareholder capital. D/E here uses borrowings divided by equity capital plus reserves because that is the deterministic balance-sheet arithmetic available in the fetched table. These definitions are simple enough to reproduce, but they are not interchangeable: a firm can show high ROE because of low equity while ROCE remains moderate. Petronet's two returns are both comfortably above the hard gate, which means the rejection is not a disguised quality judgement. It follows from the entry price and stop rule.

What this means for an owner: You can trust the direction of the balance-sheet and cash tests, but you should repeat the same definitions each quarter. If the source changes its presentation or a subsidiary becomes material, compare like with like before changing the verdict.

Terminal economics

A regasification terminal earns by making capacity available and processing gas reliably. The revenue stream therefore has two layers: contracted capacity or throughput and ancillary services such as unloading, storage and reload. The first layer supports predictability; the second can add optionality but is more exposed to customer mix. Dahej is the larger platform and Kochi broadens geographic reach, so the portfolio has scale with a different local demand footprint. The practical question is not whether India needs imported gas in the abstract, but whether customers are willing to book capacity at fees that cover maintenance, interest and expansion.

Utilisation is the operational statistic that converts installed MMTPA into earnings power. A terminal with low utilisation still carries depreciation and safety costs, which means fixed costs are spread across fewer units. The public pages confirm capacity and terminals but do not provide a single, consistently defined as-of utilisation figure for the exact 8 October bar. That is why utilisation remains WATCH rather than being assigned a guessed percentage. A subsequent investor presentation showing sustained high utilisation and contracted growth would upgrade this KPI; a fall in throughput or a material take-or-pay dispute would downgrade it.

What this means for you: Read capacity expansion as an option, not a profit. The option pays only when contracts and physical demand fill the added lanes, so the next bridge is contract quality and cash conversion.

Demand, policy and supply chain

India's gas system combines imported LNG with domestic production, pipeline transport, city-gas distribution, fertiliser, power and industrial demand. Government and PPAC datasets are useful because they show the policy setting around gas consumption, but they do not guarantee a terminal's utilisation. Petronet's strategic importance follows from the location of its terminals and the public-sector shareholding, while its shareholder return depends on negotiated tariffs, volumes, reliability and capital discipline. This separation prevents a national-energy narrative from becoming an unsupported earnings forecast.

LNG supply chains are exposed to shipping schedules, weather, boil-off management, regasification availability and global price spreads. Much of the commodity value can pass through, yet timing can still change working capital and customer affordability. A cold winter or supply interruption can raise spot prices and suppress discretionary demand; conversely, lower landed LNG prices can encourage industrial offtake. The report therefore treats domestic gas-demand inflection as a catalyst to monitor, not a confirmed target input.

What this means for you: The moat is infrastructure access and operating reliability, not control over the LNG molecule. Evidence of booked volumes and stable fee income matters more than a headline global LNG price.

Why the allocation rule comes first

A research dossier can contain many encouraging facts and still produce a rejection. The framework is designed this way because capital is scarce and a tactical position has a defined loss boundary. ROCE describes the business, D/E describes solvency, and CFO/PAT describes cash backing; asymmetry describes whether the proposed entry pays enough for being wrong. Mixing these questions would let a good company excuse a bad entry, which is the exact error the hard-gate sequence prevents.

At the supplied close, the trade risks ₹13.45 to the stop and seeks ₹39.15 to the measured base, so the ratio is 2.91. The threshold is 3.5, not 3.0, because a broad Downtrend raises the probability of failed breakouts, slippage and repeated small losses. The result is not a prediction that PETRONET must fall. It is a statement that the available reward does not compensate for the specified risk at this exact price.

The rule also protects against target inflation. Using the bull scenario of ₹353 would produce a ratio above the floor, but the bull figure is not the verified technical base and the three-year median P/E is unavailable. It would therefore be an unverified target. The measured base of ₹327.65 is conservative, reproducible and sufficient to show why patience is required.

What this means for you: The rejection is a position-sizing decision, not a company obituary. A lower entry, stronger verified target or changed regime can reopen the case; until one occurs, the capital stays in cash.
