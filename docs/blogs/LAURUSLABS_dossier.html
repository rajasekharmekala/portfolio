13. Glossary for a new owner

ROCE is return on capital employed: the operating return earned on money tied into the business. ROE is return on equity: the profit generated for shareholders’ capital. OPM is operating profit margin, the share of revenue left after operating costs but before interest and tax. CFO/PAT compares operating cash flow with accounting profit; a ratio above one means cash exceeded reported profit over the chosen period.

CDMO means contract development and manufacturing organisation: a customer pays the manufacturer to develop or produce a molecule or formulation. API is the active pharmaceutical ingredient; FDF is the finished dosage form a patient receives. Stage 2 describes a stock above a rising medium- and long-term average. RSI measures recent price speed, while ATR measures typical price movement. None predicts the future alone.

Asymmetry is the possible gain divided by the defined loss. The formula used here is (target−CMP)/(CMP−stop). A 3.5:1 floor means the target must offer at least three and a half units of potential gain for each unit of risk. Support is a price area where prior buying may reappear; it is not guaranteed. Shortlist means a candidate eligible for allocation only after all gates pass. LAURUSLABS is not eligible at this observation because the asymmetry gate fails.

14. Unit economics and margin bridge

Unit economics in a pharmaceutical manufacturer are not a simple price-minus-materials equation. A molecule moves through development, validation, quality release, packaging, regulatory filing and customer supply, so the economic return depends on yield, batch size, rejection rates, cycle time and the amount of capital held in inventory. Laurus does not publish a single group-wide contribution margin that can be used as a universal unit-cost proxy in the supplied evidence. That metric is therefore N/A. The safer observable is the consolidated operating margin: 15% in FY24, 19% in FY25, 26% in FY26 and 28% TTM.[5] The direction is positive, but a group margin can hide mix changes between APIs, formulations and CDMO work.

A margin bridge should be read as a chain of causes. Higher-value CDMO and formulations can lift mix; better plant utilisation can spread fixed costs; lower input or freight costs can help temporarily; and pricing pressure can reverse the benefit. The FY26 OPM improvement of seven percentage points from FY25 is substantial, but it is not automatically permanent. A prudent owner asks whether the next quarter holds the 26% FY26 level while sales continue to grow, rather than extrapolating the TTM 28% figure. The trigger in this dossier is OPM below 24% for two quarters, because that would show that the recovery cushion is narrowing.

The implication is that Laurus has operating leverage, which is useful when the pipeline converts, but dangerous when a project slips. The same fixed assets that improve margins at good utilisation dilute returns when underused. This is why the capital-work-in-progress figure of ₹773 crore and inventory days of 317 are monitored beside OPM. A margin result without cash release is incomplete evidence. The next review should reconcile margin, inventory and CFO in one bridge, not celebrate any one number alone.

What this means for you: The evidence supports monitoring, not improvisation. The next review should change the verdict only when the stated metric and trigger move together.

20. What the annual report can and cannot prove

An annual report is a record of what happened, how management describes the business and what accounting policies were applied. It is not a guarantee of next year’s revenue. Laurus’s FY26 report is valuable because it provides a common frame for segment revenue, manufacturing investments, people, research capability and sustainability disclosures. The investor highlights page adds a concise segment bridge: CDMO at ₹2,080 crore and Affordable Medicines at ₹4,733 crore.[1][2] Those figures support the transition narrative, but they do not by themselves establish customer retention, project probability or future return on capital.

The financial-results archive provides the next level of verification: quarterly sales, profit, cash flow and board disclosures. A disciplined reader compares the quarter with the prior year and the immediately preceding quarter. A single strong quarter can be affected by product mix, shipment timing or one-off items. The durable signal is a sequence in which revenue growth, margin, cash conversion and working-capital discipline improve together. This dossier therefore treats FY26 as evidence of improvement and keeps the forward target unverified.

The governance and shareholding pages serve a different purpose. They help identify ownership changes, board actions and disclosure timing, but they are not substitutes for the audited statements. Exchange filings are the right place to confirm a material event, and the FDA documents are the right place to interpret a regulatory process. Keeping those source roles separate reduces the risk of quoting a secondary summary as if it were a primary fact.
