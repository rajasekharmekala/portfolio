Management questions and falsification tests

Management commentary is most useful when it can be falsified. If management says premiumisation will lift margins, the test is premium mix, realisation per case and margin after marketing over multiple reporting periods. If management says capacity is ready for growth, the test is utilisation, capex, bottling throughput and inventory turnover. If management says leverage is comfortable, the test is gross debt, lease liabilities, interest cover and cash after capex. The transcript and presentation should be read for hypotheses; audited statements decide whether the hypotheses passed.[5][6]

In this case, structure receives more weight because the strategy under review is tactical momentum and the rule requires Stage 2.
