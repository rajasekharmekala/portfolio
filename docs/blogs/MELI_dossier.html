2. Corporate overview and competitive moat

MercadoLibre was incorporated in 1999 and headquartered in Montevideo. Its marketplace creates a two-sided network: merchants improve assortment, buyers improve demand and advertising inventory, and transaction density improves logistics and payments economics. Mercado Pago adds wallet, payment acceptance, credit and investment layers; Mercado Envios supports delivery.[3][16]

The moat is principally network density, data and integrated logistics rather than a single patent claim. More transactions can improve underwriting data and fulfillment utilization, but integration creates regulatory, credit, currency, cybersecurity and funding risks. Annual risk factors discuss competition, consumer protection, financial regulation, credit losses, liquidity and country exposure.[3][31]

Methodology, definitions and evidence controls

This dossier separates observed facts, calculated ratios and analyst judgments. Observed facts come from linked filings or market pages. Calculations require defined numerators and denominators. Missing observations remain N/A rather than being inferred.

Revenue and GMV

Revenue is accounting-recognized amount; GMV is transaction value before recognition. GMV growth does not automatically equal revenue growth because take rates, incentives, shipping presentation and foreign exchange differ.

Cash flow and FCF

Operating cash flow is cash generated by operations. Free cash flow is operating cash flow less capital expenditures; payment settlement and restricted balances require review before treating OCF as distributable cash.

ROIC and ROE

ROIC compares after-tax operating profit with average operating capital; ROE compares net income with average equity. Financial-platform liabilities require explicit conventions.

SBC dilution

Stock-based compensation is non-cash expense that can dilute shareholders. SBC/revenue is a monitoring ratio, not proof that dilution is immaterial.

Take rates

Commerce take rate is commerce revenue divided by GMV; fintech take rate is fintech revenue divided by TPV. Segment definitions and incentives must match.

Credit quality

Credit portfolio is outstanding receivables; NPL over 90 days is delinquency. Pair NPL with vintage losses, coverage, recoveries, write-offs and portfolio growth.

Shipping penetration

Managed-network penetration is the share of orders controlled through company logistics. Higher penetration can improve delivery while increasing fixed costs and capex.

Technical indicators

RSI measures recent price momentum; moving averages summarize prior closes; ATR estimates trading range. None proves valuation or business quality.

Weinstein stages

Stage 1 is a base, Stage 2 an advance, Stage 3 a top and Stage 4 a decline. Classification needs trend slope and volume context.

13F and Form 4

13F is a lagged report of certain institutional long positions. Form 4 transactions must distinguish open-market purchases from grants, exercises and tax withholding.

Short interest

Short interest is a point-in-time reported short position and differs from daily short-sale volume. Current percentages are withheld when date and float are not verified.

DCF discipline

A DCF specifies forecast period, growth, margin, reinvestment, terminal growth and discount rate. Sensitivity analysis is more informative than a single precise value.

Forward EPS multiple

Forward EPS multiplied by a verified three-year median P/E is admissible only when EPS period, share count and multiple series are reproducible.

Peer comparability

AMZN, SE, NU, STNE and CPNG overlap with MELI but differ in geography, accounting mix, funding and maturity; multiples are directional context.

Portfolio action taxonomy

Hold preserves exposure within risk limits. Accumulate requires passing hard gates and support-aware asymmetry. Trim or exit requires an invalidation condition.

Refresh protocol

Refresh the 10-K, latest 10-Q, earnings 8-K, price series, ownership filings and workbook before changing orders. Historical values are not silently backfilled.

Glossary for non-specialist readers

GAAP

Generally Accepted Accounting Principles, the accounting framework used in the audited statements.

GMV

Gross merchandise volume, the value of goods transacted before recognizing company revenue.

TPV

Total payment volume, the value processed through payment rails under the issuer’s disclosed perimeter.

NPL

Non-performing loan; this report uses the requested over-90-days-past-due concept when disclosed.

Take rate

Revenue divided by the relevant transaction base, such as GMV or TPV.

SBC

Stock-based compensation, an expense that can dilute shareholders even though it is non-cash at grant.

FCF

Free cash flow, conventionally operating cash flow less capital expenditure.

ROIC

Return on invested capital, after-tax operating return relative to average operating capital.

ATR

Average true range, a volatility measure used to express support distance consistently.

13F

Quarterly institutional holdings filing with a reporting lag and limited security coverage.

Form 4

Insider transaction filing; transaction codes determine whether activity is a purchase, grant, exercise or withholding.

DCF

Discounted cash flow, a valuation method that discounts forecast cash flows and a terminal value.

Stage 2

A technical regime characterized by an advancing trend; it requires trend and volume evidence.

Asymmetry ratio

Reward-to-risk calculation: target minus entry divided by entry minus stop.

Core Alpha

The strategy bucket requiring durable economics, financial quality, technical confirmation and at least 3.0x reward-to-risk.
