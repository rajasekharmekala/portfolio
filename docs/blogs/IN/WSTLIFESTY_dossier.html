2. What the company does, in plain English

Westlife runs 482 McDonald's restaurants in 79 cities[17]. It pays McDonald's for the brand and playbook and keeps the restaurant profits. It sells burgers, fries and delivery meals; about 74% of sales come through digital channels and the app has passed 55 million downloads[15]. The core growth story has two parts: open more stores (over 60 in FY27, 580–630 by December 2027)[17] and sell more per existing store (same-store sales growth, SSSG).
