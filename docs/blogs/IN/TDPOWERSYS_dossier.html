The developer decides to build an on-site, behind-the-meter natural gas generation island consisting of eight 12.5 MW industrial gas turbines. CONCRETE EXAMPLE 2: THE CIRCULAR BIOMASS COGENERATION ALTERNATOR (MAHARASHTRA, INDIA)

Coupling to a Triveni steam turbine in a 5,000 TCD integrated sugar and ethanol complex

Triveni couples its turbine to a TDPS 35 MW, 11 kV, 4-pole synchronous alternator. Because bagasse combustion produces fluctuating steam pressure, the TDPS generator is equipped with a digital brushless excitation system and automatic voltage regulator (AVR) that modulates excitation current in milliseconds, maintaining grid voltage stability despite variable boiler pressure.

TDPS books ₹14 crore for the alternator. At the same time, securing a 15-year aftermarket service contract for scheduled insulation resistance testing, bearing maintenance. In addition, spare stator coils.
