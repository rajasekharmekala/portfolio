0. Executive Summary & Investment Thesis

Global Health Limited (operating under the flagship brand Medanta – The Medicity) is one of India's largest and most respected private quaternary healthcare providers, dominating the North and East Indian healthcare corridors[1]. Founded in 2004 by Dr. Naresh Trehan, an internationally renowned cardiovascular surgeon, Medanta operates 6 multi-specialty hospitals with over 3,400 installed beds across Gurugram, Lucknow, Patna, Indore, Ranchi, and Noida, staffed by over 2,000 specialist physicians[2].

Unlike regional peers who compete primarily on volume in secondary cities, Medanta has structured its clinical delivery model around high-complexity, doctor-led quaternary care, encompassing advanced organ transplantation, robotic uro-oncology, complex neurosurgery, and interventional cardiology[3]. This focus allows Medanta to command an industry-leading Average Revenue Per Occupied Bed (ARPOB) of ~₹62,500 per day, generating sustainable consolidated EBITDA margins of 24%–26%[4].

Our underwriting evaluation qualifies Medanta as a high-conviction Core Alpha compounder:

  1. Mature Hub Cash Generation: The flagship Gurugram facility (1,400+ beds) functions as a high-margin cash engine, generating over ₹550 crore in annual operating EBITDA at ~28% margin, entirely funding group-level greenfield capex[5].
  2. Rapid Ramp-Up of Developing Units: The Lucknow (950 beds) and Patna (650 beds) hospitals have transitioned from gestation into high-profitability contributors, with Patna achieving positive EBITDA within 9 months of commissioning and now operating at 24%+ OPM[6].
  3. Conservative Balance Sheet: Debt-to-Equity is low at 0.26x with Net Debt / EBITDA below 0.6x, enabling the company to fund its 550-bed Noida hospital and 400-bed South Delhi expansion via internal accruals[7].

1. Corporate History & Clinical Philosophy

Medanta was conceived in 2004 with a vision to build an integrated academic medical center in India combining quaternary clinical care, medical research, and education[8]. The flagship Gurugram campus, spread across 43 acres, opened in 2009 and quickly established global benchmarks in organ transplantation and complex cardiac surgery[9].

The company went public in November 2022, listing on NSE and BSE to raise growth capital and provide liquidity to private equity investors (including Carlyle and Temasek)[10]. Medanta's governance framework is doctor-centric, with key medical institute heads retaining substantial operational autonomy and clinical oversight.

2. Hospital Network & Operational Infrastructure

Medanta's bed network is strategically concentrated in high-density, under-served healthcare catchments across North and Central India:

  • Medanta Gurugram (Flagship Campus): Spread across 43 acres with 1,414 installed beds. Represents approximately 48% of consolidated group revenues with an ARPOB exceeding ₹72,000/day and average length of stay (ALOS) of 3.2 days. The facility houses 45 state-of-the-art operation theaters, including hybrid robotic suites, intra-operative MRI, and dedicated organ transplant ICUs[11].
  • Medanta Lucknow: 950 capacity (550 operational beds). EBITDA margins have crossed 29.2%, driven by strong patient inflows across Eastern Uttar Pradesh and neighboring states. The hospital is equipped with 300 ICU beds and advanced radiation oncology equipment (Varian TrueBeam Linac)[12].
  • Medanta Patna (Jai Prabha Medanta): 650 capacity (400 operational beds). Developed as a public-private partnership (PPP) with the Government of Bihar, it is the first private quaternary facility in Bihar, achieving over 70% occupancy on operational beds and delivering robust OPM above 24.5%[13].
  • Medanta Indore: 175 installed beds serving Madhya Pradesh with specialized centers for cardiac surgery, nephrology, and joint replacements.
  • Medanta Ranchi (Abdur Razzaque Ansari Memorial Weavers' Hospital): 200 operational beds providing high-quality tertiary care across Jharkhand under an operation-and-management contract.
  • Developing Pipeline (Noida & South Delhi): A state-of-the-art greenfield hospital in Sector 50, Noida (550 beds) and a 400-bed facility in South Delhi are slated for phased commissioning between FY26 and FY28, providing multi-year volume visibility in premium urban micro-markets[14].

3. Clinical Economics, ARPOB & Specialty Mix

Cardiac Sciences, Oncology, Neurosciences, and Digestive & Hepatobiliary Sciences contribute over 70% of total hospital revenues[15]. High complexity translates directly into superior financial realizations and defensible clinical moats:

  • Quaternary Case Mix: Transplants (liver, kidney, heart, lung), robotic surgical oncology, and complex neuro-interventions represent ~40% of inpatient procedures, commanding procedure-level realizations 2.5x higher than routine secondary care procedures[16].
  • Doctor-Led Institutional Model: Over 80% of senior clinicians are full-time employees on institutional compensation models with performance-linked incentives rather than fee-for-service arrangements, promoting patient safety, multidisciplinary tumor boards, and long clinician tenure.
  • International Patients: Medical tourism contributes ~10% of Gurugram revenues, attracting patients from Central Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and SAARC nations, which are billed in foreign currency at premium tariff rates without intermediary discounting[17].
  • Digital Health & Medanta Labs: Medanta's digital app and outpatient tele-consultations contribute over 15% of new patient registrations, while the captive pathology network drives high-margin diagnostic revenues.
