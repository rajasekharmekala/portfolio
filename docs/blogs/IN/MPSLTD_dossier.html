Primer: how to read this dossier

MPS Limited makes content, learning and platform tools; read from the business to the accounts, cash, chart and rules. The evidence is dated 9 October 2026; the ledger controls price and chart labels.

EBITDA means earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization; ROCE means return on capital employed; EPS means earnings per share; and RSI means relative strength index, a gauge of price strength.

A base is a flat price area; a breakout is a move above it; Stage 2 is an advancing phase; Stage 4 is a falling phase; and asymmetry means gain versus risk.

Net margin is profit left from sales; free cash flow is cash left after needs and spend; working capital is cash tied up in short-term assets and bills; and a measured move is a chart target from pattern height.

Chapter 1: the business

The first chapter asks what MPS sells and why customers might renew. The answer frames the financial and market tests that follow.

Business and economics

MPS earns revenue by producing and delivering specialised information, educational content and technology-enabled learning services. Its customers are institutional publishers, universities, professional bodies and research users; that mix is different from a consumer subscription business because contracts depend on content quality, workflow integration and renewal decisions. The official corporate site describes the company as a B2B learning and platform-solutions provider, while the investor page lists subsidiaries across North America, Europe, Australia and Asia.[17] [19]

The economic attraction is repeatability: once a publisher has moved a catalogue, production workflow or learning process onto a supplier, changing vendors can create errors and retraining costs. That is a possible moat, not proof of permanence. Customers can insource, renegotiate or move work to another specialist, so the report watches renewal quality, concentration and margin durability rather than treating historical growth as guaranteed.

How revenue becomes profit

At a simple level, MPS converts specialist labour, editorial know-how and software-supported workflows into billable content and platform work. FY2025-26 official figures show revenue from operations of ₹7,684 million and EBITDA of ₹2,591 million, implying a high operating conversion before financing and tax.[9] The Screener consolidated series independently reports FY2026 sales of ₹768 crore, operating profit of ₹236 crore and OPM of 31%.[2] The different units and presentation conventions are disclosed rather than blended.

What matters for an owner is whether that conversion survives wage inflation, customer pricing pressure and acquisition integration. A strong margin can hide a weak order pipeline if it comes from temporary mix. The next financial and KPI checks therefore focus on cash, debtor days and revenue growth rather than margin alone.

Three-layer underwriting

The underwriting proceeds in order: first the non-negotiable financial and technical gates, then operating quality, and finally execution conditions. A failure at an earlier layer stops the process rather than being offset by strengths assessed later.

Fatal flaws and red-team

A red-team pass asks what would make the attractive financial story irrelevant. The first failure is regime risk: a high-volume decline in a Downtrend can continue even when earnings are sound. The second is execution risk: FY2025-26 borrowings increased from ₹4 crore to ₹61 crore, and the company’s growth record includes acquisition and subsidiary complexity that needs annual-report note-level reconciliation.[6] The third is valuation uncertainty: without a verified target anchor, an apparent P/E comfort argument is incomplete.

  1. Technical failure: if price remains below ₹2,707.02 and the stage stays Stage 3, the correct action remains rejection. A bounce alone does not change the rule.
  2. Cash-flow deterioration: if three-year CFO/PAT falls below 0.70 or debtor days rise materially above the 63-day FY2026 reading, the financial gate would also fail.
  3. Business-quality erosion: if OPM falls materially below the 31% FY2026 level while sales growth stalls, the future KPI gate would fail even after a chart recovery.

The bear case is stronger than a simple “wait for a lower price” argument: the investor may be waiting for a structural repair, not a discount. Scenario — support stress: a close at the verified 20-day swing low of ₹2,370.10 would be a decline of approximately 4.17% from the ledger close, calculated as 2,370.10 ÷ 2,473.20 − 1 = −4.17%. This is a stress scenario, not a forecast or target. The counter-case is that low use, high ROCE and cash generation give MPS the resources to rebuild. That counter-case becomes investable only when price confirms it.
