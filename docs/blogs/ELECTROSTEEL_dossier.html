1. What the company does (plain English)

Electrosteel makes ductile iron (DI) pipes and fittings that carry drinking water, plus valves and industrial paints [2][4]. Its customers are mostly government water projects such as Jal Jeevan Mission [4][17]. Correction: the watchlist tagged it "power equipment / DC demand"; the sources do not support that [22].
