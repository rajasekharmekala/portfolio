0. Executive Summary & Investment Pitch

Alldigi Tech Limited, formerly Allsec Technologies Limited, supplies business-process management, digital customer experience, payroll processing, human-resources outsourcing and technology-enabled services. The business is labour-led and recurring, so pricing, utilisation, attrition, client retention and collections matter more than physical capacity. The Quess ecosystem may provide channel and capability adjacency, but disclosed contract wins remain the correct proof standard.[1]

Consolidated Screener data show FY26 sales ₹599 Cr, operating profit ₹162 Cr, OPM 27% and PAT ₹82 Cr; TTM sales are ₹605 Cr and TTM PAT ₹85 Cr.[2] Q1 FY26 communication reported revenue ₹143.9 Cr, up 11.3% YoY, EBITDA ₹36.6 Cr, up 17.3%, and international mix 66.2%.[3] Yet CMP ₹776.95 is below 20-DMA ₹795.6, 50-DMA ₹811.8 and 200-DMA ₹817.2, so the tactical technical gate fails.[4]

Decision: REJECTED / WAIT FOR TECHNICAL RE-QUALIFICATION. Keep as a conditional watchlist candidate; no new Zerodha capital below the 200-DMA.

Founded in 1998, Alldigi developed from the Allsec Technologies franchise into a broader BPM and technology-and-digital services platform.[5] The rebrand is intended to make technology-led delivery more visible, but underwriting must distinguish presentation from actual mix shift. Quess ecosystem context can support enterprise access and shared capability; Fairfax is the larger ownership context around Quess.[6]

2. Operations & Revenue Model

2.1 Primary segments

BPM/CXM uses trained delivery teams and service-level agreements for customer interaction and back-office work. Tech & Digital is more project and platform oriented; skill mix, implementation backlog and international demand drive revenue quality.

2.2 Unit economics and contract lifecycle

Contracts move through qualification, transition, steady-state delivery, scope expansion and renewal. Employee cost, utilisation, attrition and pricing determine margins. Debtor days test whether reported profit becomes cash.

2.3 Input sensitivity

There are no conventional raw materials. Skilled labour, recruitment, training, technology platforms and connectivity are the principal inputs. International revenue brings currency and collection exposure.

2.4 Payroll proposition

Payroll is recurring because clients must process employees monthly and meet statutory deadlines. A current monthly payslip count and SmartPay retention rate were not independently verified in the fetched source set; they remain explicit monitoring asks, not invented data.[7]

3. Sector-Specific Operating Dynamics

The five most relevant KPIs are monthly payslips and active employers, retention, digital/technology mix, client concentration, and DSO/free-cash-flow conversion. Q1 FY26 Tech & Digital growth was reported at 19% YoY; FY26 debtor days were 42 and FCF ₹116 Cr versus PAT ₹82 Cr.[8]

8. Adversarial Red-Team: Fatal Flaws & Invalidation Triggers

  1. Leverage inflection: D/E is near the hard limit.
  2. Margin-peak risk: wage inflation and mix dilution can compress 28% OPM.
  3. Client concentration and execution: an account loss, SLA failure or security incident can damage utilisation.
  4. Parentage misconception: Quess/Fairfax association is not proof of cross-sell economics.
Invalidation: OPM compression >300 bps, rolling CFO/PAT <.50, D/E ≥.70, material client loss, or close below ₹700 on heavy volume.
