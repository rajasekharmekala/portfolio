3. Execution Guidelines & Invalidation Triggers

Options capital is governed by strict technical rules rather than emotional hope:

  • Theta Management: Never hold vertical spreads into the final 10 days of expiry if the long strike is out of the money; roll or close to avoid gamma risk.
