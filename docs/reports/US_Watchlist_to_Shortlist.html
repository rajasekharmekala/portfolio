Against that, return on invested capital is 9.8% (floor 12%), the chart is below its 50-day average of $537.14 (Stage 3, momentum gate NO_MOMENTUM), and reward to risk is 0.69 to 1 on the structural stop of $410.40. Layer 2 scores 2 of 5, because the AI ramp has no published dollar backlog and purchase commitments rose to $30.3 billion. The probability-weighted value is $579.30 (+9.6%), but it falls to $501.14 (-5.2%) if only the central multiple drops to its 27.74 times low. Next catalyst: third-quarter results on 3 November 2026.

Bloom Energy. Revenue rose 165.5% to $1,065.4 million and the company turned a profit, with return on invested capital of 20.8%, a free cash flow margin of 20.1% and almost no net debt (borrowings minus cash). Two Layer 1 floors still fail: the chart is Stage 3 under its 50-day average of $248.62, and stock pay is 5.8% of revenue against a 5.0% limit. The ledger's free cash flow base of $0.532 billion was replaced with $0.625 billion (27.65% a year instead of 29.9%). Next catalyst: results on 22 October 2026, which the company has confirmed.

Astera Labs. Revenue grew 104% to $392.4 million and the company has beaten the top of its own guide for six quarters, with $1.25 billion of cash and no borrowings. It fails on stock pay (16.2% of revenue against 5%, with a rising share count, so the relaxed 10% rule does not apply), on the chart (Stage 3, NO_MOMENTUM) and on reward to risk (2.40 to 1 against 3.5). The review found that the peer multiples had been set on a different earnings year from Astera's own; with both on next-year earnings the peer median is 20.33 times and the bear value is $88.43, which leaves the probability-weighted value at $296.81 (+3.8%), below the 5.10% 10-year yield. A price recovery above $315.33 or higher estimates would only re-open the review, because the verdict cannot leave REJECTED while stock pay exceeds the limit unless that gate is changed by an explicit decision. The ledger's free cash flow base of $0.092 billion was replaced with $0.277 billion (34.91% a year instead of 51.45%). Next catalyst: results on 3 November 2026.

D.R. Horton. The company is the largest US homebuilder by homes sold, with debt to capital of 23.0% and $6.1 billion of liquidity, but return on invested capital is 10.7%, free cash flow margin 9.6%, the chart is in a downtrend with falling averages, and estimates for fiscal 2027 fell 7.05% in 90 days.
