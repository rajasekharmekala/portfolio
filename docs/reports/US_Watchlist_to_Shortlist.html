Before you read

This book records one underwriting batch of two companies, Duolingo and Vistra, tested against the six hard gates of the US rule set. Both were rejected, so the shortlist does not change. Duolingo fails on share-based pay, trend and payoff even though its cash returns are excellent, and Vistra fails on return on capital, cash margin, trend and payoff even though its pay burden and debt pass. The main caveat is data age: the repository's 60-day freshness check cannot be met because the newest quarterly figures predate it and the next filings arrive about November 2026. You directed that the batch be published with every issue disclosed, so the section "Run notes and gate exceptions" lists each one, why it cannot be cured today, and what would cure it.

What was tested and why only two names

The US watchlist held 59 active names on the starting snapshot. Two had the status that makes a name eligible for this workflow, which is RESEARCH_REQUIRED with a pending-research action: Duolingo (DUOL) and Vistra (VST). Three other names, Perrigo (PRGO), Coca-Cola Consolidated (COKE) and Cleveland-Cliffs (CLF), were added by the 2026-10-09 market-intelligence run with the status PENDING and the action PENDING_UNDERWRITING. The workflow's eligibility rule does not include that status, so they were not touched and stay queued. The skill sets a batch size of four, and the workflow says to process the eligible names and disclose a short batch instead of widening eligibility. That is what this run did.

Each company got its own independent research agent, as the skill requires. Each agent fetched its sources into the run cache, computed the six gates with the working shown, ran the momentum gate in code against the frozen 2026-10-09 ledger, and wrote a full dossier. The ledger is the single source for every price, average and trend value; the verified close for both names is the 2026-10-09 close.

Decision ledger

The table gives each name's result on the six hard gates. A gate passes only if the stated floor is met. A single failed gate is enough to reject, so the rows below show the full set to explain the decision, not to average it.

Three terms recur below. A Stage is a phase of the price cycle: Stage 2 is the uptrend, with the price above rising 50-day and 200-day averages, and Stage 3 is the topping phase, where the longer trend has not turned up. The momentum gate is a code check on the chart that returns IN_MOMENTUM, ENTERING_MOMENTUM or NO_MOMENTUM; it fails a name whose price sits too far above its nearest support, or whose long-term trend is still falling. The reward-to-risk ratio compares the gain to a target with the loss to a stop; the floor is 3.5 to 1.

Company decisions

Each note below applies the gates from the ledger to one company, names the rule that decided the result, and states what would change it. The codes in the ledger mean this: FAIL_FINANCIAL is a miss on a money floor such as stock pay; FAIL_MULTIPLE is more than one gate failed; entry posture EXTENDED or NO_BUY means the price is too far above a sensible entry to buy even if the business were acceptable.

DUOL: Duolingo, Inc.

Rule that decided it: stock-based pay must be below 5.0% of revenue, and Duolingo's was 13.2% in 2025 ($137.4 million of share awards on $1,037.6 million of revenue). The first half of 2026 was 12.3%, and management guides toward a higher share. The payoff test also fails. The base-case value of $139.52 sits below the $149.92 close, so the reward is negative against a $19.36 risk to the $130.56 stop. The bull case of $164.77 gives only 0.77 to 1 against a 3.5 floor. The momentum gate returns NO_MOMENTUM because the price is 6.07 average daily ranges above the nearest support shelf and the 200-day average is still falling. Result: REJECTED, no allocation, archived. What would change it: any one of these starts a fresh underwriting, namely pay below 5% of revenue for two straight quarters, bookings growth closing on revenue growth (bookings grew 8% against 18% for revenue in the second quarter), or a close at or below about $132.55 with the 200-day average turning up. The name would then qualify only if all six gates pass together. Full working is in the DUOL dossier.

VST: Vistra Corp.

Rule that decided it: four of the six gates fail. ROIC is 7.1% to 7.5% against 12%. Free-cash-flow margin is 7.5% for 2025 and 6.9% for the first half of 2026 against 12%. The chart is in Stage 3, with the 50-day average at $143.90 below the 200-day average at $154.74, and the momentum gate returns NO_MOMENTUM. The central target of $161.48 is the market's own implied value on guided 2026 adjusted EBITDA, so it leaves no upside from the $161.48 close; the high target of $203.12 gives 1.3 to 1 against a 3.5 floor. Two other lenses do not change that: management's 2027 EBITDA opportunity range of $7.4 to $7.8 billion gives 0.19 to 0.58 to 1, and the $217.61 average analyst target gives 1.78 to 1. Debt and share-pay gates pass. Result: REJECTED, no allocation, archived. What would change it: the trend and price conditions (the 50-day average reclaiming the 200-day average for two weeks, or a hold in the $154.70 to $158.00 retest zone) and the cash condition (two quarters of free-cash-flow margin of 12% or more under GAAP, the standard US accounting rules) each address a different failed gate. Any one starts a fresh underwriting, and the name would qualify only if the return, cash, trend and payoff gates all clear together. Full working is in the VST dossier.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This batch is published with status PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS. You directed that every unmet gate be documented in the reports and that publication go ahead. The list that follows is complete as of the final review round.

  1. Stale research dates (60-day freshness gate). The citation check, run with the new --allow-stale-asof option, ended in PASS WITH EXCEPTIONS for both dossiers. It flagged 33 of DUOL's 43 found research records and 40 of VST's 43, so most of each dossier's evidence is older than 60 days. They are older than the 60-day limit measured against the 2026-10-10 run date: the FY2021 to FY2024 history (up to 648 days), fiscal 2025 annual figures (283 days), second-quarter balance-sheet and cash-flow figures for the period ended 2026-06-30 (102 days), and the August 2026 earnings releases, guidance, deal and auction dates (between 64 and 353 days). No newer filing exists. The third-quarter 10-Q filings (quarterly reports filed with the US securities regulator), expected about November 2026, will cure the quarterly items; annual items cure with the next 10-K (the annual report) in February 2027. No research date was changed to avoid the gate. Without the option, the same check prints FAIL for both dossiers. Each dossier lists every one of its records, with period and age, in its own gate-exceptions section.
  2. Reviewer gate. The reviewer model named in the repository configuration was available and ran every pass; no substitute was used. Four rounds were run, the strict maximum, each with four passes (source, consistency, tone, red team) on each of the three documents. Round 1: every document had at least one REVISE or BLOCK, mostly missing or untraceable figures, arithmetic slips and an incomplete exceptions list; all were fixed. Round 2: no BLOCK remained; DUOL source, VST source and summary consistency approved. Round 3: one DUOL source BLOCK for two figures that were true but not recorded in the research file; fixed by adding the records. Round 4 (final): all four VST passes approved; the DUOL dossier and this report still had REVISE findings (DUOL: a peer-table claim, a status label, a dilution cross-check, floor traceability, an unused citation and a missing definition; this report: two missing definitions, an unsupported TipRanks mention, and re-entry wording). Those were fixed best-effort after round 4 and not re-reviewed, so the DUOL dossier and this report carry the status BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT and are not labeled APPROVE. The VST dossier carries the status APPROVED; only its review-status sentence changed after the approval.
  3. Short batch. Two names were eligible against a batch size of four. PRGO, COKE and CLF carry the status PENDING and remain queued for the next run.
  4. Blocked or unreadable sources. Finviz, TradingView and Duolingo's investor site refused automated access. Short interest, analyst targets from those sites, and peer financial statements are shown as N/A with the reason in the dossiers. No value was filled from memory.
  5. Dossier validator does not apply. validate_report.py accepts only the market-intelligence and portfolio-audit report kinds, so it was not run on dossiers. The readability check and the citation check were run on both and the readability check passed.
  6. Net debt for Vistra after Cogentrix, the power-plant portfolio Vistra agreed to buy in January 2026. Cogentrix's earnings are not available in this run, so the combined net debt ratio is N/A. If the plants added no EBITDA at all, the ratio would be 3.19 times, above the 3.0 ceiling; the dossier gives the working and sets the Q3 2026 call as the re-check.
  7. Momentum-gate thresholds. The thresholds are marked needs_calibration in the scoring configuration, which means they have not yet been reviewed against live data. Both rejections have wide margins: Duolingo sits 6.07 average daily ranges above its nearest base and Vistra 4.0, against a ceiling of 1.2, so no plausible recalibration would change either result.
  8. Target method for both names. Duolingo's base case uses 18 times guided adjusted EBITDA, close to the 17.8 times the market already pays. Vistra's central target is the multiple the market already pays on guided 2026 adjusted EBITDA (about 10.2 times), not an independent valuation. That is why its base-case payoff is about zero. A different valuation method could move the number, but not the rejection, which also rests on the return, cash-margin and trend gates.
  9. Holdings freshness. The ledger was built with --allow-stale-holdings, as the protocol requires. The transaction log is treated as accurate and was not independently reconciled in this run. Holdings did not affect either verdict.
  10. Prior summary replaced. The stable file name for this report means this page replaces the earlier batch summary for CERT, DDOG, ELV and HUM. Those four dossiers stay published, and the earlier summary is in git history.
  11. Unrelated data warnings. The repository's data validator reports schema errors in older rows of the watchlist files. They predate this run, do not involve DUOL or VST, and were not changed. The two new archive rows pass the schema.
