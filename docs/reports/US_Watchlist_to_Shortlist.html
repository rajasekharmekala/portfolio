1. The decision at a glance

The table gives each verdict under the repository's three-layer rules. Layer 1 tests the financial floors and the type of business (the archetype), Layer 2 scores 3 to 5 operating measures that run that business, and Layer 3 turns the chart into an entry rule. Reward-to-risk is the gain from the entry price to the base target divided by the loss from the entry price to the stop. The floor is 2.0 to 1 for Core Alpha names and 2.5 to 1 for turnaround names, so a name that clears the floor only at a lower price needs a limit order, not a market purchase.

What each decision rests on

Apple. The device and services company passes every Layer 1 floor: a free cash flow margin of 29.28 percent, net cash of $62.17 billion and stock compensation of 2.94 percent of revenue. The price test fails: the central target of $327 (fiscal 2028 earnings of $10.39 at the three-year median multiple of 31.48 times) is below the $347.10 close, and the probability-weighted value of $321.50 is 7.4 percent below it. The reverse DCF, which asks what yearly growth today's price assumes for ten years, is 13.52 percent on filing cash flow against the ledger's 16.89 percent; the ledger's $107.7 billion free cash flow base could not be reproduced from the filings ($136.683 billion). The chart is a Stage 2 uptrend, but the momentum gate moved from IN_MOMENTUM to ENTERING_MOMENTUM because the 63-day return trails the S&P 500 by 2.88 points. Results are due on 2026-11-02.

Capital One. The card and auto lender is analysed on a bank basis. It is now a turnaround, not a compounder: earnings per share fell for four years, and the 12 percent return-on-tangible-equity floor is met by the latest three quarters and the trailing four (15.2 percent) but not by 2024 (11.2 percent) or 2025 (3.2 percent). The operating scorecard holds three PASS rows (card charge-offs of 3.98 percent, a 13.8 percent capital ratio against 9.0 percent required, and about $1.8 billion of annual Discover and Brex savings) and two WATCH rows (loan growth and the durability of the 8.41 percent net interest margin). An equity-basis reverse DCF gives -1.05 percent a year, and the probability-weighted value is $230.00, or 13.1 percent above the close. Cash is 3.3 percent of net worth, below the 5 percent floor, so funding is a decision before any order.

Taiwan Semiconductor. The chip foundry passes every Layer 1 floor (return on invested capital 29.5 percent, free cash flow margin 29.9 percent, net cash of about NT$2,985 billion) and its chart changed from a Stage 3 pullback with every momentum test failing to a Stage 2 leader sitting 0.21 ATR above its rising 50-day average. The base target was rebuilt from filings at $514 (24 times 2027 earnings of $21.43 per ADR), below the $545 of cycle 1, and the stop moved from $359.92 to $394.34 because the stock is now in an uptrend. Reward-to-risk is 1.81 to 1 at the close and 2.02 to 1 at $434.00, and the own-calculated reverse DCF is 16.6 percent a year. The risks no price level hedges are a Taiwan event and customer concentration (the top ten customers are 78 percent of revenue).

NBIL. The fund is not a company: it holds two times the daily move of Nebius (NBIS) and rebalances daily, which costs it about 12 percent a year above an ideal 2x. The fund is 49.3 percent below its $25.00 launch value while NBIS is 36.4 percent above its own start. A 22.39 percent bounce on 2026-10-27 left the chart in Stage 4 with the short-term turn test still failing. The three-scenario expected value is $7.65 (-39.7 percent). The sale planned on 2026-10-23 did not happen, so the plan restarts: sell 25 shares at the first regular-session open after the report is read, a limit order at $14.00 for the other 25, a hard stop at $8.32, and no shares left by the 2026-11-06 close.

Ledger fundamentals. The ledger had a reverse discounted-cash-flow value for Apple and Capital One, and none for Taiwan Semiconductor or NBIL. Apple's was replaced by a filing-based figure and the difference is explained in the dossier; Capital One's was replaced by an equity-basis figure; Taiwan Semiconductor's was solved in US dollars from filings; NBIL's is not applicable to a fund. The builder's key-figure strip and first expectations row still show the ledger value for Apple, and the dossier labels this in the text.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator has no dossier mode, so the readability check, the citation check and the review packet checks stood in for it. The shortlist recompute check reports that the Apple and NBIL base targets are below the close, which is the known pattern for a name that qualifies only at a lower price or is being sold, and was reported the same way in cycle 1.

Sources and positions. Several sources were blocked or unreadable (Apple's own site, Capital One's investor site, S&P Global, the Fed stress-test PDFs), so those items are shown as not available. Net debt at 2026-09-30 for Taiwan Semiconductor is our estimate until its full third-quarter statements are filed. NBIL's fund details (premium or discount, spread, counterparties) could not be read because the issuer page loads them by script. The positions metadata name for NBIL is wrong and was not edited.
