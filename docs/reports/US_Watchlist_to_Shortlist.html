1. The decision at a glance

The table gives each verdict under the repository's three-layer rules. Layer 1 tests the financial floors and the type of business (the archetype), using insurer equivalents of return on capital, cash margin and debt. Layer 2 scores three to five operating measures that run that business. Layer 3 turns the chart into an entry rule and a position size. Reward-to-risk is the gain from the entry price to the base target divided by the loss from the entry price to the stop. The floor is 2.0 to 1 for Core Alpha names, so a name that clears the floor only at a lower price needs a limit order, not a market purchase.

PLMR. Palomar earned a 21.7 percent annualized return on equity with a second-quarter combined ratio of 83.3 percent and gross written premium up 27.0 percent, but reported net income grew only 6.8 percent in the first half against 27.1 percent adjusted. The red team showed that the original $130.50 zone top fell below the 2.0 to 1 floor on a 27.2 million share count, so the top was cut to $129.00 and the order now waits for the 2026-11-04 results and a reconfirmation of the share count and the earnings bridge.

SKWD. Skyward earned a 20.4 percent operating return on equity and an 89.5 percent combined ratio in the first half and trades at about 11.5 times forward earnings. At an 11.8 times multiple the ratio at the $56.50 zone top drops to 1.41 to 1, so the plan waits for the 2026-11-05 report and a clean combined ratio first.
