What each decision rests on

AMD. Data Center revenue grew 107 percent to $6.7 billion, free cash flow margin is 18.7 percent and net cash is $9.9 billion. Return on invested capital is 9.7 percent as reported (13.0 percent adding back acquisition amortization). The entry math rests on a 38.7 times multiple: at a 27.74 times multiple the reward-to-risk at the top of the zone turns negative. So no order is active before the 2026-11-03 results, and a rally only triggers a rerun of the tests, not a purchase. The size is capped at 1.5 percent for the first purchase.

Bloom Energy. Third-quarter revenue rose 138 percent, but free cash flow was negative $180.6 million on record capital spending, and two customers are 43 and 26 percent of revenue. The stock fell 8.7 percent on 2026-10-22 and another 11.2 percent on 2026-10-23. The dossier gives a rung plan: sell 8.6 shares at a $205 limit by 2026-11-02, sell down to 9.93 shares in total on a close below $189.00, and sell everything on a close below $178.86. The label TRIM_PROFIT_BOOKING is the nearest in the taxonomy and is applied to a 24.2 percent loss, which the dossier discloses.

Astera Labs. The business passes the quality floors: gross margin 73.3 percent, free cash flow margin 23.0 percent, net cash $1.25 billion. Stock pay is 16.2 percent of revenue against a 15 percent cap, and the top three customers are about 86 percent of revenue. The first version assumed 45 times next year's earnings; the review showed today's multiple is 40.2 times, which gives a $256 base target with no gain. The watchlist idea's 85 percent retimer market share has no source. This is a deferral to the 2026-11-03 report, not a rejection. The reviewer did not approve this dossier after two rounds, so treat it as the least settled of the four.

D.R. Horton. The watchlist labelled it a holding, but no shares appear in the records, only five $190 calls expiring 2027-01-15 whose recorded fields are stale. Results are due 2026-10-29, so no order is live before then.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. Astera Labs also carries BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT, because the reviewer's second and last round did not approve it. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites prior-period filings, earnings calls and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: AMD 59, Bloom Energy 39, Astera Labs 49 and D.R. Horton 86. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company files its next report: AMD and Astera Labs on 2026-11-03, D.R. Horton on 2026-10-29, and Bloom Energy in February 2027.

Estimate data. The session ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow figures did not survive a check against filings for any of the four names. AMD's base counted a divested business, Bloom Energy's used a stale profit base, Astera's used a free cash flow figure that does not reconcile, and D.R. Horton's included mortgage-warehouse debt. Each dossier rebuilt the figure from cited filings at the 10 percent discount rate and 18 times terminal multiple in the scoring file. Consensus values that could not be matched to a public page were replaced or shown as not available.

Ledger date. The first attempt at this batch earlier today had to stop, because the data vendor had not yet posted the final 2026-10-23 stock closes. This run started after the closes were available and its ledger holds 2026-10-23 bars for every name in the batch.
