What each decision rests on

McKesson. Return on invested capital is 47.0 percent and net debt to EBITDA is 0.37 times, but free cash flow margin is 1.34 percent against the 12 percent floor, which a drug distributor cannot reach by structure, so the dossier uses the Tactical Momentum route instead of the compounder route. About half of per-share growth comes from buybacks, and 9.3 percent a year is priced in against 7.1 percent guided. A multiple-only fall from 20.0 times to 16.4 times would put the shares 17.03 percent lower. Cash is $2,780.20, so any buy needs a sale, and a fill before the 2026-11-04 report is capped at one share.

BlackRock. Ten percent organic base fee growth and a 47.8 percent adjusted margin in the third quarter, reported on 2026-10-14, make this a clean compounder, but the chart is Stage 3 and reward-to-risk is 1.45 to 1 at the close. At $1,095 it is 2.16 to 1, and that holds only while trailing earnings stay above about $62.44 at the same multiple. The ledger's 8.75 percent reverse DCF used GAAP profit cut by deal charges and was replaced with 5.49 percent on adjusted profit. Cash funds two shares, so the second order needs a sale.

ICE. Free cash flow margin is 39.0 percent and operating margin is about 60 percent, but return on invested capital of 8.01 percent fails the 12 percent floor, and the $6.0 billion debt-funded MarketAxess purchase lifts net leverage from 2.68 to about 3.28 times. The central ratio of 2.71 to 1 at $155.93 passes the 2.5 floor, but the weighted ratio of 2.22 does not, and holding the earnings forecast while cutting the multiple to 18 times gives $159.84. The plan is one 9-share order at $154.63 before the 2026-10-29 report and a second 9 shares only after a fresh run.

Talen Energy. Talen owns 90 percent of the Susquehanna nuclear plant with an Amazon contract of up to 1,920 megawatts, and guided adjusted free cash flow implies almost no growth is priced in. But all four static floors fail on GAAP numbers, net leverage is 4.40 times on 2026 guidance, and the hard red line of 4.0 times leverage with cover under 2.0 is tripped on filed numbers while passing on adjusted EBITDA. The credit agreement's own definition was not read, so the dossier does not claim lenders use the adjusted figure. Because that red-line pass depends on a reading, I kept Talen off the shortlist as QUALIFIED_DEFERRED.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. MCK, ICE and TLN also carry BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites annual filings, quarterly releases and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: MCK 97, BLK 88, ICE 134 and TLN 96. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company reports.

Estimate data. The session ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow figures were checked against filings and replaced for all four: McKesson 9.32 percent, BlackRock 5.49 percent on adjusted profit, ICE 6.73 percent and Talen negative 0.97 percent, because the ledger cash flows or bases did not reconcile to filings. Several multiples are our own proxies, labelled as such, because no published three-year median was found. McKesson's consensus pages were retrieved one day after the session date.

Talen's gate. Talen's PASSED_CONDITIONAL label depends on reading the leverage red line on adjusted EBITDA, because on filed GAAP numbers the red line is tripped. The dossier discloses this, and the record keeps Talen off the shortlist.

Dates. McKesson (2026-11-04), ICE (2026-10-29) and Talen (2026-11-03) are company-confirmed. BlackRock's January 2027 date is an estimate, and its Q3 report was on 2026-10-14.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.
