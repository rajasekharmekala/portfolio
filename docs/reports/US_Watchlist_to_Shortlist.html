The plan sells 7 shares in each account with $177.00 limits and keeps 16 into the 2026-11-04 report.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. ASE Technology and Uber also carry BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites annual filings, earlier quarters and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: ASX 81, QCOM 84, AXP 55 and UBER 126. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company reports.

Estimate data. The session ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow figures were checked against filings and replaced for ASE (6.7 percent, because the ledger mixed NT dollars with US dollars), Uber (3.3 percent, because the ledger used stale free cash flow) and American Express (4.11 percent on adjusted earnings, with the ledger's 4.93 percent kept as a sensitivity). Qualcomm's ledger figure of 4.73 percent was kept. Ledger consensus revisions could not be verified on a public page for ASE (one analyst only), Qualcomm, American Express or Uber and are labelled as unverified.

Verdict labels. The holding taxonomy has no label for trimming to a tier ceiling when the price target sits below the price. ASX and QCOM use TRIM_PROFIT_BOOKING as the nearest label, and the dossiers disclose the deviation and that it is not an endorsement to sell the rest.

Options book. The recorded fields for the Uber calls (days to expiry, underlying price and deltas) are stale, and only last-trade prints were found, so the audit uses forward values and the option value is unverified.

Record limits. The shortlist schema accepts only a positive asymmetry ratio, so the ASX and QCOM records carry a 0.01 placeholder while the dossiers and verdict text show the true negative ratios.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.

Earnings dates. Qualcomm (2026-11-04) and Uber (2026-11-04) are company-confirmed, and ASE's 2026-10-29 date is on its own investor calendar. American Express's late-January 2027 date is an estimate.
