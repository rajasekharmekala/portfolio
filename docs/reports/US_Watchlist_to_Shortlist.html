What each decision rests on

ORCX. The fund is a 2x daily-reset wrapper on Oracle, so it carries Oracle's negative free cash flow plus decay. Over 252 sessions ORCX fell 86.3 percent while Oracle fell 53.7 percent, and the observed lag to a perfect daily double is about 11.2 percent a year. The exact Stage 4 rule is met and Layer 2 fails. The watchlist labelled it a holding and the seed claimed an 85 percent drawdown, but no shares are held and the drawdown from the 52-week high is 87.58 percent. Flat Oracle alone would cut the fund about 9.8 percent.

Amazon. AWS sales grew 36.7 percent on a $496 billion backlog and AWS earns a 36.8 percent margin, but trailing free cash flow is negative 1.0 percent of sales and return on invested capital is 11.1 percent, both under the 12 percent floors. The plan places no share order before the report on 2026-10-29, and then only inside $229.50 to $238.00. The three call options are 30.9 percent of net worth in delta terms and fail the expiry-matched test, so the plan sells one contract on 2026-10-26, one on 2026-10-27, and keeps the last only if AMZN closes at or above $248.00 and AWS growth was at least 33 percent. The red team had asked for a correction to the option payoff timing, which was made and was not rerun, so treat this dossier as less settled than the others.

Tencent. Return on invested capital is 19.0 percent, the company holds net cash and the shares trade near 13 times earnings. The chart meets the exact Stage 4 rule, the recovery test fails and the US Defense Department's 1260H list names Tencent, so the plan sells 58 shares from 2026-10-26 at a $48.80 limit and the remaining 57 by 2026-11-13 (Schwab 70 and Robinhood 45 in total). The ledger's reverse DCF of negative 14.71 percent was a currency mismatch and was replaced with 6.6 percent. The reviewer did not approve this dossier after two rounds, so treat it as the least settled of the four.

MercadoLibre. GMV grew 36 percent in constant currency and fintech users reached 88 million, but operating margin fell to 6.7 percent from 12.2 percent and adjusted free cash flow margin is 3.2 percent against a 12 percent floor. The dossier treats that as a documented exception, and strict application would mean rejection. Reward-to-risk clears the 2.0 floor at the zone top only on the central target (3.07:1), and the probability-weighted value gives 2.0:1 at $1,728.10, so orders are capped at that price and the first purchase at 1.75 percent of net worth. The account log shows 2 shares were bought on 2026-08-11 and sold on 2026-10-23 at a $216.96 loss.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. Amazon and Tencent also carry BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites annual filings, earlier quarters, prospectuses and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: ORCX 36, Amazon 78, Tencent 49 and MercadoLibre 99. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company reports.

Estimate data. The session ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow figures were checked against filings and replaced for Amazon (11.35 percent on owner cash flow, with the ledger's 6.9 percent shown as a sensitivity), Tencent (6.6 percent, because the ledger divided a dollar value by a yuan cash flow) and MercadoLibre (17.45 percent on net income, because the ledger's cash flow counted customer funds). It is not defined for the ORCX fund itself. Ledger consensus revisions could not be verified on a public page for Amazon, Tencent or MercadoLibre and are labelled as unverified.

Secondary and non-SEC sources. Tencent is not an SEC registrant, so its figures come from HKEXnews and tencent.com. Some Amazon facts (a call transcript, the FTC trial date and a ratings summary) cite secondary pages and are labelled as reported.

Verdict labels. The holding taxonomy has no label for a rules-based exit of a sound business or for selling down option contracts. Tencent and the Amazon calls use REPLACE_UNDERPERFORMER as the nearest label, and the dossiers disclose the deviation.

Options book. The recorded value of the Amazon calls ($4,842.57) is stale against a live quote of about $4,230, and the recorded cost of two of the three contracts could not be verified, so the audit uses forward values only.

Zone edit after approval. MercadoLibre's entry-zone top was moved to its $1,728.10 order ceiling after the reviewer approved, and the dependent figures were recomputed without a further review.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.

Earnings dates. Amazon (2026-10-29) and Oracle's Investor Day (2026-10-28) are company-confirmed. Tencent's 2026-11-18 date comes from its investor calendar without a board-meeting notice, and MercadoLibre's date is not confirmed.
