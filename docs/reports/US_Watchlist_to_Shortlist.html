What each decision rests on

QCML. The fund is the GraniteShares 2x Long QCOM fund, not the Defiance fund named in the watchlist seed. It tracks twice Qualcomm's daily move closely, but all-in cost is about 22.5 percent a year, a flat Qualcomm costs about 6 percent in a quarter, and Qualcomm reports on 2026-11-04. At 60 percent volatility with Qualcomm flat, the fund would lose 9.81 percent over three months. Robinhood bought 81.4 shares on 2026-08-17 and sold them on 2026-10-23 at a $95.25 gain.

Rambus. This is a net-cash, high-margin supplier of DDR5 memory interface chips, with a 39.6 percent free cash flow margin and product revenue up 21.9 percent. The chart meets the exact Stage 4 rule because the recovery test fails on its short-term turn check, even though the base-pivot test passes. The base reward-to-risk of 2.43 to 1 is also short of the 2.5 floor, and Rambus reports on 2026-10-26, three days away. An 18 times multiple on unchanged earnings would put the shares 8.89 percent lower.

Alphabet Class A. Class A and Class C carry the same economics, and Class A trades about 0.92 percent higher for its vote, so GOOGL would duplicate what you hold. Free cash flow margin is 11.95 percent against the 12 percent floor, although Cloud revenue grew 81.8 percent and Layer 2 scores 4.5 of 5.0. The 7.16 to 1 reward-to-risk comes from a stop only 2.7 percent away, so it overstates the setup. A 22 times multiple on base earnings would give $297, 7.25 percent lower. Alphabet reports on 2026-10-28.

Alibaba. The exact Stage 4 rule is met, return on invested capital is about 5.5 percent and free cash flow margin is negative 6.9 percent, so Layer 1 fails, and customer management revenue fell 7.5 percent. AI cloud revenue grew 44.9 percent at an 11.6 percent margin, which is real. The ledger's negative 16.06 percent reverse DCF mixed yuan with dollars and was replaced. The US Defense Department's 1260H list names Alibaba, and the company's September-quarter report date is not confirmed.

A correction that affects the earlier Alphabet audit

The GOOGL work found that GGLL is a 2x fund according to its current prospectus, while the positions label and the earlier GOOG dossier treated it as 1.5x. On the 2x basis, combined Alphabet exposure is GOOG 9.56 percent plus twice GGLL's 2.77 percent, or 15.10 percent of net worth. That is above the 6.5 percent TIER_2 ceiling and about 0.1 points above the 15 percent hard cap. After the 8-share GOOG trim it would still be 12.02 percent, so the GOOG verdict is reinforced rather than changed. The GOOG records carry a note, but the GOOG dossier itself was not edited, and the positions file label needs correcting.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. GOOGL and BABA also carry BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites annual filings, prospectuses and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: QCML 59, RMBS 69, GOOGL 76 and BABA 113. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company reports.

Estimate data. The session ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow figures were checked against filings and replaced for Rambus (10.12 percent, because the ledger's cash flow was unsupported), Alphabet (10.19 percent on owner cash flow, because the ledger used a $22.7B cash flow against the company's $53.3B) and Alibaba (not defined on negative free cash flow, because the ledger mixed yuan with dollars). It is not defined for the QCML fund, and the dossier uses the Qualcomm look-through.

Fund and source gaps. QCML's current net assets are unverified because data sites differ from the filed June figure. Alibaba's court ruling after July and its quick-commerce losses were not found, and some pages were blocked, so company copies and a third-party transcript site were used. Several multiples are our own proxies, labelled as such, because no published three-year median was found.

Verdict labels. The two dossiers with more than one class or fund (Alphabet Class A and QCML) record that the watchlist seed labels did not match: GOOGL is not held and QCML is a GraniteShares fund, not the Defiance fund named in the seed.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.

Earnings dates. Rambus (2026-10-26), Alphabet (2026-10-28) and Qualcomm (2026-11-04) are company-confirmed. Alibaba's 2026-11-24 date is a third-party estimate.
