1. The decision at a glance

The table gives each verdict under the repository's three-layer rules. Layer 1 tests the financial floors and the type of business (the archetype), Layer 2 scores three to five operating measures that run that business, and Layer 3 turns the chart into an entry rule and a position size. Reward-to-risk is the gain from the entry price to the base target divided by the loss from the entry price to the stop. The floor is 2.0 to 1 for Core Alpha names and 2.5 to 1 for tactical and turnaround names, so a name that clears the floor only at a lower price needs a limit order, not a market purchase. A limit order is an instruction to buy only at or below a named price.

What each decision rests on

Gulfport Energy. The Ohio and Oklahoma gas producer passes the four financial floors (return on invested capital 16.3 percent after removing non-cash hedge gains, free cash flow margin 18.1 percent, net debt to adjusted EBITDA 1.04 times) and scores 3.5 of 5.0 on Layer 2, but only two of the five operating measures pass outright. The chart is labelled Stage 4, a long slide below falling averages, which is not a rejection because the turnaround test (Test C) passes. The dossier's own reverse DCF is minus 1.45 percent a year against consensus 2027 profit growth of 9.6 percent, so the price assumes shrinking cash flow. The weak points are a trailing cash flow flattered by the January 2026 gas spike, a new chief executive and a vacant finance chief, and the target depending on a profit multiple of 8.5 times when the stock trades at 6.93 times, so the zone was lowered until the 2.5 floor held at today's multiple.

Blackstone. The alternative asset manager is judged on return on equity (32.38 percent) and distributable earnings in place of return on invested capital and free cash flow, passes every static test and scores 4.0 of 5.0 on Layer 2: fee-related earnings, fee-earning assets and perpetual capital pass, while fund redemption stress and carried interest realizations are watch items. Stage 4 is allowed because Test C passes, but a fill below the 20-day average would normally fail the short-term-turn test, so every order is conditional on a re-check on the fill date and filled shares are exited at the next open if the failure shows. Two single-factor stresses leave the zone top below the 2.0 floor (earnings-only 1.57 to 1, multiple-only negative), which the dossier states plainly.

CME Group. The futures exchange and clearing house has a 66 percent operating margin, debt of about 0.22 times EBITDA and 62 percent free cash flow margin, so the wait is about price and not quality. The round-1 review showed the repository's rule for a base or turnaround setup puts the stop at the 63-day low minus one average true range ($250.22), not the swing low, and the zone top fell from $277 to $275 to keep 2.0 to 1. The scenario payoff at $275 is about 1.5 to 1 against the bear case, and a 19.5 times multiple on 2028 earnings is worth about $280, so the zone protects against an earnings miss better than against a lower multiple.

EOG Resources. The verdict and base target ($173.12) are unchanged from 2026-10-23. The close fell 1.3 percent to $160.92 while the averages kept rising, so the support distance fell from 3.36 to 2.13 average true range units and the zone moved to $139.76 to $143.47 with a $135.67 stop. The weighted value rose from $165.70 to $169.06 after five points of probability moved from bear to bull and the bull multiple fell from 9.07 to 8.94 times. The dossier's reverse DCF is plus 0.14 percent a year against the ledger's plus 5.32 percent on a free cash flow base that does not reconcile to the filings. The working limit is $142.88, the highest price where 2.5 to 1 holds with no re-rating.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers, plus BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT for BX and EOG. The exceptions, in plain words:

Stale as-of records. Each dossier's citation check ran with the stale-as-of allowance and printed PASS WITH EXCEPTIONS. The exception lines (145 for GPOR, 27 for BX, 31 for CME and 75 for EOG) are annual and quarterly filings, earnings releases and dated company statements older than 60 days, which are the latest periodic documents; the next filings follow each company's coming report (the third-quarter reports for GPOR on 2026-11-04 and EOG on 2026-11-06, the Blackstone 10-Q due in a few weeks, and the CME fourth-quarter report in early February 2027). Every as-of date is true, none was edited, and each dossier explains this in its own gate-exceptions section. It lowers confidence only in that the book could miss a change since the last filing. The checker dated the ages from 2026-10-29 for two of the dossiers because it ran after midnight Pacific; the session is 2026-10-28 and the dossiers say so.

Ledger reverse DCF and consensus. The ledger holds a reverse DCF and consensus revisions only for EOG (plus 5.32 percent), and that figure does not reconcile to the filings. For GPOR, BX and CME it holds none. Each dossier therefore solved its own from cited cash flow (minus 1.45 percent for GPOR, 4.85 percent for BX on distributable earnings, 8.14 percent for CME, plus 0.14 percent for EOG) and rendered from a copy of the ledger that adds only that symbol's reverse DCF and revision blocks (and, for EOG, a corrected analyst count). The real ledger was not edited, every price and technical figure comes from it unchanged, and each dossier discloses the patched copy in its own run notes.

Reviewer limit. GPOR and CME received reviewer APPROVE in round 2 (three NOTE findings each, left as written so the approved text did not change). BX received REVISE in round 2 for template code that had leaked into the entry-zone paragraphs; that and the red team's request to drop an unsupported 10-day probation exception were fixed after the cap and not reviewed again. EOG received REVISE in round 2 for a stale $143.47 order price in the head and the position paragraph; this was fixed after the cap, which also moved the working limit from $142.89 to the exact $142.88, and was not reviewed again. The red team judged the GPOR, CME and EOG verdicts to hold in round 2 and judged BX 'should be stricter' in both rounds, a finding answered by the conditional limit rules and the probation fix. None of BX or EOG is labelled APPROVE.

Red team model substitution. The red team model configured for this workflow was blocked by the provider on its first attempt for BX ('Provider blocked the response'); the pass was rerun once on the same model and succeeded, so no substitution was needed.
