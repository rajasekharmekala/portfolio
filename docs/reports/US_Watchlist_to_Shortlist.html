VCYT case note: a proven core, an unproven expansion

Veracyte's US labs turn physician-ordered samples into genomic reports. The company bills payers after it delivers the results. Decipher tests prostate cancer tissue. Afirma tests thyroid fine-needle aspirates. In Q2 2026, Decipher brought in $91.9 million and Afirma $51.2 million. Together, they made up about 95.2% of total revenue of $150.3 million. Despite its range of tests, the firm depends heavily on these two franchises.1, 3, 14, 15

Studies support the tests, but do not remove business risk. In the 2018 blinded Afirma validation study, sensitivity was 91% and specificity was 68%. The study found a 96% negative predictive value at 24% cancer prevalence. That result is not a guarantee for every patient. Decipher measures the expression of 22 genes in prostate tissue. The tests support decisions; they do not ensure an outcome or lasting payer reimbursement.13, 15

In Q2 2026, the company launched Prosigna and TrueMRD. Prosigna uses the PAM50 classifier on breast tissue. TrueMRD uses a tumor-informed, whole-genome method and blood tests for muscle-invasive bladder cancer. The company reported Medicare coverage for TrueMRD in Q2. Its October 2026 announcement said Prosigna coverage was effective August 19 for qualifying patients. Coverage and launches do not prove material adoption or added profit.1, 16, 18, 19

In Q2 2026, total volume was 50,967 tests, up 13% year over year. Testing volume was 48,389, up 14%. Decipher volume was about 29,700, up 17%. Afirma volume was about 18,600, up 10%. Testing revenue rose 19% to $145.7 million. On the July 30 call, management said the reported $3,010 testing average selling price included about $4.5 million in prior-period collections. Without those collections, normalized ASP was about $2,900. The higher reported ASP is not wholly recurring current-period pricing.1, 2

The Q2 2026 filing attributes 33% of total revenue to Medicare and 13% to UnitedHealthcare. It counts Medicare Advantage with the relevant private payer. Collection, coverage and reimbursement changes therefore matter. The company also reports single-source inputs. Its 2025 annual filing describes competition. Neither clinical evidence nor payer coverage removes these risks.3, 17

If coverage or collections weaken, reassess the business before any order. If the price reaches support, wait for the earnings release and check the trend again. A break below the stop challenges the trade, not automatically the business.
