What each decision rests on

EQT. The low-cost Appalachian gas producer has a repaired balance sheet, with net debt falling from $9.1 billion to $5.5 billion, and a free cash flow margin of 39.4 percent over twelve months. Return on invested capital of 9.1 percent fails the 12 percent floor. The 3.02 to 1 ratio at $50.43 needs a re-rating from 15.4 times to 16.4 times earnings, and the earnings estimate must stay at or above about $3.53, not the $3.26 bear case that gives only 1.03 to 1. The order is placed only after the 2026-10-28 call and a same-day check, and an 11.9 times peer-median multiple would put the shares 14.6 percent below the entry. Buying needs a sale elsewhere, because cash is $2,780.20.

Halozyme. Royalties on ENHANZE-enabled subcutaneous drugs grew 49.6 percent, free cash flow margin is 48.5 percent, and return on invested capital is 20.6 percent. But the shares have doubled to $125.63, they are 3.16 ATR above their average with a relative strength index of 73.98, and the weighted reward-to-risk is only 1.73 to 1 at the top of the zone. The hinge is holding about 12 times 2027 earnings, and a return to 9.77 times would give about $104. Any fill before the 2026-11-05 report is capped at 12 shares. The ledger's 17.12 percent reverse DCF could not be reproduced and was replaced with 5.66 percent.

Regeneron. Return on invested capital is 19.9 percent excluding idle cash, net cash is $15.12 billion, and the chart is a clean Stage 2 uptrend. Reward-to-risk is 0.51 to 1 at the close, and the weighted ratio at $752 is 1.51, so the verdict rests on the central target. Regeneron reports on 2026-10-30, so no order should rest through the report and any buy needs a fresh rerun. A 12 times multiple alone would give $752.40, 6.54 percent lower. The reviewer did not approve this dossier after two rounds, so treat it as less settled.

Intuitive Surgical. The surgical robotics company has 84 percent recurring revenue, $6.80 billion of net cash and a Layer 2 score of 4.5 of 5.0. It reported on 2026-10-20 with a $450 million antitrust settlement charge, fell 8.1 percent, and recovered to $452.29. The 2.06 to 1 ratio at $404.00 depends on 44 times earnings and $10.50 of earnings per share together: at 40 times it is 0.57 to 1, and 36 times gives $378, 16.43 percent below the close. Cash of $2,780.20 covers six shares, not seven.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. EQT and REGN also carry BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites annual filings, quarterly releases and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: EQT 71, HALO 115, REGN 117 and ISRG 49. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company reports.

Estimate data. The session ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow figures were checked against filings and replaced for EQT (0.80 percent), Halozyme (5.66 percent) and Regeneron (4.23 percent, with 8.21 percent after stock pay as a cautious cross-check), because the ledger's cash flow figures did not reconcile to filings. Intuitive Surgical's was rerun at 17.05 percent. Ledger consensus revisions could not be verified on a public page for EQT and are labelled unverified; Regeneron's FY2027 EPS of $62.70 is a single-vendor figure. Several multiples are our own proxies, labelled as such, because no published three-year median was found.

Dates. EQT (2026-10-27 and 10-28), Regeneron (2026-10-30) and Halozyme (2026-11-05) are company-confirmed. Intuitive Surgical's January 2027 date is an estimate.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.
