What each decision rests on

NBIL. The watchlist text and the holdings label both named the wrong fund. NBIL is the GraniteShares 2x Long NBIS Daily ETF, a swap-based fund that targets twice the daily move of Nebius Group (NBIS) and charges 1.50% a year. The ledger price check holds together: NBIS fell 12.18% on 16 October and NBIL fell 24.49%, against 24.36% for a perfect two-times fund. At about 110% annualised volatility the daily reset costs about 22% a quarter, so NBIS has to rise 13.8% in three months for NBIL to finish flat. On the underlying, return on invested capital is -6.3%, free cash flow margin is -434% and stock pay is 13.9% of revenue, and the fund's own chart is below its 50-day and 200-day averages. Layer 2 scores 2 of 5 and reward to risk is 0.10 to 1 against 3.5. The dossier names the limb of the holding rule that applies (the reason for owning the fund is impaired) and sets a ladder: Nebius near $188 prompts a re-read, and only an NBIL close above $24.54 with the momentum test passing reverses the verdict. The sell plan is a limit at $14.92 for up to five sessions, then market if NBIL closes below $11.85. Next catalyst: Nebius third-quarter results, estimated for 10 November 2026.

Arista. Return on invested capital is 27.1%, free cash flow margin 48.9%, net cash $13.34 billion and stock pay 4.8% of revenue, so every financial floor passes. The Core technical floor fails (Stage 3, close under both the 20-day and 50-day averages) and so does reward to risk (1.60 against 3.0). Layer 2 has 2 of 5 passing against a minimum of 4. The reviewer's red team showed that the add zone of $162.64 to $169.02 only clears the 3.0 floor if the central price-to-earnings multiple re-rates from 33.56 to 40.86; at 33.56 the central price is $175.18 and reward to risk at $169.02 is 0.42 to 1. The first verdict, ACCUMULATE_ON_DIP, was therefore replaced with REJECTED for new money. This is a decision about adding, not a sell signal: no exit rule is met, so the 9.7299 shares are held under the $154.27 stop, and a close below it becomes REPLACE_UNDERPERFORMER. The ledger's Reverse DCF used $3.892 billion of free cash flow that does not match the filings and was replaced with $5.155 billion (14.92% a year instead of 18.66%). Next catalyst: third-quarter results on 3 November 2026.

DigitalOcean. Next catalyst: third-quarter results, estimated for 4 November 2026, and $312.3 million of convertible notes that mature on 1 December 2026.

CrowdStrike. Annual recurring revenue is $5.84 billion (+25%), net new recurring revenue was a record $332.8 million and free cash flow is $1.52 billion on the company's own definition. Return on invested capital is -14.1% on a GAAP basis, stock pay is 23.1% of revenue and free cash flow after stock pay is only 5.1% of revenue. The Reverse DCF (also built in a per-company copy) says the price needs 32.8% a year of free cash flow growth for ten years against 14.3% implied by management's own long-term goal. Our central 12-month value of $210.01 is below the $227.39 close. The verdict is revisited on a weekly close above $286.99 on volume, a pullback to about $177, or stock pay below 10% of revenue with the share count falling. Next catalyst: fiscal third-quarter results, estimated for 1 December 2026.
