What each decision rests on

Microsoft. Azure grew 43 percent, commercial backlog is $678 billion, and operating margin is 46.8 percent. Capital spending is 34.9 percent of revenue, so free cash flow margin is 20.2 percent now but about 9 percent on fiscal 2027 consensus, below the 12 percent floor. The first read was ACCUMULATE_ON_DIP. The red team showed that a gap to the $430 bear price would cost 56.63 basis points of net worth at the 3.5 percent tier ceiling, against a 50 basis point budget, so the verdict became WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT with fills before the report capped at 3.09 percent.

Apple. Services is 26 percent of sales but 42 percent of gross profit, the iPhone grew 22 percent, free cash flow margin is 29.3 percent, and net cash is $62.2 billion. At about 36 times forward earnings the price is about 20 percent above its median multiple, so the setup only works at $296 to $304. The watchlist idea claimed $110 billion of annual buybacks; the filings show $82.2 billion over the last twelve months. A 26.72 times multiple would put the entry ceiling below the stop, so every fill needs a fresh check after the 2026-11-02 report.

Capital One. Return on tangible common equity is 15.2 percent against a 12 percent floor, the common equity tier 1 ratio is 13.8 percent, and Discover integration synergies are running at about $1.8 billion a year. Free cash flow margin and net debt to EBITDA do not apply to a bank. The stock is in a Stage 1 base under falling averages but passes the turnaround test, so the dossier allows starter-size limit orders only and sets the bottom of the zone at half an average true range above the 63-day base low.

Taiwan Semiconductor. Gross margin is 66.4 percent, high-performance computing is 68 percent of revenue, and trailing free cash flow margin is 29.9 percent. The ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow figure mixed US dollar prices with Taiwan dollar cash flow, so the dossier recomputed it in one currency at 16.5 percent a year. The stop was moved to the wider $359.92 base-low level, the tier cut to TIER_3, and the weight held to 2.5 percent until the January 2027 report, because a gap through the tight stop would breach the risk budget.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites prior-period filings, earnings calls and policy items that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: Microsoft 78, Apple 86, Capital One 36 and Taiwan Semiconductor 89. Microsoft's latest periodic filing is its fiscal 2026 annual report, because the September-quarter filing follows the 2026-10-28 results. These exceptions lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company files its next report.

Estimate data. The data vendor's figures were present in the session ledger this time, but several did not survive a check against filings. Microsoft's free cash flow of $16.5 billion could not be reproduced, Apple's reverse discounted-cash-flow base differed from the filings, Taiwan Semiconductor's mixed US dollar and Taiwan dollar inputs, and Capital One's net-profit proxy is not a bank cash-flow measure. Each dossier rebuilt the figure from cited filings at the 10 percent discount rate and 18 times terminal multiple in the scoring file and explains the difference. Consensus and analyst-target figures that could not be matched to a fetched public page were replaced or shown as not available.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.

Earnings dates. Microsoft's date (2026-10-28) is company-confirmed. Apple's 2026-11-02 date is company-scheduled but was read from a data provider. Capital One's late-January 2027 date and Taiwan Semiconductor's mid-January 2027 date are estimates. Each dossier labels its own.

Reviewer independence. Two reviewers each read one file outside their allowed inputs (a writer's research-build script and the run brief). Both said it did not affect any finding, and both dossiers were approved on their packets.

Peer names not on the watchlist. The dossiers surfaced SYF and JPM (from Capital One) and INTC, GFS and UMC (from Taiwan Semiconductor). None was added, because adding them would widen the underwritten queue without their own research.
