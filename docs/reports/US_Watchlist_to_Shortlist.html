Company monograph

UnitedHealth Group links UHC health plans with Optum services [5][14][15]. UHC prices benefits and pays claims for employers and public programs [5][15]. Optum Health coordinates clinicians and care [1][5]. Optum Insight sells data, software and revenue-cycle services [5]. Optum Rx manages drug plans and prescription supply [5]. Each unit has different costs. MCR, member retention, clinic margins, backlog, adjusted scripts, cash flow and debt are key measures [1][4][5].

The moat may come from network scale, client ties and linked claims and care data [5][14][15]. It can weaken through mispriced care, member losses, thin clinic coverage or rule changes [4][5][22]. 2025 earnings and forecast cuts hurt management trust [9][10][11][12]. We need repeated MCR and Optum margin gains to trust this recovery [1].
