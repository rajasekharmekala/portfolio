Start here. This book records a four-name underwriting batch rather than a prediction. The verified session is 2026-10-09, while the run was assembled on 2026-10-10 Pacific time. Return on invested capital (ROIC) is the profit earned from the money tied up in a business; it is like the interest rate earned by the company’s operating engine. Free-cash-flow margin is cash left after operations and capital spending divided by revenue; it is the cash that can repay debt, fund reinvestment or return to owners. Net debt to EBITDA compares borrowings with a rough cash-earnings measure. Stock-based compensation (SBC) is pay made in shares, so it is an owner dilution test even when it is not a cash expense. Reward-to-risk is upside to a target divided by the loss to a stop. The configured floor is 3.5, so a target that offers only a few cents for each dollar risked cannot qualify. Stage 2 describes a rising trend, but trend strength never waives a financial gate. Support is a price area where demand previously appeared; a stock can remain in Stage 2 and still be too far above support to buy. Read the ledger first, then each case note. A REJECTED result means no new shortlist allocation and archive routing under the US workflow. It does not claim the business is worthless. It says the evidence, price and risk budget do not meet the rules today. A later review can reverse the result only after a new verified session, a new filing or company disclosure where appropriate, and a recalculated target and stop. Figures marked with a real filing period are not silently relabelled as current. Where a metric cannot be verified, the dossier uses N/A and explains why. The S&P 500 regime is Uptrend (narrow breadth), but index strength is context rather than a substitute for company-level underwriting. The important consequence is simple: these four names do not displace an incumbent, so the existing books remain intact.

Displacement economics

Displacement is competitive capital allocation. A challenger must first pass Layer 1, then compare KPI pass rate, support distance, catalyst proximity and asymmetry against the weakest incumbent. None of these names passes that prerequisite. The correct action is to archive the candidates with their audit trails, not to force an entry because a chart looks interesting.

The triggers are conditional reruns, not standing buy orders. A support pullback without those repairs does not qualify.
