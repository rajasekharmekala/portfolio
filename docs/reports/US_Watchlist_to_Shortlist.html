Before you read

This batch underwrote four leveraged products: three funds you hold (TQQQ, UDOW and SPXL) and one note you do not (FNGU). A leveraged fund resets its exposure every day, so it loses value to volatility and cost even when its index goes nowhere, and the dossiers cover the index, the trend and that decay, not a company. The answer for all three held funds is hold-only with dated exits and no adds, and the note is rejected. No product joins the shortlist as a new candidate, so this batch sends no alert email.

What each decision rests on

TQQQ. Net assets are $39.75 billion with ten swap banks, and the fund tracks three times the Nasdaq-100 closely. Over twelve months it gained 23.65 percent against 46.10 percent for three times QQQ, so decay and financing took about 22 points. The plan holds all 35.0806 shares, sells everything on a close below $63.61, cuts to 18 shares on 2027-01-22 if the close is under the 50-day average, and sells the rest on 2027-04-23. The first version allowed an 8-share add; review showed a price-only add lacked momentum confirmation and the add was removed.

UDOW. The Dow is price-weighted, so Goldman Sachs and Caterpillar carry about 17.8 percent of the stock sleeve between them. The first version added 2 shares at $64.75, but a base case matched to the holding period (to 2027-01-22) gave only $69.19 and a reward-to-risk of 0.28 at the close, so the add was dropped. KEEP_COMPOUNDING is the nearest label and is used only for a managed, time-limited hold: sell half at a $69.19 close, everything below $60.46, and everything on 2027-01-22.

SPXL. SPXL has about $6.84 billion of assets and costs about 10 percent a year in drag. Its weighted outcome is $274.75 against a $275.63 close. The plan trims 0.7209 shares at the 2026-10-26 open to 5.9 shares (1.97 percent of net worth, under the 2.0 percent tier ceiling), checks the size on 2026-11-02 and 2026-12-01, and exits fully by the 2026-12-31 close. The dip zone of $250.00 to $252.50 is a trigger for a fresh run, not an order. A red team check corrected its ratio from 2.85 to about 2.51 once decay before the entry date is counted.

FNGU. FNGU is a Bank of Montreal exchange-traded note, so it is unsecured bank debt with no assets behind it, a financing charge of about 18.5 percent a year plus a 0.95 percent fee, and an issuer call right it used on the original note in 2025. Over twelve months the index gained 12.6 percent and the note 1.5 percent. The watchlist labelled it a holding, but the records show none. The Stage 4 red line is clear because both averages are rising, so the rejection rests on reward-to-risk and cost.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS and BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT for all four dossiers and this report. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites prospectuses, annual reports, index methodologies and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: TQQQ 39, UDOW 26, SPXL 34 and FNGU 33. They lower confidence only in how current those specific documents are, and they clear as each sponsor files its next report.

Fund data. Reverse discounted-cash-flow and consensus revisions are not defined for a fund, so each dossier says so and uses the look-through valuation of the underlying index instead (for example a forward P/E of 21.2 for the Nasdaq-100). Decay, tracking and scenario figures are our own calculations from daily closes, labelled as such, and the scenario formula was checked against the decay tables in the prospectuses. Several net-asset figures from data sites did not reconcile with filings and were not used.

Source access. Some pages (an SEC alert, an exchange press release and an index provider page) were rate-limited or blocked, and no claim depends on them.

Verdict labels. The holding taxonomy has no label for a hold-only position with dated exits. TQQQ and SPXL use WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT and UDOW uses KEEP_COMPOUNDING as the nearest labels, and each dossier discloses the deviation.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.

Dates. The FOMC meetings on 2026-10-27/28 and 2026-12-08/09 are confirmed by the Federal Reserve calendar. Index reconstitution and distribution dates marked as estimates in the dossiers are derived from past patterns.

Email. No product newly qualified for the shortlist as a candidate and no displacement test was run, so the conditional email policy sends nothing for this batch.
