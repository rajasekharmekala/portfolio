What each decision rests on

NVDL. NVDL is a 2x fund, and a daily reset still costs a lot: over twelve months NVDL gained 7.49 percent against 34.04 percent for a simple 2x, losing 14.96 points to volatility decay and 11.59 points to financing. The watchlist labelled it a holding, but no shares are held. The plan is two limit buys of 30 shares each, cancelled at the close of 2026-11-12 so that no more than 30 shares are held into NVIDIA's expected report on 2026-11-18, which NVIDIA has not confirmed. A 13.26 times multiple on NVIDIA alone would put the fund 11.6 percent lower.

Oracle. Oracle's cloud revenue grew 121 percent and its remaining performance obligations are $664 billion, but trailing free cash flow is negative $28.7 billion on $75.7 billion of yearly capital spending. The chart meets the exact Stage 4 rule, and Layer 2 fails with two failed measures. The dossier says to sell all 35 shares with limit orders on 2026-10-26 and 2026-10-27, and at the open on 2026-10-28 before Oracle's Investor Day. It also states the hold case honestly: a probability-weighted value of $143.11, 13.4 percent above the close. The reviewer did not approve this dossier after two rounds, so treat it as the least settled of the four.

Alphabet. Return on invested capital is 33.5 percent, Cloud revenue grew 82 percent, and the company holds $142.3 billion of net cash. Free cash flow margin of 11.95 percent misses the 12 percent floor by 0.05 point because of capital spending. The position is 9.56 percent of net worth against a 6.5 percent tier ceiling, and the 1.5x Alphabet fund GGLL adds about 4.2 points of look-through exposure. The plan sells exactly 8 shares in three dated limit steps by 2026-10-28, leaves 16.81 shares, and makes no automatic buys. A 20 times multiple on its own would put the base value below the stop.

Chipotle. The exact Stage 4 rule is met: the close of $27.52 is below a falling 50-day average of $33.52, which is below a falling 200-day average of $34.31, and the recovery test fails. The business lines pass narrowly (return on invested capital 20.2 percent, no borrowings) but restaurant-level margin is falling, and free cash flow clears the 12 percent floor only because of a one-off tax-timing gain. The account log shows the 100 shares were bought on 2026-08-11 and sold on 2026-10-23 at a $465.06 loss, so a purchase before 2026-11-22 would defer that loss.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. Oracle also carries BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites annual filings, earlier quarters, prospectuses and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: NVDL 89, Oracle 51, Alphabet 56 and Chipotle 56. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company reports on 2026-10-28.

Estimate data. The session ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow figures were checked against filings and replaced for Oracle (not defined, because free cash flow is negative), Alphabet (22.3 percent on the company's own free cash flow), Chipotle (6.66 percent, because the ledger counted leases twice) and the NVIDIA look-through for NVDL (14.66 percent). The fund itself has no cash flow, so its reverse DCF is not defined.

Secondary sources. Rating-agency pages and a news agency page for Oracle were blocked, so Oracle's ratings and the share of its backlog tied to one customer are cited to secondary reports and labelled as reported.

Verdict labels. The holding taxonomy has no label for a weight trim at a loss or for a full sale driven by cash flow. Alphabet uses TRIM_PROFIT_BOOKING and Oracle uses REPLACE_UNDERPERFORMER as the nearest labels, and both dossiers disclose the deviation.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.

Earnings dates. Alphabet (2026-10-28), Oracle's Investor Day (2026-10-28) and Chipotle (2026-10-28) are company-confirmed. NVIDIA's 2026-11-18 report date and Oracle's Q2 date (about 2026-12-09) are estimates.
