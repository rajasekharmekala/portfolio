What each decision rests on

Vistra. The nuclear, gas and retail power company has a 20-year contract with AWS for 1,200 megawatts and one with Meta for 2,609 megawatts, and guided adjusted free cash flow implies about negative 1 percent growth is priced in. But return on invested capital is 11.0 percent and free cash flow margin 11.7 percent, both just under the 12 percent floors, and 2027 earnings estimates fell 8.23 percent in 90 days. One leverage rule applies: net debt over adjusted EBITDA above 3.2 times on filed numbers ends the idea, and the pending Cogentrix purchase would put it at about 3.25 times if funded with new borrowing. The low 2027 estimate would give $129.68, 7.47 percent below the close.

GE Aerospace. Return on invested capital is 31.4 percent, free cash flow margin is 17.2 percent and the backlog is $212.1 billion, so every Layer 1 and Layer 2 test passes. The stock fell after its 2026-10-20 beat-and-raise report and is deeply oversold, but it trades near 35 times 2026 earnings, and a multiple-only move to 26.5 times would give $270.57, 5.3 percent lower. The red team's stricter entry lowered the order ceiling to $283.00, and the order is conditional on funding, because buying 5 shares would leave cash at about 1.7 percent of net worth against a 5 percent floor.

Cloudflare. Revenue grew 35.9 percent with 120 percent net retention, but share-based pay is 20.2 percent of revenue, free cash flow after that pay is negative $192.8 million, and return on invested capital is negative on every basis. The reverse DCF needs 45.84 percent a year against a 34.69 percent analyst forecast, and the stock trades at 193.9 times 2027 earnings. A 2.5 to 1 ratio at today's price would need a $450.48 target. The chart is a healthy Stage 2, so this is a quality and valuation call.

Spotify. Return on invested capital is 22.5 percent, free cash flow margin is 17.0 percent, and subscribers are 306 million, but the stock is 2.74 ATR above its average with a relative strength index of 70.61, and reward-to-risk is 0.32 to 1 at the close. The ledger's 23.91 percent reverse DCF mixed euros with dollars and was replaced with 11.79 percent in euros.
