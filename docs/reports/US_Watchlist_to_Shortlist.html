Why the business remains on the watchlist

Apple links devices, software, chips, stores and services. iPhone remains the earnings anchor. Services has much higher margins. Q3 FY2026 sales were $109.417B, up 16% year over year. iPhone grew 22%, Mac 29% and Services 12%; iPad fell 6% [1][2][3]. Nine-month operating cash flow was $116.996B and capex was $6.799B [2]. Apple does not report customer churn, device retention by group or AI use and revenue [1][2][5]. Without those figures, investors cannot tell whether owners return more often or spend more per account. The next test is whether new products and software promises create durable demand.

The mix matters more than one growth rate. Services generated $109.158B in FY2025 and reported a 75.4% gross margin, compared with 36.8% for Products [1][12]. That margin supports research, retail and buybacks; it also makes App Store rules and payments regulation financially important [13][14]. This evidence makes the software roadmap the next execution test.
