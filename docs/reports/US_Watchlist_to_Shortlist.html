What each decision rests on

Nasdaq. The business passes every hard red line: net debt to EBITDA is 2.47 times, interest cover is 7.9 times and free cash flow margin is 39.1 percent. Return on invested capital is 10.6 percent on the reported basis, which misses the 12 percent standard, and 12.5 percent when the amortization of acquired intangibles is added back, a narrow pass. It qualifies as a tactical breakout. The problem is price: the stock closed 4.11 ATR above its 50-day average with a relative strength index of 74.18, so reward-to-risk is only 1.14 to 1. The plan is therefore a confirmed-hold limit order, not a resting one. The order is armed only after a daily close back above the 50-day average on rising volume, with the target and ratio rerun the same evening. The $98.39 ceiling is a snapshot that depends on a 25.99 times multiple, and a fall to the 19.88 times year-end 2023 multiple would put the value at $94.23, below the order price. For that reason the tier was cut from TIER_3 to TIER_4 after the red team's challenge.

Estée Lauder. The turnaround is real: adjusted operating margin rose from 8.0 to 11.2 percent and free cash flow almost doubled. The market has already paid for it, at 32.7 times fiscal 2027 consensus earnings. Our probability-weighted value of $108.51 sits level with the $108.82 close. The watchlist seed's 4.8 to 1 depended on a $96.67 stop above the 20-day swing low of $88.68, which breaks the stop rule. On the rule stop of $86.96 the ratio is 0.08 to 1 at the close. The 2.5 floor clears only at $93.73 or lower. Fiscal first-quarter results are due on 2026-11-02 before the open, so any fill before then carries a gap risk that a stop cannot cover.

Noble. Noble reported third-quarter results after the close on 2026-10-26: adjusted EBITDA of $201 million (down 5 percent), an adjusted loss of $0.57 a share against a $0.23 consensus, negative free cash flow, narrowed 2026 EBITDA guidance and higher capex guidance. Only one of the four turnaround signals is positive. A review is triggered if 2027 EBITDA evidence points toward about $1.2 billion.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all three dossiers and this report; NE also BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. The dossiers cite annual filings, quarterly releases and other pages older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed 77 exception lines for NE, 53 for EL and 87 for NDAQ. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company reports.

Ledger fundamentals feed. The Yahoo fundamentals feed returned HTTP 401 during the ledger build, so the ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow and consensus-revision fields were empty for all three names. The analysts computed the reverse DCF with the repository's solver from filed cash flows (NE +23.76 percent a year, EL +12.08 percent, NDAQ +8.9 percent on equity value) and took consensus figures from fetched public pages. The automatic rows in each Chapter 5 table therefore read N/A. Consensus figures come from third-party pages, and NE's predate its third-quarter miss.

Price-bar timing. The first ledger build, a few minutes after the close, had no 2026-10-26 stock bars yet. It was retried once and the second build was current for all three names.

Gate-table wording. The renderer's momentum-gate table prints simplified limits in its rule column (for example 1.2 ATR) while the ledger applied the limits shown in the measured column (for example 1.6 ATR). Each dossier says so in plain words above the table. The renderer script was not changed, and the wording is a flagged defect for a later fix.

Median multiples. No source publishes a 3-year median multiple for EL or NDAQ, and NE's is not available, so each dossier uses a labelled proxy or sensitivity and says so.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead.

Batch size. Only three names were eligible, so the batch is short by one.
