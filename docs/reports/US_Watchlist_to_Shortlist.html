What each decision rests on

Adobe. Free cash flow was $10.6 billion over the last year, a 40.8% margin, and the price implies that cash flow shrinks about 4.9% a year for ten years. The base target of $331 against a $215.75 stop gives 2.91 to 1, below the 3.5 turnaround floor, and the operating scorecard falls short of the 4 of 5 pass minimum. Next catalyst: the fourth-quarter report around 9 December 2026, the first under the incoming chief executive.

UnitedHealth. Margins are recovering (medical care ratio 88.4% against 89.9% a year earlier) and the company raised its 2026 outlook for the third time. The base target of $450 against a $347.03 exit level gives only 0.89 to 1 at $401.43. The $360.00 to $369.90 zone is an alert to re-underwrite, not a buy signal. Open legal and tax matters are not priced into the scenarios.

NVIDIA. Return on invested capital is 80.2%, free cash flow margin 41.9% and the company holds net cash. The verdict is a chart verdict: Stage 3 and all three momentum tests fail. Two operating watch items are customer concentration and $530.5 billion of commitments, guarantees and supply obligations. Next catalyst: the third-quarter report, estimated around 18 November 2026 and not yet confirmed by the company.

Meta. Revenue grew 28% in the June 2026 quarter, but stock-based pay is 11.0% of revenue against a 5% limit, free cash flow fell to 1.3% of revenue in that quarter, and the 2026 capital spending guide is $130 to $145 billion. Next catalyst: the third-quarter report on 21 October 2026, five days after the data date.

Run notes and gate exceptions

The workflow requires a disclosure of every gate that the existing evidence cannot satisfy. Four exceptions apply to this batch. First, the ledger's Reverse DCF and consensus-revision blocks were empty, because Yahoo's fundamentals endpoint returned an authorization error (HTTP 401) during the ledger build. Each dossier therefore computed its Reverse DCF from SEC filings with the repository solver and took revisions from fetched pages; the first rows of each Chapter 5 expectations table and the Estimate Revisions cell in each key-figures strip still show the blank ledger values, and each dossier says so before the table. Second, stale as-of dates, as the next table shows. Third, secondary sources (Yahoo, StockAnalysis, MarketBeat and similar pages) supplied consensus figures, price targets and median multiples for every company, because several aggregators returned HTTP 403 and company investor-relations pages carry no estimates. Fourth, earnings dates for NVIDIA and Adobe are aggregator estimates, not confirmed on the company's own calendar.
