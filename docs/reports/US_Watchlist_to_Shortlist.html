Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all three dossiers and this report; VCYT and AJG also BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. The dossiers cite annual filings, quarterly releases and other pages older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed 75 exception lines for VCYT, 133 for AJG and 88 for AGYS. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company reports (AJG on 2026-10-29, VCYT on 2026-11-04).

Ledger fundamentals feed. Yahoo refused the fundamentals feed with HTTP 401 and 429 errors, so the ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow and consensus-revision fields were empty for all three names, even after a second build that listed them as extras. The analysts computed the reverse DCF with the repository's solver from filed cash flows and took consensus figures from fetched public pages. The automatic rows in each Chapter 5 table and the key-figures label for estimate revisions therefore read N/A or NO_CONSENSUS. Consensus figures come from third-party pages, and 30 and 90 day revision history is not published by a free source.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead.

Blocked sources. Several pages could not be fetched (HTTP 403 or 401 from some investor-relations, rating-agency and transcript sites), so credit ratings are not cited for AJG and some call quotes for VCYT come from an aggregator. Each dossier lists them.

Batch size and claims. Only three names were eligible, so the batch is short by one. An earlier US watchlist run held claims on the same three names until they expired at 06:30 Pacific on 2026-10-28; it had produced no output, and this run claimed the names after the expiry.
