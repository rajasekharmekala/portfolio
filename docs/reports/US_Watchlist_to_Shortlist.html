What each decision rests on

Agilysys. The hospitality software maker passes every hard red line and every static floor: net cash of $137.6 million, a free cash flow margin of 23.3 percent, and stock compensation of 7.0 percent of revenue. Its return on invested capital is 16.6 percent net of cash but only 10.5 percent on the gross basis, so that floor passes only by the adjusted routes. Subscription revenue grew 32.6 percent and the Layer 2 scorecard is 4.5 of 5.0 with one watch item, the pace and cost of the Marriott rollout. The problem is price, not business: reward-to-risk is 0.38 to 1 at the close. The red team showed that a price-only fall to $99.00 is itself a lower multiple, since $99.00 is 30.1 times fiscal 2028 earnings, so any fill now needs a fresh support test and a fresh valuation test, and the size was cut from TIER_3 to TIER_4.

Nasdaq. The exchange and market-technology company has a 39 percent free cash flow margin, net debt of 2.47 times EBITDA and management that beat its Adenza integration promises. The red team showed that return on invested capital adjusted for acquired-intangible amortization is 12.5 percent at the trailing tax rate but 11.8 percent at the top of guided tax, so the compounder pass is not firm and the dossier reclassifies it as a turnaround candidate with the 2.5 to 1 floor. At the $104.36 close the stock is 3.93 ATR above its 50-day average with a relative strength index of 72.8, and the entry zone fell to $92.00 to $93.40. Instead of a fixed 25 times earnings trigger, the plan now requires a same-evening valuation test at the order price.

Veracyte. The cancer-diagnostics company is profitable and cash generative, with net cash and a trailing free cash flow margin of 29.5 percent. Return on invested capital is a marginal 11.1 percent and stock compensation of 8.6 percent of revenue passes only under the 15 percent allowance. The red team showed that when stock pay and normal tax are both charged against cash flow, the base value is about $40.02 and the zone gives a negative reward-to-risk, so the zone is provisional and is not approved for an order today. The next results come on 2026-11-04, and the final Medicare fee table follows in November.

Arthur J. Gallagher. The broker passes the debt and cash lines (net debt to adjusted EBITDAC 2.42 times, free cash flow margin 14.7 percent) but misses the 12 percent return-on-capital floor on every basis (5.8 percent reported, 9.9 percent fully adjusted), so it qualifies only as a turnaround candidate. It closed 1.64 ATR above its 50-day average with reward-to-risk of 0.52 to 1. The zone survives the base case at 2.56 to 1 but fails two stress tests, so no order is armed until after the third-quarter report on 2026-10-29 and a refreshed target and stop clear 2.5 to 1 at each order price.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run completed with the status PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS. AGYS and VCYT also carry BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. The dossiers cite annual filings, quarterly releases and other pages older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed 103 exception lines for VCYT, 61 for AJG, 46 for AGYS and 82 for NDAQ. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company reports (AJG on 2026-10-29, VCYT on 2026-11-04). Records for earlier fiscal years stay excepted by design.

Red team model. The configured red-team model was blocked by the provider on several calls. The first-round red teams for AJG and NDAQ ran on the configured model after one retry. The round-2 red teams for AGYS and VCYT ran on the runner's default agent, and both returned APPROVE. The substitution is recorded in the manifest and in those two dossiers.

Ledger fundamentals. The ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow and consensus-revision fields were empty for VCYT and AJG, so the analysts computed the reverse DCF from filed cash flows and took consensus figures from fetched public pages. The first rows of those two Chapter 5 tables are written by the builder from the ledger and read N/A. For AGYS and NDAQ the ledger values were used and compared with the filings, and the dossiers explain where they differ.

Builder wording. A few cells are emitted by the dossier builder and cannot be edited: the 50-day trend label (it compares against 10 sessions ago), the sizing-rule text that names a 200-day moving average and a Stage 1 base, and some table-first headings. The review packet's one remaining mechanical flag for AGYS, NDAQ and VCYT reads that rule text as a ticker, and the reviewers marked it a false positive. The dossiers explain each in prose.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead.

Blocked sources. Some pages could not be fetched (HTTP 403 or a bot wall from rating-agency, exchange and transcript sites), so the dossiers say which figures come from a second route. Each dossier lists them.

Overrides. The earlier dossiers for these four names were generated on 2026-10-26 (NDAQ, research as of 2026-10-26) and on 2026-10-28 at about 07:36 Pacific (AGYS, AJG and VCYT, research as of 2026-10-27). This run's research date is the same or later and its generation time is later, so it replaces them under the allowed-override rule in the common workflow rules.
