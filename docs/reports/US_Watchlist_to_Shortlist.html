1. The decision at a glance

The table gives each verdict under the repository's three-layer rules. Layer 1 tests the financial floors and the type of business (the archetype), using insurer equivalents of return on capital, cash margin and debt. Layer 2 scores three to five operating measures that run that business. Layer 3 turns the chart into an entry rule and a position size. Reward-to-risk is the gain from the entry price to the base target divided by the loss from the entry price to the stop. The floor is 2.0 to 1 for Core Alpha names and 2.5 to 1 for turnaround names, so a name that clears the floor only at a lower price needs a limit order, not a market purchase.

What each decision rests on

Arch Capital. The third-quarter combined ratio was 82.5 percent (under 100 means an underwriting profit), the annualized return on equity was 18.1 percent and book value per share was $67.90. The seed note's figures all checked out. Net premiums written fell 3.0 percent. The base value of $114 is $76 of book value at 1.50 times, and at a lower 1.43 times the value is $108.68. That stress cut the top of the buy zone from $97.50 to $96.30. Our own reverse DCF, the growth the price assumes, is -5.33 percent a year at an 18 times terminal multiple, and it is -1.88 percent at 10 times.

American International Group. The seed note was out of date: AIG sold its last Corebridge shares in May 2026 and is now a pure property and casualty insurer. Its combined ratio improved from 95.8 in 2021 to 89.0 in the second quarter of 2026, and core return on equity rose from 9.1 to 11.1 percent, still under the 12 percent compounder floor, so the dossier uses the turnaround archetype. Third-quarter results arrive after the close on 2026-11-03. A 9.0 times multiple or a 10 percent earnings cut alone would lower the target to $79.29 and the ratio at $76.00 to 0.69 to 1, so the zone is rebuilt after the release. One reserve rule governs arming, cancelling and rejecting the order, and rejection overrides arming.

Markel. Markel compounds book value per share through underwriting, a $18.8 billion float invested mostly in stocks, and its Markel Ventures businesses. Return on tangible equity is 12.9 percent over twelve months, but the GAAP return is 9.8 percent, and consensus earnings for 2026 were cut 9.8 percent in 90 days (pages dated after the session). The third-quarter results and an Enstar reinsurance cover on about $4.8 billion of reserves were announced on 2026-10-28, after the close. The chart is a Stage 1 base below a falling 200-day average. The plan is a half-size first fill with limits at $1,750 to $1,810, each tested again before it is used.

Kinsale. Kinsale has the best underwriting margin of the group: a 75.4 percent combined ratio in the third quarter and an operating return on equity of 25.6 percent. Its gross premium has been falling about 5 percent a quarter, which is the one failed Layer 2 measure. The reviewers' stress of a 22 percent sustainable return on equity (instead of 24 percent) lowered the value to $354.73 and the highest buy price that still clears 2.0 to 1 from $329 to $323. There is no resting order; a close inside the zone only starts three checks before any purchase.
