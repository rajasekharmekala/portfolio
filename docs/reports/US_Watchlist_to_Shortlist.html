1. The decision at a glance

The table gives each verdict under the repository's three-layer rules. Layer 1 tests the financial floors and the type of business, called the archetype, using the equivalents that suit an insurer, a pipeline or a gas producer. Layer 2 scores three to five operating measures that run that business. Layer 3 turns the chart into an entry rule and a position size. Reward-to-risk is the gain from the entry price to the base target divided by the loss from the entry price to the stop. The floor is 2.0 to 1 for Core Alpha names, so a name that clears the floor only at a lower price needs a limit order, not a market purchase.

What each decision rests on

Berkshire Hathaway. The business holds $365.5 billion of cash and Treasury bills against $128.6 billion of borrowings, an insurance float of $177.5 billion and a net-cash balance sheet. Return on equity is 11.7 percent over twelve months, 0.3 point under the 12 percent floor, and 6.6 percent on operating earnings alone, so the gate is PASSED_CONDITIONAL, not a clean pass. The base value is $381 of book value per B share at 1.50 times, which gives $571. The hinge is the multiple: if the market prices operating earnings at 20.8 times, the value is about $466 and no price in the zone clears the floor, which would turn the verdict to REJECTED. The probability-weighted value of $547.8 is only 1.4 percent above the close.

RenaissanceRe. The seed figures all checked out from the Q3 release: combined ratio 79.7 percent, operating return on equity 18.9 percent and tangible book $242.51 a share, or 1.37 times at the close. The seed left out a GAAP net loss to common shareholders of $161.4 million caused by $879.8 million of bond price losses, and a combined ratio of 92.4 percent before reserve releases. The base value is $302.83 of end-2027 book at 1.20 times, or $363. The January 1, 2027 renewals are the dated catalyst, and a property-catastrophe price cut of 15 percent or more would rebuild the target.

Williams Companies. About 90 percent of earnings are fee-based, leverage is 3.76 times, and the growth plan is real, but the stock trades at 30.7 times 2026 estimates, the highest in its peer set, and cash after dividends and growth spending is negative $2.06 billion over twelve months. The ledger's support distance is 3.33 ATR from the swing low, while the momentum gate measures 1.05 ATR from the 50-day average; the dossier explains both and uses the lower anchor for the zone and stop. The 2.01 to 1 ratio at the zone top does not survive a lower multiple or a lower earnings estimate, so the zone is provisional until the 2026-11-02 release and the 2026-11-03 call are read.

BKV Corporation. The close of $21.17 sits below a falling 50-day average of $23.36, which sits below a falling 200-day average of $26.63, and the recovery test fails on one condition. That is a Stage 4 breakdown, which the rules treat as a technical disqualifier. The financial floors also fail on return on invested capital (10.8 percent against 12 percent) and negative free cash flow, and the 2026 capital plan of $690 million to $875 million is about twice the $356 million of twelve-month operating cash flow. The reopen test needs all four recovery conditions to pass together on a fresh gate run after the 2026-11-05 report.
