What each decision rests on

Walmart. The operating story is healthy: eCommerce sales grew 23 percent, advertising grew 38 percent and three quarters in a row came in above guidance. But free cash flow margin is 1.84 percent against the 12 percent floor, the shares trade at 42.2 times earnings, and reward-to-risk is negative 0.12 to 1 at the close. Moving only the multiple from 37.9 times (our own proxy for a median, not a published figure) to 30 times would put the shares 22.4 percent lower. The three $145 calls expiring 2027-06-17 would need $148.82 to break even, 24.1 percent above the close, and every expiry-matched scenario ends below the strike. The plan sells all three by 2026-11-18 so none are held into the 2026-11-19 report.

EDC. This Direxion fund gives three times the daily move of the MSCI Emerging Markets Index. It tracks well, but it costs about 11 percent a year all-in on top of volatility decay of about 17.6 percent a year at current volatility. The index is cheap at a forward price-to-earnings of 9.72, but that does not outweigh the cost over a 13-week window. Swap bank names and funding spreads are not published, so the dossier does not call the product safe. The seed's 10.4 percent ROIC and its 54 percent five-year loss could not be reproduced as stated.

TARK. The fund delivers twice ARKK's daily return with a correlation of 0.9978, but it loses about 9.9 percent a year to costs and decay, so ARKK must gain about 12.6 percent a year for the fund to break even. Net assets are about $11.77 million, and the sponsor has closed funds in 2025 and 2026. If recent higher costs persisted, the central upside would shrink to 1.52 percent. The seed's return on capital and share-based pay figures are not defined for a fund.

MSTX. At 82 percent volatility in MicroStrategy, a flat three months costs the fund about 22 percent, and Strategy would need to gain about 13.3 percent for the fund to break even. Raising volatility to 100 percent with Strategy unchanged would put the fund at $11.31, below the stop. The position is 3.41 percent of net worth against a 2.00 percent ceiling, so the plan sells 96 shares with a $16.50 limit from 2026-10-26 through the close of 2026-10-28, with a market-order fallback in the last 30 minutes, and keeps 82.480594 shares (1.58 percent). The dossier states plainly that the stop does not cap the loss through a gap or an overnight bitcoin move. The red team's changes were made but not rerun, so treat this dossier as less settled.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. MSTX also carries BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites annual filings, prospectuses and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: WMT 67, EDC 59, TARK 29 and MSTX 50. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company or fund reports.

Estimate data. The session ledger's reverse discounted-cash-flow figure for Walmart was replaced (12.62 percent on operating cash flow less depreciation, because the ledger's 75 percent haircut is not a reported figure). It is not defined for the three funds, and the dossiers use look-through valuations of the underlying. The ledger's Walmart revision label could not be verified on a public page and is labelled unverified.

Fund data gaps. EDC's swap bank names and financing spread are not published in any filing reached, and TARK's bid-ask spread and counterparty ratings were not verified. MSTX's reserve and preferred-stock balances are about four months old and are described as a possible cushion, not a verified one.

Verdict labels. The holding taxonomy has no label for trimming a leveraged fund to a tier ceiling when the target is below the price. MSTX uses TRIM_PROFIT_BOOKING as the nearest label, and the dossier discloses the deviation and that it is not an endorsement to sell the rest.

Options book. The recorded fields for the Walmart calls (value, underlying price, delta and days to expiry) are stale, and only a delayed last-trade quote was found, so the audit uses forward values and the option value is unverified.

Record limits. The shortlist schema accepts only a positive asymmetry ratio, so the MSTX record carries a 0.01 placeholder while the dossier and verdict text show the true negative ratio.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.

Earnings dates. Walmart's 2026-11-19 date is company-confirmed. Strategy's Q3 report date is not confirmed, and the funds have no earnings dates.

Email. No name newly qualifies for the shortlist, so no email is sent under the conditional policy. Holdings audits and rejections send nothing.
