Business quality, operating proof, and capital demands

TSMC is a dedicated foundry that manufactures chips from customer designs. Its FY2025 filing says wafer fabrication made up 86% of revenue, while its ten largest customers supplied 78%. HPC was 58% of 2025 revenue and rose to 68% in Q3 2026. This mix supports strong demand, but it also ties growth to a concentrated group of large customers and AI investment plans. See the FY2025 Form 20-F.

Q3 2026 revenue was NT$1,494.24 billion, up 50.9% year over year in NT dollars. Net income rose 68.7% to NT$763.06 billion, and diluted ADR EPS was US$4.60. Gross margin was 66.4%, down 1.3 percentage points sequentially as 2nm ramp costs diluted margin. Advanced technologies supplied 79% of wafer revenue; N2 represented 8%. Management guided to Q4 revenue of US$52.3–$54.3 billion, a 65%–67% gross margin, and about 44% FY2026 revenue growth in US dollars. The current capex plan is US$60–$64 billion. The SEC Q3 release, Q3 presentation, and Q3 call transcript support these figures.
