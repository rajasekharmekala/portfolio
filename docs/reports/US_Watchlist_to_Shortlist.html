What each decision rests on

Adobe. Return on invested capital is 40.1 percent, free cash flow margin is 40.8 percent and stock pay is 7.97 percent of revenue, just under the 8 percent limit. Net new annual recurring revenue is down 36 to 37 percent from a year earlier, which is the one failed operating measure. The market price already assumes the business shrinks (a reverse discounted-cash-flow reading of -3.9 percent a year), so the case needs recurring revenue to stabilise. The reviewer did not approve the dossier after two rounds, so treat it as the least settled of the four. The watchlist idea also listed Adobe as a holding, but the Schwab and Robinhood records show no Adobe shares, only one $350 call option expiring 2027-06-17, whose break-even of $370.70 sits above the $360 bull target.

UnitedHealth. The medical care ratio improved to 88.4 percent from 89.9 percent and the Optum margin to 5.6 percent from 3.6 percent, which qualifies it as a turnaround. Free cash flow margin of 5.08 percent misses the 12 percent compounder floor, so it cannot be treated as a compounder. At $418.09 the stock is 4.87 average true ranges above support, so the dossier sets a conditional fill rule: the target must be at least the entry price plus 2.5 times the distance from the entry to the $351.20 stop. If the share price multiple falls back to its historical 17.35 times, reward-to-risk at the top of the zone drops to 1.07:1, and the dossier says to skip that part of the zone.

NVIDIA. Data Center revenue is $89.0 billion, up 117 percent, at a 75.0 percent gross margin, and free cash flow margin is 41.9 percent. The risks are $279 billion of supply commitments, up to $108.5 billion of guarantees tied to customers, and the next earnings report, expected 2026-11-18 but not confirmed by the company. The price sits 0.13 average true ranges above its 20-day swing low, yet the momentum gate reads NO_MOMENTUM, so the dossier uses a limit order and requires the gate to be re-run at the actual fill.

Meta. Revenue grew 26 percent in the third quarter, but capital spending was 54 percent of revenue and free cash flow was negative $2.12 billion. Trailing free cash flow margin of 10.4 percent and stock pay of 11.2 percent of revenue both fail the compounder floors, and none of the turnaround signals the rules require could be verified. The decision is REJECTED, moved to the watchlist archive with a review date of 2027-01-21 or earlier if free cash flow turns positive.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. Adobe and Meta also carry BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT, because the reviewer's second and last round did not approve them. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Adobe, UnitedHealth and NVIDIA cite prior-period filings, earnings calls and rule announcements that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: Adobe 20, UnitedHealth 17 and NVIDIA 48. Meta reported on 2026-10-21 and needed none. Each is a prior-period fact, so the exceptions lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company files its next report.

Estimate data. The data vendor's consensus and valuation feed returned an authorization error on the run date, so the session ledger holds no reverse discounted-cash-flow or consensus figures for these four names. The underwriting computed the reverse discounted-cash-flow growth rates from cited SEC filings with the repository's own solver at the 10 percent discount rate and 18 times terminal multiple in the scoring file. Consensus earnings and 90-day revisions were taken from fetchable public pages where they exist and shown as not available where they do not (for example the 90-day change for Adobe, the 30-day and 90-day changes for Meta, and the 30-day changes for UnitedHealth). This lowers confidence in the consensus readings. It would be cured by a working vendor feed on a later run.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.

Earnings dates. The next report dates for Adobe (2026-12-09), NVIDIA (2026-11-18) and Meta (about 2027-01-27) are estimates from third-party calendars, not company confirmations. UnitedHealth had not announced its date. Each dossier names the date as unconfirmed.

Peer names not on the watchlist. The dossiers surfaced INTU and ADSK (from Adobe), CI (from UnitedHealth), AVGO (from NVIDIA) and PINS (from Meta). None was added to the watchlist in this run, because adding them would widen the underwritten queue without their own research.
