What each decision rests on

NBIL. The watchlist text called it an ultra-short Treasury ETF yielding 5.22 percent. The fund's own filings show a 2x daily-reset fund on Nebius that holds Treasury bills plus one swap with Clear Street. Since its $25.00 launch the fund is down 55.5 percent while Nebius is up 27.1 percent, which is volatility decay. The chart is Stage 4 and fails the recovery test, so the dossier sets an exit plan: sell 25 shares at the open on 2026-10-26, a $13.50 limit for the rest with a hard stop at $9.13, and flat by 2026-11-06 unless Nebius announces its report date earlier. That last date is provisional.

Arista. Return on invested capital is 28.5 percent, free cash flow margin is 47.2 percent, and the company has $13.3 billion of net cash. The seed's 32 percent ROIC was return on equity, and its net cash of $8.6 billion was understated. Gross margin slipped to 62.9 percent from 65.2 percent, and two customers are 42 percent of revenue. The shares are held with no adds before the 2026-11-03 report. The $230 call is provisionally a replace on an option-specific test, and its live price has to be checked before the report. The reviewer did not approve this dossier after two rounds, so treat it as the least settled of the four.

DigitalOcean. Return on invested capital is 7.6 percent, stock pay is 9.4 percent of revenue, and a July equity raise of about $1.47 billion retired $472 million of convertible principal. The position is 6.17 percent of net worth against a 2.0 percent ceiling and a 1.4 percent cap before the 2026-11-03 report. The dossier says to sell about 42.2 shares by the close on 2026-11-02, keep about 12.4 shares under the $83.68 stop, and close the call in the same window. On the company's own cash guide the base value falls to about $82, so the $88.34 to $88.92 zone is a watch level, not an order.

CrowdStrike. The watchlist labelled it a holding, but the position records show no shares and no options, so the dossier treats it as a candidate. ARR is $5.84 billion, up 25 percent, and free cash flow margin is 28.1 percent. Stock pay is 23.1 percent of revenue against a 15 percent cap, and the owner cash margin after stock pay is 5.1 percent against 10 percent. At the close the reward-to-risk is negative, and a multiple-only stress to 60 times cash flow gives $132.30, 48.6 percent lower. It would be re-underwritten if the price enters $157.16 to $170.06 and stock pay trends below 20 percent of revenue.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS for all four dossiers and this report. Arista also carries BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT, because the reviewer's second and last round did not approve it. Each dossier repeats its own exceptions in a section before its sources.

Stale source dates. Every dossier cites prior-period filings, earnings calls and releases that are older than 60 days, and no newer version exists for the historical periods they describe. The citation checker was run with its stale-date allowance and printed an exception line for each record: NBIL 85, Arista 78, DigitalOcean 95 and CrowdStrike 75. They lower confidence only in how current those specific figures are, and they clear as each company files its next report: Arista and DigitalOcean on 2026-11-03, and CrowdStrike in early December.

Estimate data. Several figures in the session ledger did not survive a check against filings, so each dossier rebuilt its reverse discounted-cash-flow figure from cited filings at the 10 percent discount rate and 18 times terminal multiple in the scoring file. Arista's trailing free cash flow could not be reproduced, DigitalOcean's base used a proxy above reported free cash flow, and CrowdStrike's did not match the filings. For a leveraged fund the figure is not defined, and the dossier says so. Consensus values that could not be matched to a public page were replaced or shown as not available.

Option prices. The recorded fields for the Arista and DigitalOcean calls are stale against the ledger, and neither has a live quote, so their verdicts are provisional until a broker quote is checked before 2026-11-03.

Holding labels. The holding taxonomy has no label for a partial sale or for a hold at Stage 3. DigitalOcean's partial sale uses REPLACE_UNDERPERFORMER for the excess, and Arista's shares use KEEP_COMPOUNDING as the nearest label while disclosing that the Stage 2 clause is not met.

Structural validator. The repository's structural validator for reports accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so it cannot return a pass for a company dossier. The readability check, the citation check, the review packet's code checks and the Research Desk layout check were run instead, and all passed.

Earnings dates. Arista's 2026-11-03 and DigitalOcean's 2026-11-03 dates are company-confirmed. The Nebius Q3 date and CrowdStrike's date of about 2026-12-01 are estimates, and each dossier labels its own.
