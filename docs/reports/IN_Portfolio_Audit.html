Chapter 7: Convergence, Hysteresis Locks & Monitoring Contract

To ensure short-term reruns never whipsaw your orders, our anchor plan is saved in data/allocations/IN.json. Three deterministic rules lock stability across any 5-day rerun window. First, a ±2.5% price hysteresis band keeps borderline calls steady during normal market noise. Second, held stops ratchet upward only.
