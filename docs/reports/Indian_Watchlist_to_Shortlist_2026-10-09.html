How the hard-gate estimate works

The number

The report treats a candidate as eligible only when every applicable Layer 1 number clears its floor.

How it was calculated

The cash test is three fiscal years of cash flow from operations divided by three fiscal years of profit after tax; the asymmetry test is target minus close divided by close minus stop.

The inputs

Inputs are the frozen ledger, deep-fetched financial tables and cited exchange or investor-relations records, each labeled with its as-of date.

The range

A passing range is not a forecast: it is the permitted band defined by the scoring configuration, including ROCE, leverage, cash conversion and support distance.

If the number is off

An unverified value becomes N/A and the candidate stays rejected until a primary source repairs the evidence.

Risk boundary. These are research dispositions, not trade instructions. A candidate can return to the queue only when the named rule repairs: technical structure, financial returns, cash conversion, target anchoring or balance-sheet evidence. No candidate is promoted because it is cheap, oversold or associated with an investor lead.
