Glossary and decision rules

ROCE is operating profit divided by capital employed. CFO/PAT is operating cash flow divided by profit after tax over the specified three-year window. ATR is average true range, a way to express price distance in units of normal movement. RSI is a speed indicator; an oversold reading can occur during a continuing decline. A target anchor is a cited forward-EPS times median P/E or a measured move. Asymmetry is target minus close divided by close minus stop. The rules change only when the cited metric crosses its floor and the new technical evidence passes the momentum and support tests.
