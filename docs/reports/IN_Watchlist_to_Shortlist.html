How one number was worked out: RNITAI's distance from its stop

The number

RNITAI's price sits 5.8 average true ranges above its review stop.

How it was calculated

Take the close minus the stop, then divide by the average true range (ATR). The close is ₹108.00, the stop is ₹83.57, and the ATR is ₹4.24. The gap is ₹24.43, and ₹24.43 divided by ₹4.24 is 5.8.

The inputs

The close and the ATR come from the 12 October 2026 ledger. The stop is the 20-day swing low of ₹85.69 less ₹2.12, which is the buffer the dossier uses.

The range

A distance of 5.8 ATR is wide. A normal day moves about one ATR, so the stock would need to fall roughly six normal days' worth before the stop is reached.

If the number is off

If the ATR were higher, the same gap would look smaller. A stop closer to the price would make the verdict more sensitive to a single bad session, which is why the dossier treats ₹83.57 as a review level and not a guaranteed exit.

  • Unverifiable Screener figures. The Screener pages fetched for this run show blank placeholders for several multi-year figures. Where a figure could not be found in a cached primary or secondary source, the dossier shows N/A with the reason, and no figure was filled from memory.
  • Parallel publication. While this run was in progress, another underwriting pass of the same four names was published (commit fb4ebc4). The owner chose to publish this batch over it. The verdict wording for INTERARCH and SANGHVIMOV differs between the two passes: this run uses REPLACE_UNDERPERFORMER, and the earlier pass used "exited in stages". The records and dossiers in this run are the ones now in place.
  • Portfolio-audit floors. The packet builder also ran the book-length checks written for full portfolio audits (total prose length, a case note for every holding, a glossary heading and a market research record). They do not apply to a four-name batch digest, so they are recorded here as an exception rather than met.
  • Reviewer routing. One round-1 reviewer session was given the INTERARCH fix instructions by mistake and applied the INTERARCH content fixes. The INTERARCH review pass that approved the result was a separate session. The RNITAI round-2 reviewer was a fresh agent, so it did not share that session's context.
