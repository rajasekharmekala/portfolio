This page reports the four watchlist names that were underwritten in this batch, and what happened to each one. The framework has three layers that a candidate must pass in order. Layer 1 holds the hard floors: return on capital, debt, three-year cash conversion, the technical posture of the share price, a momentum test and an upside-to-downside ratio. Layer 2 scores three to five operating measures, and Layer 3 maps the distance from support onto an entry timing.

A candidate that fails any Layer 1 floor is rejected and archived, and its Layers 2 and 3 are not read. The momentum gate is computed in code from the frozen price ledger. It fails a name that is neither in a rising trend nor entering one from a tight base, and a name that fails the gate gets no allocation however good the business is.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This batch was published with gate exceptions. Each dossier carries its own list, and the list is repeated here so the batch can be read in one place. A gate exception is a check that the existing evidence could not fully satisfy. It is disclosed here, and it is not described as a pass.

  • Stale as-of dates. Most of the financial records are for the year to 31 March 2026 or the quarter to 30 June 2026. Both are more than 60 days before the session, and no newer filing existed when the batch ran. The citation check was run with its stale-date exception, and every as-of date was kept at its true period. Counts by name are in each dossier: APOLLOHOSP 12 records, KALPATPOWR 22, ADFFOODS 19, SUNPHARMA 46 of 52 found records.
  • Sources that could not be read. The company results PDFs and some exchange filings are binary files that the research tool could not read, so those figures were not used. Where a figure was in a listed source but not in the cached text, it was not recorded.
  • Figures not found. A forward EPS anchor and a three-year median P/E were searched for, three queries each, and not found in any readable source. The asymmetry floor is therefore marked unverified for KALPATPOWR, ADFFOODS and SUNPHARMA, and is not counted as a pass.
  • APOLLOHOSP debt floor. The Screener-derived debt-to-equity is 0.90 against a 0.70 limit. A company-definition figure of 0.62 from an aggregator could not be recorded with its source, so it was removed, and the floor is shown as unresolved. The verdict does not depend on it.
  • Reviewer gate. Each dossier went through the independent reviewer on the configured model, with at most two rounds. KALPATPOWR, ADFFOODS and SUNPHARMA were approved in round 2, with editorial notes fixed. APOLLOHOSP was not approved: round 2 returned REVISE on two wording items and one unrecorded comparison, which were fixed after the round limit with no further review. Its status is BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT, not APPROVE. The red team returned verdict_holds for all four names in round 1, so no second red-team round was needed.
  • Batch summary check. This summary is a batch report, not an audit, so the audit word-count and case-note floors were not applied to it. Its figures are taken from the four dossiers and the records, and each of those carries the full gates.
  • Ledger failures outside this batch. The price ledger could not fetch C2C, DUMMYHEG and WSTLIFESTY. None of them is in this batch, and no figure in this page depends on them.
  • Holdings. The holdings record is treated as accurate, and the ledger was built with the stale-holdings allowance. No holding is changed by this batch.

Notes, glossary and sources

The glossary terms used in this page are defined where they first appear in the dossiers. The main ones are these. ROCE is the return a company earns on the money invested in it, a measure of how well it uses capital. The 50-day average is the mean closing price over the last 50 sessions, a trend line that smooths daily noise. ATR is the average daily price range, used as a unit of normal movement. Stage 1 is a base, Stage 2 is an uptrend, Stage 3 is a topping pattern, and Stage 4 is a decline.
