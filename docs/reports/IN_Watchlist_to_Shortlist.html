The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph states the rule behind the verdict, the number that triggered it and the level or event that would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier, with five-year tables, a peer comparison, growth scenarios and sources.

Neuland Laboratories: rejected, conditionally

Neuland makes active ingredients and custom-made drug ingredients for other companies, with a return on capital of 26 percent and net cash. Its operating score is 2.5 because the filings read do not document whether the 2025 inspection of its Unit II plant closed with one observation or fewer, so that row is graded WATCH under the dossier's own cut-off. The dossier says this is a documentation gap and not a finding of a bad inspection, and a filing that documents the closure would restore 3.0. The price is 48.6 times trailing profit, a promoter director pledged 96,000 shares that the dossier reads as borderline, and the central value of ₹18,080.85 is under the price.

Ajanta Pharma: rejected, conditionally

Ajanta sells branded medicines in India, Asia and Africa and generics in the United States, with a return on capital of 34.5 percent and almost no debt. The exchange-filed shareholding table shows 20.65 percent of the promoter holding pledged at 30 September 2026, over the 20 percent limit. A news report gives 11.67 percent, but the same article reports a larger total, so the dossier keeps the filed figure. Even with a corrected pledge the name would be only a deferred candidate, because the central value gives 1.47 to 1 at the close against the 2.0 floor. Two consecutive filings under 20 percent are the first of five checks to reopen it for review.

Welspun Corp: deferred candidate, not shortlisted

Welspun makes large pipes for oil, gas and water, with a US order book booked through FY28 and net cash of about ₹2,336 crore. All four red lines are met and the operating score is 3.5. Its quarter to June includes a ₹548 crore one-off gain, and every earnings-based target anchored on the repository's rule sits below the price. The label WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is the dossier's own editorial reading, not the rule-qualified state, which is reached only when six checks pass. A target near ₹2,792 at the zone top would be needed, and that is necessary but not sufficient.

Menon Bearings: deferred candidate, not shortlisted

Menon makes engine bearings and aluminium castings, with a return on capital of 25.5 percent and modest debt. The deferral rests on two judgments: our central earnings of ₹9.41 a share at a 25.52 times median multiple give a value under the price, and a retired September 2024 pledge is read as history. A strict reading that counts past quarters would make it a rejection. Trading is thin, at about ₹0.89 crore a day, so any order would need its own liquidity check. The reopening checks include receivables improving and a filing that shows cash conversion recovering.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. That means some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. Each exception below names what failed, why it cannot be cured now, how it lowers confidence and what would cure it. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS.

Data date. The run took place on Saturday 24 October 2026. The benchmark's 23 October bar was present and every batch name had a 23 October bar, so every price and technical level uses that session. News and results dated after that session are used in no gate or calculation.

Stale periodic filings. The latest periodic filing for each company is the quarter to 30 June 2026, between 71 and 99 days old, so the citation check lists many records as EXCEPTION AS-OF lines. Each dossier lists each group with its age and cure. The cure is to rerun the research once the September-quarter results are filed.

Ledger fields. The ledger has no reverse DCF or consensus revisions for these names, so each dossier states its own recompute beside the ledger's value and labels it a price-implied diagnostic. Several figures rest on secondary readings, such as news summaries of pledge filings, and are labelled where used. The ledger's support anchor for each name is the 23 October session low, not a tested floor, and the builder prints a template label "verified support" that the dossiers disclose or relabel.

Validator mismatch. The shared structural validator has only market-report and portfolio-audit modes. Run on a single-company dossier, it fails a check built for those formats, which asks for case notes for all holdings, and it prints one to three template bridge warnings. The review packet code skips it for dossiers, and no script was modified. The readability check also looks for estimate parts and a Chapter 7 that this summary does not carry, because the dossiers do. The cure is a dossier mode in the validator and a summary mode in the readability check.

Reviewer gate. All four dossiers received REVISE in round 1 from the reviewer. In round 2 the reviewer returned REVISE again for Neuland, Ajanta and Welspun, so those three closed as BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT, with the remaining findings fixed afterwards without a third review. Menon Bearings was approved by both the reviewer and the red team in round 2. The red team approved Neuland, Ajanta and Menon in round 2 and held the verdict for Welspun with findings that were fixed. This summary report was not reviewed separately.

Status changes during review. Welspun and Menon were first written as rejections on the reward-to-risk floor alone. They were recast as deferred candidates because the scoring file's routing rule makes only a Stage 4 breakdown a technical disqualifier, and an earlier dossier in this repository with a central value under the close was deferred. Neuland moved the other way, from a deferral to a conditional rejection, when its Unit II inspection row was graded by its own cut-off.

Judgment calls. The Neuland rejection rests on the regulatory grade and on reading customer concentration on audited figures. The Ajanta rejection rests on the filed pledge reading. The Menon deferral rests on its central value and on a current-encumbrance reading of the pledge rule. If the repository owner confirms a different reading, those labels would change with the same reopening steps.

Source quality. One company agent used a private page cache because the shared cache is off limits, and no agent disabled certificate checks. Pledge disclosures filed with SEBI were not read directly for Ajanta, and the lender details come from news summaries.

Holding labels. The verdict list has no hold-and-wait label, so the routing rule's WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is applied to pipeline names and printed with its plain meaning. The orchestrating agent could not start nested subagents, so the four company agents and the reviewers ran as separate sessions, one per company.
