What the business must prove

Interarch designs and manufactures pre-engineered steel-building systems, then delivers engineering, project coordination, safety and erection work. Its industrial and manufacturing exposure is concentrated, while the July order book offers visibility. Repeat orders as a share of revenue fell from 82% in FY25 to 72% in FY26, so new-project demand and execution remain important. Management has said, “we need to take orders we can deliver on time.” The discipline is sensible; the test is whether it protects margin and collections while capacity expands.
