The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph states the rule behind the verdict, the number that triggered it and the level or event that would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier, with five-year tables, a peer comparison, growth scenarios and sources.

Hindustan Aeronautics: shortlisted candidate, no purchase enabled

Hindustan Aeronautics makes military aircraft, helicopters and engines for the armed forces, with almost no debt and an order book of about ₹2.55 lakh crore. It passes every quality test, but its chart is in Stage 3 (a topping or pullback phase) with no momentum, and the close is 1.49 daily ranges above support. The dossier treats it as a Core Alpha challenger candidate, one slot of ten if admitted, and it displaces nothing. Any limit order (an order that fills at your price or better) of 31 shares near ₹4,691 stays inactive until a held pullback to support and a refreshed written re-underwriting both pass. Its central value rests on a multiple that fails the 2.0 floor if it falls to 30 times, and its operating cash flow leans on a combined liabilities line that could not be split into customer advances. Delivery of the first Tejas Mk1A aircraft by 31 December 2026 is the dated test.

Polycab India: deferred candidate, not shortlisted

Polycab is India's largest cable maker, with a 33.2 percent return on capital and top credit ratings. It is deferred and not shortlisted, because three things stay open together. A tribunal order admitting a ₹2.79 crore insolvency petition is stayed on appeal, and the order itself and the deposit compliance have not been verified from the primary document. The chart fails all three momentum tests. And three-year cash conversion is 1.05 as reported but 0.59 once the build-up in letter-of-credit bills is removed, which misses the compounder standard on that basis. The central value of ₹9,050 uses today's 35.5 times earnings, and at 30 times the reward-to-risk falls to 0.65. No order is enabled.

Balu Forge Industries: sell both shares

Balu Forge makes forgings and defence shells. Its three-year cash conversion is 0.27 against a 0.25 red line, which passes narrowly but fails the 0.50 compounder floor, and its operating score is 2.0 of 5.0 with working capital days (the days of sales tied up in stock and receivables) and cash conversion both failing. The promoter pledge rose from 1.37 to 2.86 percent in a falling stock, which fails the rising-pledge limb. The dossier also records a pending tax block assessment, a property purchase from the managing director and a bond whose terms are not disclosed. The sale follows from the operating score alone. The 5.83 to 1 ratio is flattered by a stop 3.7 percent below the close.

Bliss GVS Pharma: sell the one share

Bliss GVS makes suppositories and pessaries sold mostly in Africa, and a new controlling owner paid ₹299 a share a month earlier, against a close of ₹623.35. The owner's stake is reported as 95.77 percent pledged, against a 20 percent limit, on Trendlyne and Screener readings that the dossier labels secondary because the trustee filing was not fetched. The central value is ₹310, and the dossier shows that a retained takeover premium would give about ₹806, so the sale is a policy decision on the pledge reading and not a finding of distress. The plan is a limit sell at ₹615 on 26 October, then a market sell on 27 October if unfilled.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. That means some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. Each exception below names what failed, why it cannot be cured now, how it lowers confidence and what would cure it. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS.

Data date. The run took place on Saturday 24 October 2026. The benchmark's 23 October bar was present, and the four batch names each had a 23 October bar, so every price and technical level uses that session. About 200 other instruments had stale bars and were not used.

Stale periodic filings. The latest periodic filing for each company is the quarter to 30 June 2026, or for Polycab the quarter to 30 September 2026, and the citation check therefore lists many records as EXCEPTION AS-OF lines, from 32 to 104 per dossier. Each dossier lists each group with its age and cure. The cure is to rerun the research once the September-quarter results are filed.

Ledger fields. The ledger has no reverse DCF or consensus revisions for several of these names, so each dossier states its own recompute beside the ledger's value and explains the gap. Several figures rest on secondary readings, for example the promoter pledge at Bliss GVS and Balu Forge, the tribunal history at Polycab and some Tejas delivery reports at Hindustan Aeronautics. They are labelled where they are used.

Validator mismatch. The shared structural validator has only market-report and portfolio-audit modes. Run on a single-company dossier, it fails a check built for those formats, which asks for case notes for all holdings. The review packet code skips it for dossiers, and no script was modified. The readability check also looks for estimate parts and a Chapter 7 that this summary does not carry, because the dossiers do. The cure is a dossier mode in the validator and a summary mode in the readability check.

Reviewer gate. All four dossiers received REVISE in round 1 and REVISE in round 2 on consistency or sourcing points, which were corrected afterwards without a third review under the two-round cap, so their status is BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT and none received reviewer approval. The red team approved all four in round 2. This summary report was not reviewed separately.

Holding labels. The holding verdict list has no hold-and-wait label, so the routing rule's WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is applied to pipeline names and printed with its plain meaning. The orchestrating agent could not start nested subagents, so the four company agents and the reviewers ran as separate sessions, one per company.

Cache note. The shared page cache keys each entry without the query string, so exchange announcement links that differ only by query string share one entry. This run cited company-hosted documents where possible, and other runs that cite those links may hit the same collision.
