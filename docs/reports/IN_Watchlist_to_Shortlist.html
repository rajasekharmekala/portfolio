Effect on the shortlist and displacement

The shortlist keeps its Core Alpha and Tactical Momentum books, and both are full. Because none of the four names passed the momentum gate, no candidate can take a challenger slot, and no candidate qualifies to replace a current holding. The displacement test therefore has nothing to evaluate, and the shortlist is unchanged except for the rejected records written to the master sheet.

The ROBU holding verdict is recorded against the holding, not as a displacement. A displacement would need a shortlisted challenger that passes the gates, and none does here. The rule is written this way so that a weak holding is not swapped for a name that has not itself earned a place.

What this means for you: nothing in this batch changes what you should buy. The displacement decision is held until a challenger passes the momentum gate, which is the test that protects the book from chasing.
