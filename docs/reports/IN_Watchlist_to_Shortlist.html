Terms used in this report

Watchlist and shortlist. The watchlist is the queue of names waiting for full underwriting. The shortlist is the smaller set of names that have passed every gate and are candidates for buying. A rejected name stays on record with the reason, and it leaves the active queue.

Stage. A simple four-phase description of a stock's trend. Stage 1 is a base, a period of sideways trading after a fall. Stage 2 is a markup, a rising trend. Stage 3 is a topping phase, and Stage 4 is a markdown, a falling trend. Only Stage 1 or Stage 2 can pass the Core technical floor, and only Stage 2 can pass the Tactical floor.

Momentum gate. A code test run on the ledger for every name. It has two routes, Test A (already in an uptrend) and Test B (entering an uptrend). Failing both means no allocation, however good the business is.

Asymmetry. The asymmetry is the upside to a target divided by the downside to the stop. Core names need at least 3.0 and Tactical names at least 3.5. A target that cannot be anchored to a verifiable figure fails this test.

ATR. The ATR is the average true range, the typical daily price swing of a stock. Distances to support and stop levels are measured in multiples of it.

Cash conversion (CFO/PAT). The three-year sum of operating cash flow divided by the three-year sum of reported profit. The floor is 0.70, so a firm must turn at least 70 percent of its profit into cash.

All four names fail the technical floor under the momentum gate, which is the rule that decides the verdict. ELGIEQUIP and NAVINFLUOR have strong financial records, with return on capital employed of 22 and 21 percent respectively. POWERMECH also fails cash conversion at 0.59 against 0.70. None of the four has a Layer 2 or Layer 3 evaluation, because Layer 1 failed first, as the framework requires.

The clearest lesson in this batch is about timing. SHILCTECH and POWERMECH were recorded as buy candidates on 27 September 2026, and their trends have since broken. A good business is not enough: the price must also be in an uptrend that the momentum test can confirm. The dossiers state the price at which each name would be re-tested, and the re-test is a check, not an entry.
