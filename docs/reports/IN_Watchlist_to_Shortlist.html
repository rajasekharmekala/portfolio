The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph states the rule behind the verdict, the number that triggered it and the level or event that would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier, with five-year tables, a peer comparison, growth scenarios and sources.

Coforge: deferred candidate, not shortlisted

Coforge is an IT services firm with banking, insurance and travel clients, a signed order book of about 2.2 billion dollars and AA+ credit. Its second-quarter results came out after the 23 October close, so no price or gate in the dossier reflects them. After the Encora acquisition its return on capital is about 12.8 percent on all the capital employed, so the compounder test passes only on the goodwill-free reading, and the dossier calls the fit conditional. Two board seats and the chair question are open, and the Encora sellers' lock-in length is unpublished. A purchase needs a post-results reconciliation, evidence that the return on capital is recovering toward 15 percent, and the governance and lock-in questions closed from filings. A 78-share limit order (an order that fills at your price or better) at ₹1,863.23 or lower would be the later plan.

Trent: deferred candidate, not shortlisted

Trent runs Westside, Zudio and Star stores, with debt near 0.37 times equity, and its chart is in Stage 1 with no momentum. Its bonus issue distorted the price feed, so the dossier corrected the 52-week high to ₹3,399.70. The business clears every red line, but only just: its operating score is 3.0 of 5.0, cash conversion after leases is 0.73 against a 0.70 line, and cash after leases turned negative in FY26. The central value uses a judgment multiple of 62 times, and the ratio falls under the 2.0 floor above 60.73 times. The plan waits for the September-quarter results, a sustainable-multiple bridge and a held retest (a return to the support zone that holds) of ₹2,546 to ₹2,667.

Solar Industries India: deferred candidate, not shortlisted

Solar Industries makes explosives and defence munitions, with a 38.1 percent return on capital and a ₹21,350 crore order book. It trades at 83 times trailing earnings. Three things stay open together: a debt-funded ₹12,951 crore acquisition of Omnia whose own pro forma leverage could pass 1.20 times equity, a promoter pledge that has risen to 2.95 percent of promoter holding, and a family-trust dispute. The pledge test is judged not triggered because the 200-day average is rising, and the dossier says a stricter reading would reject the name. The plan waits for a filed financing bridge, primary-filing evidence and a retest of ₹17,816 to ₹18,127.

Shilchar Technologies: rejected

Shilchar makes transformers, has no debt and earns a return on capital above 50 percent. Its June quarter fell sharply, with sales down 15 percent, profit down 50 percent and the operating margin (profit before interest and tax over sales) at 16.4 percent against 33.0 percent a year earlier. The close is below falling 50-day and 200-day averages, and the 200-day test holds by only 0.86 percent, which the dossier states openly. The rejection also rests on the weak operating score and the failed entry tests, so it would stand if the thin chart limb lapsed. The cure is a close above about ₹3,944 or the 50-day average, and a margin of at least 20 percent with a stated utilisation of at least 70 percent after the 28 October results.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. That means some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. Each exception below names what failed, why it cannot be cured now, how it lowers confidence and what would cure it. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS.

Data date. The run took place on Saturday 24 October 2026. The benchmark's 23 October bar was present and every batch name had a 23 October bar, so every price and technical level uses that session. Coforge's second-quarter results were approved after that close and are used as an overlay only, in no gate or calculation.

Stale periodic filings. The latest periodic filing for each company is the quarter to 30 June 2026, or for Coforge a deck from the same period, so the citation check lists many records as EXCEPTION AS-OF lines, from 29 to 100 per dossier. Each dossier lists each group with its age and cure. The cure is to rerun the research once the September-quarter results are filed.

Ledger fields. The ledger has no reverse DCF or consensus revisions for these names, so each dossier states its own recompute beside the ledger's value. The ledger's price feed for Trent was distorted by a bonus issue, and the dossier corrected the affected values and marked them inline; the corrected copy of the ledger used for rendering changed no number. The feed for Shilchar Technologies has too few sessions to test the 200-day slope, so the dossier rebuilt the series. Several figures rest on secondary readings, for example promoter pledges and some news items, and are labelled where used.

Validator mismatch. The shared structural validator has only market-report and portfolio-audit modes. Run on a single-company dossier, it fails a check built for those formats, which asks for case notes for all holdings. The review packet code skips it for dossiers, and no script was modified. The readability check also looks for estimate parts and a Chapter 7 that this summary does not carry, because the dossiers do. The cure is a dossier mode in the validator and a summary mode in the readability check.

Reviewer gate. Shilchar Technologies was approved by the reviewer in round 2. Coforge, Trent and Solar Industries received REVISE in round 2 on consistency or sourcing points, which were corrected afterwards without a third review under the two-round cap, so their status is BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. The red team held the verdict for all four. A template label in the Solar Industries table still prints "verified support" for an untested anchor, because the builder hardcodes it, and the dossier says so. This summary report was not reviewed separately.

Holding labels. The verdict list has no hold-and-wait label, so the routing rule's WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is applied to pipeline names and printed with its plain meaning. The orchestrating agent could not start nested subagents, so the four company agents and the reviewers ran as separate sessions, one per company; two sessions timed out and were restarted from their saved files.

Cache note. The shared page cache keys each entry without the query string, so exchange announcement links that differ only by query string share one entry. This run cited company-hosted documents where possible, and other runs that cite those links may hit the same collision.
