1 What this batch did

The watchlist holds names waiting for a full underwriting pass. Each run takes the next four pending names in watchlist order. This run took ASTRAMICRO, EPACKPEB, TANFACIND and NETWEB, the first four pending names in the synced record file. All four are current holdings in the Zerodha account.

Each name was measured against the same hard gates: financial health, technical posture and asymmetry, which is the upside to the target divided by the downside to the stop. A name that fails a gate cannot be bought on the strength of its story, so each outcome depends on the gates and not on the narrative.

The market backdrop matters for how these holdings are read. The ledger regime for the Nifty 50 benchmark is Downtrend, and 19.9% of the 497-stock breadth pool sits above its 50-day average. A downtrend does not change any gate in this batch. It does mean a recovery would have to come from the stocks themselves rather than the market.

The batch result is simple. Every name failed at least one hard gate. Cash conversion was below the 0.70 floor for three names: ASTRAMICRO, TANFACIND and NETWEB. Two of those three are marked for replacement review, and NETWEB is kept on a provisional basis, as chapter 3 explains. All four have moved from the active watchlist to the archive, with a full record of the gate that failed.

Run notes and gate exceptions

Each item listed here is a gate that the evidence could not fully satisfy. Each one is disclosed and none was hidden. The status for the batch is best effort after the review limit.

  • Stale as-of periods. The newest annual and quarterly periods for all four names are more than 60 days old. The newest quarter ends on 30 June 2026, which is 104 days before the report date. The citation check was run with the stale-date allowance, and every stale period is listed in its dossier.
  • Dossier validator. The full report validator covers market-intelligence and portfolio-audit reports only. Dossiers were checked by the citation gate, the readability gate and the review packet, and the layout check runs at publication.
  • Market-report validator. The full market-report validator flags this summary on checks written for market reports. It asks for market-level inputs that a watchlist batch does not use, such as FII and DII flows, option-chain positioning, bulk deals, delivery data, results calendar and promoter pledge. It also asks for a primer, a glossary, a book-length text, case notes and triggers for every holding in the portfolio, and an estimate section. None of these was copied into this summary to satisfy the check. The citation gate (PASS WITH EXCEPTIONS, the disclosed stale periods only), the readability gate and the Research Desk layout check were run in full.
  • Review packet digest. For this dossier format the packet digest is truncated and has no verdict or scenario table. The verdicts were checked directly against each dossier body, and each dossier records this in its own gate-exceptions chapter.
  • Review status. Round 1 was REVISE for all four dossiers. Round 2 gave APPROVE for EPACKPEB and NETWEB on the reviewer pass and REVISE for ASTRAMICRO and TANFACIND. The red team returned APPROVE for ASTRAMICRO and TANFACIND and REVISE for EPACKPEB and NETWEB. Remaining evidence-backed findings were fixed best effort after round 2, and no third round was run. Status: BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT for all four. Verdicts are provisional where marked.
  • Shared cache refresh. Two research agents refreshed cache entries for pages that other runs may also cite, using a forced fetch. Those entries now hold this run's page text.
  • Unverified inputs. ASTRAMICRO's cash figures are aggregator-sourced, and its annual report could not be read. EPACKPEB's live stop is unconfirmed: the formula gives 201.04, while earlier records show 218. TANFACIND's earlier stop of 2,920 is unverified and is not used.
