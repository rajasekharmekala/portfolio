Before you read

This report sums up the third batch of the watchlist to shortlist process for India: four companies you already own, each with its own full research dossier. Three are holds with no additions, and one, Welspun Enterprises, is a sale. The sale rests on a failed cash-conversion test and a target below today's price, and the three holds rest on charts that show no confirmed uptrend and on a gain to the target that is too small for the loss to the stop. Every dossier was revised after review without a clean approval, and the run notes list the checks that could not be fully met.

The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph states the rule behind the verdict, the number that triggered it and the level or event that would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier, with five-year tables, a peer comparison, growth scenarios and sources.

Welspun Enterprises: sell all 190 shares

Welspun Enterprises builds and runs roads, water and tunnelling projects. On its consolidated accounts, operating cash over three years was −₹505.78 crore against ₹1,065.17 crore of profit, a ratio of −0.47 against the 0.25 floor, and the dossier finds no turnaround evidence that clears it. The parent alone shows 0.58, but the debt and the road receivables sit in the subsidiaries, so the dossier treats the consolidated figure as decisive and says that choice is its own. The central value of ₹606.03 is below the ₹666.90 close, so reward-to-risk is negative. The plan sells all shares at the next open, with a close below ₹631.65 as a backstop. A reversal needs audited FY27 accounts that clear the cash line, and a target that gives 2.0 to 1 or better.

Afcom Holdings: hold 120 shares, add none

Afcom leases aircraft for air cargo. Its three-year cash conversion of 0.12 fails the 0.25 line on audited figures, and the dossier keeps the hold only as its own judgment that a cash turnaround is under way. The evidence is a positive FY26 operating cash flow and one limited-review quarter, set against receivables that grew by ₹76.73 crore. The exit test is a rolling ratio over FY25, FY26 and the half-year to September 2026. It needs half-year operating cash of about ₹68.5 crore on an assumed ₹79.24 crore profit, which is stricter than a true three-year window. The stop is ₹1,319.60, and any add is a single limit order (an order that fills at your price or better) of 111 shares at ₹1,354.00 under tight conditions.

TD Power Systems: hold 245 shares, add none

TD Power Systems makes generators and motors for gas turbines, engines and data-centre power. It passes the red lines, with debt close to nil and three-year cash conversion of 0.475, which misses the 0.50 relief line for a compounder. The ₹721.68 target uses two analysts' profit estimate. On our own estimate, reward-to-risk is 0.14 at the close, so no buying zone exists. The holding is 3.83 percent against a 3.5 percent ceiling for a new position, and the plan is to sell all shares at the next open after a close at or below ₹642.93. Pages show about ₹640 for 23 October, which we could not verify, so check the official close before acting.

Apar Industries: hold 7 shares, add none

Apar Industries makes conductors, specialty cables and transformer oils, and it passes every red line. The trailing profit includes an estimated ₹176 crore oil inventory gain, and the central value of ₹14,707.04 sits at the price, so there is no reward to measure. Results come on 29 October 2026, and the dossier sets a clean-profit test for them: below about ₹251 crore sells all shares, and below about ₹291 crore blocks any add. The stop is ₹12,911.43, set at the 63-day base low (the flat floor built before a new move) less half an average daily range because the literal formula sat inside one day's range. A limit add of up to 4 shares needs filed results, a support hold and a passing momentum test.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. That means some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. Each exception below names what failed, why it cannot be cured now, how it lowers confidence and what would cure it. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS.

Data delay. The price feed had no 23 October 2026 bar for the Nifty 50 after retries. All prices and technical levels therefore use the 22 October session. Some pages show 23 October prints, and the dossiers label those unverified and use none of them in a calculation.

Stale periodic filings. The latest periodic filing for each company is the quarter to 30 June 2026, and no September-quarter results exist yet. The citation check therefore lists many records as EXCEPTION AS-OF lines, 118 for Welspun Enterprises and 98 for Apar Industries, with grouped lists for the other two. The matching dossier lists each group with its age and cure. The cure is to rerun the research once the September-quarter results are filed.

Ledger fields. For all four companies, the ledger's market value or reverse-DCF fields follow a price or share count other than the 22 October close and the filed share count. Each dossier states its own figures beside the ledger's and explains the gap in the primer and at the chapter 5 table. The cure is to rebuild the ledger fundamentals at the verified close.

Validator mismatch. The shared structural validator has only market-report and portfolio-audit modes. Run on a single-company dossier, it fails checks built for those formats, such as case notes for all 19 holdings. The review packet code skips it for dossiers, and no script was modified. The readability check also looks for estimate parts and a Chapter 7 that this summary does not carry, because the dossiers do. The cure is a dossier mode in the validator and a summary mode in the readability check.

Reviewer gate. All four dossiers received REVISE in round 1 and REVISE in round 2, and their remaining findings were corrected afterwards without a third review, under the two-round cap. Their status is BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT and none received reviewer approval. The red team held the verdict for all four, and the stricter purchase gate it asked for at TD Power Systems was applied. This summary report was not reviewed separately.

Holding labels. The holding verdict list has no hold-and-wait label, so the routing rule's WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is applied to current holdings and printed with its plain meaning. Afcom holds on a failed red line only through the writer's own judgment, which its dossier states in every section. The orchestrating agent could not start nested subagents, so the four company agents and the reviewers ran as separate sessions, one per company.

Cache note. The shared page cache keys each entry without the query string, so exchange announcement links that differ only by query string share one entry. This run cited company-hosted documents instead, and other runs that cite those links may hit the same collision.
