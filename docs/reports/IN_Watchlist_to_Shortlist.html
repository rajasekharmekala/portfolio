BLISSGVS: Bliss GVS Pharma

REPLACE_UNDERPERFORMER Sell the one share (₹652) and add nothing. A promoter pledge is a promise of shares to a lender if a loan is not repaid. Our India red line allows at most 20% of the promoters' holding to be pledged, and on 30 September 2026 Catalyst Trusteeship, acting for debenture holders, took a pledge on 5,09,84,595 shares, which is 47.95% of the voting capital and 95.8% of the 5,32,36,264 shares held by the new promoters, Anupam Rasayan and Mates Visa Consultancy (50.07% after 28 September). The business itself is clean on the other red lines (debt to equity 0.018, interest cover 17 times, three-year cash flow to profit 1.13, Stage 2 chart), but Layer 2 scores 2 of 5 because customers take 204 days to pay, and the price of ₹652 is 2.18 times the ₹299 the new owner paid for its block. Our own base value of ₹613.35 (estimated FY27 profit per share of ₹14.50 times the 42.3 times multiple the stock has traded at since the deal) is below the price, so reward to risk is negative, and the 5.28 to 1 from a measured move holds only after a close above ₹739.90. The verdict reverses only if the pledge falls below 20% (or filings prove it secures only the ₹300 crore of debentures, which would be evidence for a deliberate exception, not a clearance), the scorecard reaches 4 of 5, and the price is near ₹512 or the base value exceeds ₹800. A close below ₹615.33 confirms the exit.

HAL: Hindustan Aeronautics

REJECTED HAL is a debt-free, AAA-rated defence maker with an order book of ₹2,54,538 crore (7.7 times sales) and a Stage 2 chart, and every red line and return floor passes (return on capital 32%, three-year cash flow to profit 1.31, no pledge). It fails for two independent reasons. Reward to risk is 1.48 to 1 against the core floor of 3.0: the target of ₹5,976 is next year's consensus profit per share (₹172.72) times the three-year median multiple of 34.6, and the stop is ₹4,321.75. Layer 2 scores 2 of 5, because no Tejas Mk1A fighter had been handed over as of the 3 October report (35 were scheduled by March 2026) and only 14 of 99 GE engines had arrived. FY26 cash flow was also lifted by a ₹21,717 crore rise in customer advances, and 31% of pre-tax profit was other income. A lower price alone does not change the verdict: it needs documented accepted jet deliveries and engine receipts, a 30% operating margin in the Q2 and Q3 FY27 results, a refreshed target and stop, and a price in the entry zone (₹4,550 to ₹4,735 on today's inputs, where a 28 times multiple would cut the payoff to 0.24 to 1).

POLYCAB: Polycab India

REJECTED Polycab is a market leader in cables and wires (return on capital 33.2%, AAA-rated), and on paper its reward to risk is 4.80 to 1 (target ₹11,201 is FY27 profit per share of ₹233.85 times a 47.9 times median multiple; stop ₹7,597.59). The rejection rests on two rules. The chart floor fails: a core name needs a Stage 2 uptrend or a base breakout, and the ₹8,219 close sits below its 20-day, 50-day and 200-day averages in a Stage 1 base. And Layer 2 scores 3 of 5 against the 4 needed, with volume growth in single digits while rupee sales rose 53% on copper prices, and the cash picture relying on ₹6,556 crore of letter-of-credit supplier credit that management says will normalise (reported debt to equity 0.01, but 0.51 counting it; three-year cash flow to profit 1.05 as audited and 0.59 without the change in that credit). The 4.80 depends on the 47.9 times multiple: at today's 39.2 times it is 1.53, and at the Street mean target of ₹9,906 it is 2.71. A move to WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT needs a weekly close above the falling 50-day average (₹8,624) on volume, December-quarter volume growth of about 18% (1.5 times a market growing about 12%) with a wires-and-cables margin above 13%, and a full five-measure scorecard rerun showing 4 of 5.

COFORGE: Coforge

REJECTED Coforge is a fast-growing software services firm (12-month executable order book of US$2,228 million, free cash flow 95% of profit, red lines all passing) that bought the US firm Encora in April 2026. It is rejected on reward to risk: ₹58.72 of FY27 consensus profit per share times the three-year median multiple of 52.6 gives a ₹3,088.67 target, and against the ₹1,467.12 stop (the lowest of the swing low and the 50-day and 200-day averages, less one average daily range) the ratio is 2.34 to 1 against the 3.0 floor. Layer 2 is met at 4 of 5, but the price is 1.65 ranges above its 50-day average, which is the no-buy band. Material flags: organic dollar growth without Encora was about 10.6% against the 33% headline; return on capital falls from 23.5% in FY26 to about 12% on the June 2026 balance sheet; the independent chairman and the nomination committee chair resigned in September after an internal audit finding; and the Encora sellers hold 21.83% of the shares, indirectly pledged to their lenders, with a six-month lock-up that appears to end around 23 October. The verdict changes on a close at or below ₹1,872.51 after the 23 October results with no further governance break, and a lower price repairs only the price test, not the capital-return floor.

What this means for you: one action, a token sale of one Bliss GVS share worth ₹652 (0.01% of your account). The other three names need no order today. Coforge is the only one with a defined price and date at which a re-underwriting is worth running (a close at or below ₹1,872.51 after 23 October), while HAL and Polycab each need a business or chart change first.
