Jindal SAW: deferred candidate, not shortlisted

Jindal SAW makes welded, ductile iron and seamless pipe, with a confirmed order book of about US$1.135 billion and an AA credit rating. Its results on 16 October showed an operating margin (profit from running the business as a share of sales) of 6.9% against 11.3% a year earlier, and profit to owners fell 69%. The share fell 23% from the 16 October close, and the record cannot explain all of it. No red line is breached on the company's own interest cover formula, but cover on profit before interest and tax is 1.67 for the half year against the 2.0 limit, so a stricter reading would reject it. The dossier lists every reading and the status each gives. Our central value of ₹172.79 is under the close, and the price needs a margin near 15.5% in FY28 to be justified.

Mahindra Holidays and Resorts: rejected

Mahindra Holidays sells holiday memberships and runs resorts. The chart fails the Stage 4 test: the close of ₹185.47 is below a falling 50-day average, which is below a falling 200-day average. That test alone decides the verdict. Borrowings are also 1.68 times equity on the plain reading against the 1.20 limit, but counting debt net of cash gives 0.58 and clears it, and the rule text does not choose between them. Return on capital is 8.12% against the 15% standard, and cash after capital spending, leases and interest was negative in FY26. Reopening needs the chart to clear first and a debt reading to be settled.

Repro India: rejected

Repro India is a small print-on-demand and book distribution company, with a market value of about ₹410 crore and thin trading. The chart fails the Stage 4 test, and the operating test scores 1.5 of 5.0, under the 3.0 minimum. The dossier's financial gates use filings available before the session; the September results, whose release time was not established, sit in a labelled overlay. Interest cover on the company's own formula and three-year cash over profit are shown on every reading, and the verdict does not rest on them. The central value of ₹209.43 is under the close.

Websol Energy System: rejected

Websol makes solar cells and panels, with net cash, debt to equity of 0.19 and three-year cash over profit of 1.36. The chart fails the Stage 4 test, which is the deciding breach, and the dossier found no real alternative reading. The pledge on the latest filing is 15.64% of the promoter holding, against 89.39% at 30 June, and the dossier infers that a loan repayment released it. The paper reward-to-risk clears the floor on the median multiple, but on the scoring file's current-multiple wording the target of about ₹58 is under the close. The margin fell from 47.3% to 33.7% in a year.

What this means for you is no purchase today. The next filings that could change these views are the December-quarter results, and no result date is confirmed in these dossiers.

Validator mismatch. The shared structural validator has only market-report and portfolio-audit modes. The review packet code skips it for dossiers, and no script was modified. The readability check also looks for estimate parts and a Chapter 7 that this summary does not carry, because the dossiers do.
