IGIL operating case and walk-the-talk check

IGI earns fees for independent reports on natural diamonds, laboratory-grown diamonds, finished jewelry and colored stones. Its advantage rests on trade trust, trained graders and a network near manufacturers and retailers, not exclusive rights. The FY2025-26 annual report lists 36 laboratories in 10 countries and 21 schools, but that report ends March 31 and spans 15 months. It cannot establish current utilization. Dealer concentration is disclosed: the top ten made up 46.46% of dealer and distributor sales. At H1 FY27, operating cash flow was ₹4,157 million, or 89% of EBITDA, helped by a working-capital release. The Screener snapshot also flags working-capital days rising from −103 to 113; the periods and calculation basis are not reconciled, so the headline conversion ratio does not establish normalized cash conversion. [9] Management's initial FY27 guide was 15% revenue growth and 20% EBITDA growth; H1 delivered +27% and +34%. The CFO expects continued outperformance but gave no replacement numeric guide. That is evidence of execution ahead of the original pace, not a full-year forecast. WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is triggered by RSI 77.37 and 13.98% extension above the 10-week EMA. Reconsider only within ₹353.72–₹369.73 after the trend and next filing hold; two weekly closes below ₹327.65 invalidate the plan. [2][3]

The incoming watchlist used “Diamond Testing Monopoly.” The evidence does not support that wording. GIA, HRD Antwerp, GCAL and SGL publish competing certification services, and IGI itself identifies retailer self-certification as a risk. We retain CORE_ALPHA monitoring because report volumes, returns and Stage 2 momentum are strong, but qualify the moat as brand, access and process consistency. No customer-retention series or independent accuracy comparison appears in the reviewed sources. [4][13][14][15]

Management's walk-the-talk score is positive but incomplete. H1 sales and EBITDA growth exceeded the initial annual growth guide. The CFO's later language is directional rather than a replacement forecast, and India standalone EBITDA margin fell 340 bps as leadership, marketing and commission costs increased. Track whether those costs create repeat business; the full-year result will resolve the open question.
