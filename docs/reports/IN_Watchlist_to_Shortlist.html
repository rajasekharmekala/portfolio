Before you read

This report sums up the twenty-fourth batch of the watchlist to shortlist process for India, with four companies and a full research dossier for each. Hitachi Energy India (POWERINDIA) and GE Vernova T&D India (GVT&D) are strong power-grid equipment makers whose prices sit well above support. The core shortlist book is already full, so each is qualified but deferred and neither is shortlisted today. CIE Automotive India (CIEINDIA) is a sound business in a downtrend, and Bharat Forge (BHARATFORG) earns too little on its capital for its price, so both are rejected and archived. Nothing here is a buy today, and the calls are wrong if the September-quarter results, due between 28 October and 14 November, change the facts.

Hitachi Energy India: qualified but deferred, no order

Hitachi Energy India builds transformers, switchgear and high-voltage direct-current converter systems for the power grid. No hard red line fails. Debt to equity is 0.02 and the promoter pledge is nil. Three-year cash from operations is 1.95 times profit. The operating test scores 4.0 of 5.0 with no failing row. The order backlog of ₹32,222 crore is about 3.5 times a year of sales, but about two-thirds of it sits in a few large HVDC projects.

The problem is price. At about 110 times trailing profit, the reverse DCF needs about 29.6 percent profit growth a year for ten years. A reverse DCF solves for the growth that the current price already assumes. The chart is in a Stage 1 base, a flat stretch after a decline, and sits 2.7 times its typical daily range above support. So the verdict is WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT. The plan is a limit order, an order that fills only at a set price or better, in the zone of ₹25,420 to ₹26,700. The stop is ₹24,318.58 and the planned size is 3.5 percent of net worth. The call fails if the market multiple falls below about 62.5 times on unchanged profit estimates. It also fails if the September-quarter operating margin, which is profit before interest and tax as a share of sales, comes in under 15 percent. Those results are expected about 6 November, with a deadline of 14 November. [1]

GE Vernova T&D India: qualified but deferred, no order before 30 October

GE Vernova T&D India makes the same kind of grid equipment and is debt free. Its return on capital is 77.4 percent and its cash from operations is 1.55 times profit over three years. The operating test scores 4.0 of 5.0. But new orders fell 30 percent in the June quarter and its margin slipped. The price already assumes about 24.7 percent profit growth a year for ten years. Reward-to-risk is only 0.78 at the close and 2.13 at ₹3,720.

The dossier sets a clear order of events. There is no order before the results on 30 October 2026. A limit order in the ₹3,533 to ₹3,720 zone follows only if the operating margin is at least 23 percent. The verdict is sensitive to one judgement. At a 50 times target multiple instead of 55, the floor fails at the zone top. [2]

Bharat Forge: rejected and archived

Bharat Forge is India's largest auto-component forger and is growing in defence and aerospace. It passes every red line. It passes the operating test narrowly, at 3.0 of 5.0, with one failing row because its overseas plants earn almost no margin. It is rejected on archetype fit. Return on capital is 12.6 percent against the 15 percent standard. None of four trend signals is positive. The price to earnings is 85.9 against a three-year median of 67.0. The chart fails all three momentum tests.

Reward-to-risk of 2.01 at the close is real but fragile, and it is not the deciding test. The dossier says what would reopen the case after the 5 November results. Parent margin must be at least 27 percent, group margin at least 17.5 percent and profit before exceptional items at least ₹520 crore. The dossier also flags that the user's options ticket on this name, a bull call spread expiring 29 October 2026, carries a stale underlying price. It should be looked at in the options workflow. [3]

CIE Automotive India: rejected on the chart and archived

CIE Automotive India makes forgings and castings for vehicles and turns profit into cash well. Debt to equity is 0.07, cash from operations is 1.27 times profit over three years, the pledge is nil and the company holds net cash. It scores 3.5 of 5.0 on the operating test with one failing row, European sales. It qualifies as a turnaround value case at 15.0 times earnings.

It is rejected only because of the chart. The stock is in a literal Stage 4 downtrend, and the close is 2.45 times its typical daily range above support. Consensus estimates are being cut and India's operating margin has fallen for two quarters. The cure is specific. It is a close at or above the 20-day average of about ₹373.82 with positive relative strength or a positive two-week change. A margin recovery in the results due about February 2027 would also count. Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. That means some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS, and the BHARATFORG dossier is also BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT.

Data date. The run took place on Tuesday 27 October 2026 (Pacific time). The Nifty 50 final bar for 27 October is verified and the ledger was built for that session. The first ledger build lost price data for many names because the price vendor rate-limited requests from several concurrent runs, including BHARATFORG. The ledger was rebuilt once the vendor recovered, with the four batch names included, and every price in this batch comes from that rebuilt ledger. No bar was relabelled.

Stale periodic filings. The citation check was run with the option that allows old as-of dates, because the latest periodic filing for each name is the June 2026 quarter (or, for CIE Automotive India, the half-year to June 2026 and the calendar 2025 annual report) and the September-quarter results are not yet filed. The checker listed about 24 stale records for CIE Automotive India, 61 for GE Vernova T&D India, 66 for Bharat Forge and 65 for Hitachi Energy India (the count can differ by one between the checker and a dossier's own text). Each dossier groups them with ages and cures, and no as-of date was edited. The cure is the September 2026 results.

Source access. Several sources were blocked or unreadable: Business Standard, Moneycontrol and PIB pages returned HTTP 403, the Screener consolidated page for GE Vernova T&D was empty, Screener pages for several peers were rate-limited, and the exchange PDFs had to be text-extracted into the cache by hand. Where a figure was unavailable the dossier shows N/A or uses a named aggregator. The three-year median price to earnings is not published for Hitachi Energy India or GE Vernova T&D India, so each dossier states the substitute multiple it used and how the verdict changes without it.

Ledger fields. For GE Vernova T&D India the ledger carried no consensus EPS (earnings per share) (profit per share) for the next year, which the dossier discloses. For Bharat Forge the ledger reverse DCF rests on a profit figure derived from market value and trailing price to earnings, so the dossier re-solves it on filed profit and cash flow and shows both.

Template and tooling. The shared dossier builder printed momentum-gate rule wording that did not match the scoring file, and the reviewers flagged it in round 1 for every dossier. The builder now reads those thresholds from the scoring file, prints readable measured values and can show the report status on the first screen; this repository change is part of the publication. The shared glossary pass still skips terms placed directly inside a paragraph, so the writers wrapped first uses by hand in their specifications. The fetch cache drops query strings from addresses, so several exchange-filing links share one cache key; the dossiers cite the unique file paths instead.

Reviewer gate. The reviewer and the red team ran as separate agents with the configured models. The reviewer asked for changes in round 1 for all four dossiers and approved POWERINDIA, GVT&D and CIEINDIA in round 2. The red team held the verdict for all four in round 1; for CIEINDIA it asked for two wording changes, which were applied, and approved in round 2. For BHARATFORG the round 2 reviewer left one wrong figure (a percentage gap on the options ticket) and three notes. The writer corrected the figure afterwards without a third review, so BHARATFORG closes as BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. A status line was added to every dossier after the final review and changes no figure or conclusion. This summary report was not reviewed separately.
