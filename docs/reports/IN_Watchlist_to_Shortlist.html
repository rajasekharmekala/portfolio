SAGILITY: Sagility

WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT Do not buy at ₹45.30 and rest no order of any kind before the 22 October 2026 results on the September quarter. The binding rule is the reward-to-risk floor: with a base target of ₹56.70 and a stop of ₹41.48 the ratio is 2.98 at the close and reaches 3.5 only at ₹44.86 or lower. All five India red lines pass (three-year cash flow to profit 2.00, debt to equity 0.06, interest cover 13.5 times, no pledge of Sagility shares, Stage 2), but reported return on capital is 13.4% because 67% of assets are goodwill from the carve-out, and the three leading clients are about 60% of revenue. After the results, a limit order in ₹44.28 to ₹44.80 is considered only if the adjusted EBITDA margin is at least 24% and organic growth at least 10%, at 3.5% of your account in two halves. A September margin of 23% to 24% means wait and re-run. The verdict turns to REJECTED on a close below ₹41.48, a margin under 23% or organic growth under 10%, an adverse unfunded ruling in the Texas case, or a lapse of the US$45 million bank guarantee on 31 January 2027 without extension. The probability-weighted value is ₹52.66, against a price of ₹45.30.

AIIL: Authum Investment and Infrastructure

REJECTED Authum is an investment holding company, so debt to equity is waived and the dossier judges it on its own measures. The red lines pass (three-year cash conversion 0.49 against the 0.40 line, interest cover 11.6 times, no pledge, Stage 2), but the static floors fail: return on capital is 13.1% against 15%, cash conversion is 0.49 against 0.70, the 200-day average is still falling and reward to risk is 1.27 against 3.5. Earnings are mostly paper: FY26 profit after tax was ₹1,929 crore while total comprehensive income was a loss of ₹55.77 crore, and profit booked on actual sales was about 8% of profit. The shares trade at 3.3 times book value, against 1.7 times for Bajaj Holdings. Only 1 of 5 operating measures passes. The market-intelligence seed's 6.1 to 1 ratio used a stop above the swing low and does not survive the tactical stop rule. Re-underwrite after the September-quarter results due by 14 November 2026, if return on capital reaches 15%, realised profit is at least half of reported profit and fresh-book bad loans stay under 3%.

SHRIPISTON: SPR Auto Technologies

REJECTED The company is the former Shriram Pistons and Rings, renamed on 2 April 2026 (the watchlist name is correct). No red line is crossed: three-year cash conversion is 1.02, debt to equity 0.62, no pledge, Stage 2. It is rejected because Layer 2 scores 3 of 5 (4 needed), reward to risk is 1.99 against 3.5, and the price is 36.8 times earnings against a three-year median of 21.1. The 53% revenue growth is largely bought: three Antolin India entities were acquired on 8 January 2026, and management says growth without them was about 16%. The 2.2-point fall in promoter holding is dilution from the ₹1,000 crore QIP, not selling. A buy needs two steps: the Q2 results (board 30 October, call 2 November) must show a standalone EBITDA margin of at least 22.5% and growth at or above the industry, which would move the verdict to WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT, and an archetype must then qualify with profit growth above the first quarter's 9.4% and a reward to risk of at least 3.5 before any limit order in ₹4,380 to ₹4,466.

BALUFORGE: Balu Forge Industries

REPLACE_UNDERPERFORMER Sell the two shares (₹961.40) and do not build the position. The cash red line fails: three-year cash from operations is ₹147.97 crore on ₹556.3 crore of profit, a ratio of 0.27 against the 0.40 red line, and about ₹805 crore of share issues paid for ₹904 crore of capital spending. The auditor flagged ₹339.08 crore of trade receivables and ₹24.41 crore of cheques recorded but not deposited, an income-tax search of January 2026 has no written outcome, and FY26 growth was 19.9% against guidance of 40% to 45%. Layer 2 scores 2 of 5, reward to risk is 2.55 and the momentum gate returns NO_MOMENTUM. The position is too small to matter either way, so the decision is about not adding. A close below ₹460.95 invalidates the tactical setup. The review reopens only if the half-year cash flow with the Q2 results shows operating cash of at least ₹60 crore for the six months and standalone receivables under ₹300 crore; that reassesses the cash red line and does not by itself change the verdict. Our own check found no split or bonus, but the ledger's 52-week high of ₹678 sits just outside the true window (the true high is ₹667) and its reverse-DCF base is 8.8% above audited profit; the dossier shows both corrections.

What this means for you: one action, a token sale of Balu Forge worth ₹961 (0.02% of your account). Run notes and gate exceptions

Status: PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS. Four exceptions apply, and each dossier carries its own run-notes section with the detail. First, the latest periodic filing for every name is the quarter ended 30 June 2026, which is more than 60 days before the 16 October session, and no newer filing existed. The citation check was run with its stale-date allowance and printed PASS WITH EXCEPTIONS for all four (stale records: SAGILITY 134 of 151, AIIL 104 of 113, SHRIPISTON 115 of 162, BALUFORGE 67 of 91). Every as-of date is true and none was edited. The cure is the September-quarter results: Sagility on 22 October, SPR Auto on 30 October, Balu Forge and Authum by mid-November. Second, the shared validator accepts only market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, and the readability checker expects the fixed chapters of a company dossier (estimates, a chapter 7 and numbered notes), so neither fits a batch summary; the four dossiers themselves passed the readability check, and the citation check and the layout check were run on this summary as well as on the dossiers. Third, sources that could not be read: the Balu Forge FY26 annual report (HTTP 403), several concall transcripts, ICRA rationales behind a login wall, and the Zerodha charges page (HTTP 429); nothing was filled from memory, and each dossier says what is missing. Fourth, the shared fetch cache strips query strings, so several BSE announcement links collapse to one cache key; the writers cited the direct file addresses instead. The ledger was rebuilt once for the same 2026-10-16 session with the three non-held batch names added as extras so that their reverse-DCF and consensus blocks exist; the ledger was not edited otherwise. Confidence is lower on any figure that depends on a stale filing, and every decision is written so that it changes on a stated price or on the next results.
