Before you read

This report sums up a batch of four India companies, each with a full research dossier. Ipca Laboratories (IPCALAB), a drug maker, passes every safety rule and joins the shortlist, but only as an order that waits for its 5 November results. Alkem, Garden Reach Shipbuilders (GRSE) and Lincoln Pharmaceuticals are rejected: Alkem and GRSE are in long downtrends with too little reward for the risk, and Lincoln scores below the operating-test line. Nothing here is a buy today. Each call is wrong if the next results change the facts behind it.

What the batch found

Each row gives the verdict, the 27 October close, the stop, our central target and the reward-to-risk ratio, which is the gain to the target divided by the loss to the stop. A stop is the price at which the idea is wrong. The scoring file asks for at least 2.0 for a core name. Read the ratio together with the status column, because a good ratio on paper does not beat a failed rule or a chart that has not turned.

Three rules decided these outcomes. The first is a set of hard red lines covering debt, share pledge, cash conversion and a chart test called Stage 4. In Stage 4 the close sits below a falling 50-day average, which sits below a falling 200-day average. GRSE meets that test and also fails the cash-conversion line (three-year cash over profit of -0.60 against a 0.25 floor). Alkem meets the chart test with its recovery test failing. Lincoln does not meet it as written, because its 200-day average is rising.

The second rule is the operating test, scored out of 5.0. A name needs at least 3.0 and may have one failing row. IPCALAB scores 4.0 with no failing row, Alkem 3.0 with one, GRSE 2.5 and Lincoln 2.5. The third rule is the reward-to-risk floor, which only IPCALAB meets, and only at the top of its buy zone.

The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph gives the rule behind the verdict, the number that triggered it and what would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier.

Ipca Laboratories: shortlisted core challenger, conditional order

Ipca makes branded medicines for India and generics and ingredients for export. No hard red line fails: debt to equity is 0.10, the promoter pledge is nil, three-year cash over profit is 1.37 and the chart is Stage 2 (a rising trend) with the momentum gate passing. Layer 2 scores 4.0 of 5.0 and group operating margin (profit from operations as a share of sales) has risen from 15 percent to 22 percent in three years. The catch is price. The close of ₹1,980.80 gives only 1.58 to 1 reward-to-risk, and the 2.0 floor holds only at ₹1,945.90 or lower on today's inputs, and only while the share trades at about 36 times yearly profit or more. So the verdict is ACCUMULATE_ON_DIP by limit order (an order that fills only at a chosen price or better), with no order before the 5 November 2026 results, then a refreshed value, stop and highest entry price. The red team called the first verdict too soft; the written answer was to make the order conditional on that refresh, and the final red-team round approved.

Alkem Laboratories: rejected

Alkem is a cash-rich Indian branded-generics maker with a return on capital employed of 21 percent. It is rejected on the chart and on reward-to-risk: the close of ₹5,078 sits below falling 50-day and 200-day averages, the recovery test fails, and reward-to-risk is 0.80 to 1. Two open risks add to this: a forensic audit approved on 21 October 2026 with no stated amount, and a US regulator rating on its Daman plant that supplies about 45 percent of its US sales. The idea reopens only if a fresh run passes all four recovery conditions, the audit proves immaterial and the price falls to ₹4,917 or lower.

Garden Reach Shipbuilders: rejected

GRSE builds warships for the Indian Navy and has an excellent delivery record, no debt and the top credit rating. It fails two hard red lines: three-year cash over profit is -0.60 because customer advances are being used up, and the chart is Stage 4 with the recovery test failing. The order book has fallen from ₹22,680 crore to ₹13,596 crore, and our central value of ₹1,630 is below the close. The case depends on one unsigned corvette contract of about ₹33,000 crore. The first test is the half-year cash flow statement on 4 November 2026.

Lincoln Pharmaceuticals: rejected

Lincoln is a small, debt-free exporter at 11.4 times trailing profit, which looks cheap until the profit is cleaned of about ₹42 crore a year of other income, which puts it at 13.2 times and about level with its own history. It scores 2.5 on the operating test, no archetype fits, and reward-to-risk is 0.14 to 1 at the close. This is not a chart rejection, because the 200-day average is still rising. It reopens only if core operating margin reaches about 17 percent, and even then a fresh valuation, stop and entry test is required.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. Some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS, and IPCALAB and ALKEM are also BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT.

Stale periodic filings. The citation check was run with the option that allows old as-of dates, because the latest periodic filing for each name is the June 2026 quarter or the FY26 annual report. The checker listed 57 stale records for IPCALAB, 46 for ALKEM, 39 for GRSE and 49 for LINCOLN. Each dossier groups them with ages and cures, and no as-of date was edited. The cure is the next results: Lincoln on 29 October, GRSE on 4 November, IPCALAB on 5 November and Alkem around 13 November 2026.

Review limit. All four reviewers returned REVISE in round 1. In round 2 GRSE and LINCOLN were approved. IPCALAB and ALKEM were not: each had one open consistency finding that the writer fixed after the last review and that was not re-reviewed, so both are best-effort. The red team approved all four in round 2, after asking for a stricter verdict on IPCALAB in round 1.

Structural validator. The shared validator script accepts only the market-report and portfolio-audit kinds, so a single-company dossier cannot print its pass line. The readability and citation checks passed and the modern-layout check passed for all four dossiers. The cure is a dossier mode in the script.

Template wording. The shared dossier builder prints fixed rule labels in the momentum-gate table and a stop-rule sentence that differ from the limits actually applied. Each dossier states the applied limits in nearby prose, and no repository script was modified. The cure is to update the builder.

Run recovery. Midway through the run a parallel session removed this run's worktree, which held the rendered dossiers and the fetch cache. The dossiers were re-rendered deterministically from their saved specs, and the cached source pages were restored from the original saved text or by re-fetching the live pages. The reviewed text was unchanged apart from the generation timestamp. This summary report was not reviewed separately.

Judgment calls. IPCALAB clears its core floor only at the zone top and only while the share keeps a multiple of about 36 times profit, so a small cut to our estimate fails it. Displacing DIVISLAB rests on the Layer 2 score being the first test in the displacement order. Email: not sent.
