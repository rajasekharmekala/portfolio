Evidence, delivery and main risks

The latest Q1 FY27 call reported 20,000 TPA of planned HFC-32 capacity, about 65% under long-term arrangements, and ₹315 crore committed against a stated ₹395 crore project cost. The FY26 annual report and January approval use about ₹495 crore for the broader project scope. The company has not reconciled the two figures in the evidence available at cutoff. The diligence treats HFC-32 as an option until commissioning, saleable production and margin are reported. [1] [4]

The walk-the-talk check is mixed. The February 2023 HF expansion notice gave a 15–18 month timeline; the company announced the expanded capacity in October 2024, about two months past the upper bound, and reported 95% utilization in FY26. Both solar DHF phases were commissioned within FY26 and the annual report recorded orders of ₹1,068 crore through FY29. The next evidence point is Q2, whose call was scheduled for 3 November after the market cutoff. [6] [7] [8] [9]

The main risks are the premium multiple, a delay or cost overrun at HFC-32, weak early utilization, sulphur and fluorspar price swings, pass-through lags, customer concentration that is not fully disclosed, and long-run refrigerant substitution. The first catalyst is operational proof, not the announced total contract value. [3] [10] [11]
