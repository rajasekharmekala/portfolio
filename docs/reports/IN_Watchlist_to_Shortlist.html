Business quality and operating evidence

Zydus enters the quarter with scale and a broad product engine. Its FY26 annual report lists 44 manufacturing facilities, 10 research centres, more than 1,500 scientists, and ₹22.732bn of R&D investment, or 8.4% of revenue. The company reported more than 55% of FY26 revenue from branded pharma, wellness, and MedTech businesses. In India, Q1 FY27 branded formulations grew 20% year over year; chronic and sub-chronic products represented 54.2% of the India business on AWACS MAT June 2026. These are useful indicators of repeat demand and a shift toward brands, though product-level margins and utilization are not published in the materials reviewed.[1][7]

Growth also came from outside India: international formulations generated ₹9.7bn, up 34% year over year. North America revenue was ₹31bn, up 5% quarter over quarter, with five ANDAs filed, nine approvals including four tentative approvals, and 11 launches. Zydus launched NUFYMCO, its first U.S. biosimilar, and completed the Assertio acquisition during Q1. Consumer Wellness delivered ₹14.3bn, up 67%, with domestic growth of 5% and international like-for-like growth of 25%; MedTech generated ₹2.8bn. Launch count and top-line growth are not the same as return on capital. The acquisition cost burden is already visible, so investors should test margins and cash generation alongside the commercial activity.[7][25][26]
