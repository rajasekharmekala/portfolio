Before you read

This report sums up the twenty-second batch of the India watchlist process, with a full research dossier for each of four companies. CG Power, Divi's Laboratories and R R Kabel pass every safety rule and the operating test, but each trades above its support level, so all three join the shortlist as challengers with no order today. Waaree Renewable Technologies is rejected because its chart is in a long decline, its operating test fails and our central value is below the market price. Nothing here is a buy today. Each call is wrong if the September-quarter results change the facts behind it.

What the batch found

In Stage 4 the price has fallen for a long time. The close sits below a falling 50-day average, and that average sits below a falling 200-day average. Only Waaree Renewable Technologies meets that test. The second rule is the operating test, scored out of 5.0. A name needs at least 3.0 and may have one failing row. CG Power scores 4.0, Divi's and R R Kabel each score 3.5, and Waaree scores 1.5 with two failing rows. The third rule is the reward-to-risk floor, which Waaree fails at every price.

The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph gives the rule behind the verdict, the number that triggered it and what would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier.

CG Power: shortlisted core challenger, no order

CG Power makes motors, transformers, switchgear and railway electronics, and is building a semiconductor packaging plant. No hard red line fails: debt to equity is 0.02, the promoter pledge is nil in every quarter from September 2024 to September 2026, and three-year cash over profit is 0.74, with a range of 0.56 to 0.87 depending on how a restated year is read. Return on capital employed is 26.7 percent but has fallen from 61 percent in FY23 because most of a ₹3,000 crore share sale is still unspent. The operating test scores 4.0 of 5.0 with no failing row. The price is in a Stage 3 pullback, 2.07 average-true-range units above support, which is inside the band where the rules say do not buy, and the momentum test says there is no momentum. The shares trade near 96 times trailing profit, so the verdict holds only while the market keeps paying a very high multiple: at 70 times our FY28 profit per share the value is ₹875 and the zone-top ratio falls to 0.68. The verdict is WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT with a limit order (an order that fills only at a chosen price or better) in ₹790.54 to ₹821.03, and no purchase above ₹842.51. A momentum upgrade needs a fresh, complete pass of the gate, not just a dip.

Divi's Laboratories: shortlisted core challenger, no order

Divi's Laboratories makes active ingredients and custom-synthesis molecules for global drug companies, plus nutraceutical ingredients. Every hard red line passes: debt to equity is close to zero, interest cover is 152 times, there is no pledge, and three-year cash over profit is 0.89. The operating test scores 3.5 of 5.0 with no failing row, and the chart is in Stage 2, the rising phase of a trend, with the momentum gate reading in momentum. The difficulty is price. At 86 times trailing profit the stock needs a multiple near 75 times on consensus FY28 profit per share of ₹151.13 to reach our ₹11,335 target. That gives 1.91 to 1 at the close and 2.86 to 1 at ₹9,270.48. The scenario mix returns only about 9.2 percent a year from that level, below the 12 percent required return in the scoring file, so we went one step stricter than the rules and set a ceiling of ₹8,940.04, the price at which the mix returns 12 percent. No order is placed before the 7 November 2026 results. The rules alone would allow ACCUMULATE_ON_DIP; WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is a judgment call and the dossier says so.

R R Kabel: shortlisted tactical challenger, no order

R R Kabel makes wires, cables and fast-moving electrical goods such as fans and switches. No hard red line fails: debt to equity is 0.13, interest cover is 10 times, the pledge has been nil for twelve quarters, and three-year cash over profit is 1.02, although FY26 alone was 0.60. Return on capital employed is 28.1 percent and the operating test scores 3.5 of 5.0 with no failing row. The price is in a Stage 3 pullback only 0.81 units above support, and the momentum test says there is no momentum. The reward-to-risk ratio of 4.94 to 1 clears the 2.5 tactical floor, but it fails if the multiple falls below about 32.6 times. Because a gap through the stop at the ₹1,798 bear value would cost more than the planned risk budget, the plan was cut to a 2.0 percent weight with a 1.0 percent first order, placed only after the 28 October 2026 results and a full session that holds ₹2,106.80.

Waaree Renewable Technologies: rejected

Waaree Renewable Technologies builds solar power projects and, since a ₹1,225 crore purchase in 2026, a transmission tower maker. It is rejected on the chart and on value, and the operating test fails separately. The close of ₹677.30 sits below a falling 50-day average of ₹825.18, which sits below a falling 200-day average of ₹909.97, and the recovery test fails. The operating test scores 1.5 of 5.0, with failing rows for cash conversion (first-half operating cash was negative) and customer concentration (two customers gave 67 percent of FY26 revenue). Borrowings rose from ₹82 crore to ₹1,413 crore after the purchase. Our central value of ₹645 is below the close, so reward-to-risk is negative. The case reopens only when the chart recovers on a complete bar and the FY27 annual accounts show cash conversion and receivable days improving.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. That means some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. Each exception below names what failed, why it cannot be cured now, how it lowers confidence and what would cure it. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS, and the Divi's Laboratories dossier is also BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT.

Stale periodic filings. The citation check was run with the option that allows old as-of dates, because the latest periodic filing for each name is the June 2026 quarter or the FY26 annual report; September-quarter results are filed on 28 October (R R Kabel), 7 November (Divi's) and later for the others. The checker listed many stale records per name (99 for Divi's, 102 for R R Kabel), all grouped with ages and cures in each dossier, and no as-of date was edited. The cure is the September 2026 results.

Source access. Several sources were blocked, script-rendered or unreadable: an India Ratings rationale, the BSE shareholding page and a Q2 presentation for CG Power; an MNRE progress page and two peer pages for Waaree; call transcripts for Divi's, where call summaries and broker notes were used; and the India Ratings press release for R R Kabel. Where that happened the dossier shows N/A or names the aggregator. The ledger has no analyst consensus for Waaree, so its forward estimates are our own and are labelled as such. The cache script could not fetch several hosts, so pages were cached from the exact text retrieved.

Template and tooling. The shared dossier builder prints a few fixed labels (the momentum-gate rule wording and some table cells) that do not match the scoring file, and its glossary pass skips terms placed directly inside a table cell. Each dossier was rendered through a scratch post-processing step in the run directory that corrects those labels and wraps first uses, and each dossier says so. No repository script was modified. The cure is to update the builder.

Reviewer gate. The reviewer and the red team ran as separate agents with the configured models. CG Power, R R Kabel and Waaree were approved by the reviewer in round 2 after round 1 asked for changes, and their red teams held the verdict. For Divi's the round 2 red team approved, but the round 2 reviewer asked for two more changes (a plan sentence that no longer matched Chapter 7 and an unsupported broker claim), which were applied afterwards without a third review, so Divi's closes as BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. Wording-only notes left open by approving reviewers (one 94 versus 96 times cell in CG Power, three phrases in Waaree) were not reopened. This summary report was not reviewed separately.

Judgment calls. CG Power clears its floor only while the market keeps paying a multiple above about 78 times. Divi's WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is stricter than the rules require, because its expected return at the first support level is below the 12 percent hurdle. R R Kabel fails its floor if the multiple falls below about 32.6 times. Waaree has no analyst consensus. Email: not sent.
