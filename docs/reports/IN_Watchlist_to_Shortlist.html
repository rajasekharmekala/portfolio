This page reports one batch of the watchlist-to-shortlist process, which takes the first four eligible names in watchlist order: BSE, HBLPOWER, MENONBE and ACUTAAS. The watchlist is the queue of companies waiting for a full underwriting, and the shortlist holds those that have passed every test. Each name is tested against three layers of hard floors, an operating scorecard and an entry-timing test. A company that fails a floor is rejected at once and moves to the archive, where its reasons stay on file. The verdict column is the decision, and the gate column names the floor that failed.

Glossary

RSI (relative strength index) is a 14-day speed gauge that runs from 0 to 100, where a reading above 70 means the stock has risen unusually fast.

ATR (average true range) is the stock's normal daily move in rupees, which is used to measure distance to support in multiples of a day's move.

A base is a sideways trading range that forms after a decline, and a breakout is a close above the top of that range.

Asymmetry is the ratio of the possible gain to the target over the possible loss to the rule stop, so 3.0 means the gain is three times the loss.

The number: the target MENONBE needs for a 3.5 ratio

The number is 383.03 rupees. It is the price at which the possible gain, measured from today's close, is 3.5 times the possible loss to the rule stop.

How it was calculated

The target equals the close plus 3.5 times the risk per share. The risk per share is the close minus the rule stop, which is 286.25 minus 258.60, or 27.65 rupees. So the target is 286.25 plus 3.5 times 27.65, which is 383.03 rupees.

The inputs

The close of 286.25 is the ledger close for 13 October 2026. The rule stop of 258.60 is the swing low of 268.15 less half of the 19.10 ATR, taken from the ledger. The 3.5 floor is the Tactical Momentum asymmetry floor in the scoring file.

The range

On the 380 target that the earlier shortlist used, the ratio is 3.39 to 1, which is 0.11 below the floor. A verified target between 380 and 383.03 would still leave the name short of the floor, so the range that matters starts at 383.03.

If the number is off

If the rule stop moved up to 268 instead of 258.60, the risk per share would fall to 18.25 rupees and the target needed would fall to about 350, which would change the answer. That is why the stop is set by the rule, not by the earlier shortlist entry.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This batch was published with gate exceptions. Each one is listed here so the summary repeats the dossier notes, and none of them changes a verdict in this batch.

  • Source age. The citation gate passes with exceptions for all four dossiers. Several records are older than the 60-day limit because the latest quarterly filing is the June 2026 quarter, and the annual figures are for the year to March 2026. Each dossier lists its records with their ages and the cure, which is the September 2026 quarter or the FY27 annual results. No as-of date was changed to pass the gate.
  • Reviewer gate. Each dossier went through one reviewer round with a red-team pass in round 1. BSE returned APPROVE in round 2 with notes only. HBLPOWER, MENONBE and ACUTAAS returned REVISE in round 2, and their best-effort fixes were made after the two-round limit and were not re-reviewed, so their status is BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. The writer judges that none of the four verdicts changes because of these open items, but for the three unreviewed names that judgment is the writer's, not a reviewed one.
  • Momentum script defect. The support-distance function in scripts/momentum_gate.py takes the lowest of the candidate support levels, not the nearest, so distances to support are overstated. For MENONBE this turns a passing Test A condition into a failure. The script is a repository file and was not changed. Its effect on this batch is limited to MENONBE, because the rejection rests on the asymmetry floor. The cure is to change the function to use the nearest support and rerun the gate.
  • Thresholds. The momentum thresholds in scoring.json are marked as needing calibration against live data. The verdicts here rest on the Layer 1 floors, which are not in that state.
  • Symbol alias. The watchlist symbol HBLPOWER trades on the NSE as HBLENGINE. The ledger alias maps the one to the other, and the dossier links were set to the NSE symbol.
  • Validator floors. The full validator reports thin sections, because its word floors (500 prose words per section) are written for market and audit reports. This register is a short decision summary. Padding it to the floors would add no analysis, so the floors are disclosed here as a residual finding; the four dossiers carry the full analysis.
  • Ledger failures. The price download failed for C2C, DUMMYHEG and WSTLIFESTY. None is in this batch. C2C and WSTLIFESTY remain in the queue, and no cell in this batch depends on them.
  • Holdings. The holdings log is treated as the authoritative record, and the stale-holdings warning from the ledger is recorded in the manifest. No holding is in this batch.
