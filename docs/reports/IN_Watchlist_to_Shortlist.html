Operating evidence and business quality

Management reported ₹194.5 crore H1 FY27 revenue, ₹54.7 crore EBITDA and ₹36.2 crore PAT. EBITDA margin rose to 28.1% from 23.3% in H1 FY26. The period-end order book stood at ₹760 crore and was expected to execute in 15–18 months. Cash conversion improved to 63 days from 81 days at FY26 close, while the existing Halol unit was near 90% utilization. These indicators support a real demand and capacity constraint; they do not show how concentrated customers are or how much of the order book can be cancelled, repriced or deferred. Yash’s product qualification, installed field base and application engineering can create switching friction, especially for high-voltage equipment where failure is costly. The company’s own materials cite 50,000-plus installed bushings, 60-plus countries and a 96%-plus repeat-order rate; these are issuer claims, not independently reconciled customer data. The new plant is the central execution test: management said imported-core assembly had begun invoicing, but commercial production of localized RIP product was expected in Q4 FY27 and localized 420/550 kV products no earlier than Q4 FY28 or mid-2028. A facility opening is not the same as qualified production. The product overview, OIP range, RIP/RIS range, quality systems and IEC 60137 standard show the technical context. Hitachi Energy, GE Vernova and Trench Group provide relevant product comparisons.

In the call, CEO Keyur Shah described the facility as “on track and as expected”; finance was represented by Executive Director Darshan Thakkar, who said management intended to maintain PAT margins despite higher depreciation. No CFO joined the call, so we do not attribute that statement to the CFO. The presentation says the 36-month U.S. representation agreement covers 22 states, while the call described close to 27 states. H1 exports were only 5% of revenue, so the sales footprint has not yet demonstrated a material earnings contribution. Management record, catalysts and risk

The firm listed in 2024, leaving a short public record for checking two or three years of management promises. The ₹220 crore facility is progressing: ₹106 crore had been spent by 30-Sep, and assembly with imported cores had started invoicing. The remaining milestones are localized RIP commercial production in Q4 FY27, customer qualification for higher-voltage products and a stated FY30–31 peak-revenue ambition near ₹1,000 crore. Those are forward targets and are not included as committed base-case revenue. The queued ₹2,000 crore figure is removed as unverified.

The thesis rests on localizing imported cores, ramping capacity without quality slips, and collecting the backlog before the supply cycle normalizes. A premium multiple makes delays costly. Customer concentration, detailed warranty experience, order cancellation terms and product-level economics are not disclosed in the materials reviewed. Potential warrants and ESOPs add 6.36% dilution if fully converted. The market regime is weak, and this BSE SME name may have wider spreads and thinner depth than the large-cap shares used in the benchmark.
