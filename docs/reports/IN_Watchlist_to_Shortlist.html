Before you read

This report sums up one batch of four India names, each with a full research dossier. NRB Bearings makes bearings and is sound, but its price already pays for the recovery, so it is rejected. Alicon Castalloy sells aluminium castings and is rejected because plant use and spending missed their targets, which leaves two failing rows in its operating score, a five-measure scorecard of the business. Huhtamaki India and Minda Corporation pass the safety rules and the operating test, but at today's prices the reward does not cover the risk, so they wait for a lower price and fresh results. Each dossier states the price or event that would change its verdict, and nothing here is a buy today.

What the batch found

Each row gives the verdict, the 28 October close, the entry zone (the price range where we would place a buy order if the name qualified), the stop, our central target and the reward-to-risk ratio. That ratio is the gain to the target divided by the loss to the stop. A stop is the price at which the idea is wrong. The scoring file asks for at least 2.0 for a core name and 2.5 for a tactical or turnaround name. Read the ratio at the close together with the ratio at the top of the entry zone. The first is what a buyer gets today. The second is what a limit order, which is an order to buy only at or below a set price, would get after a pullback.

The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph gives the rule behind the verdict, the number that drove it and what would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier.

NRBBEARING: a sound business, priced for the recovery

NRB Bearings makes needle and cylindrical roller bearings. It passes every hard red line: debt to equity 0.16, interest cover 25.3 times, three-year cash conversion 0.94 and a promoter pledge of 3.29 percent of the promoter holding. The pledge matters because it was 77.7 percent at March 2026, with a top-up in January 2026, and a promoter stake sale of about seven points cleared it. The operating test scores 3.5 of 5.0 with no failing row, but the chart is a Stage 3 pullback with the momentum gate at NO_MOMENTUM: the close of ₹454.85 sits below the 50-day average of ₹493.48 and 1.17 ATR (average true range, the typical daily price swing) above support at ₹428.55. The rejection is a price-and-timing call, not a quality call. Our weighted value of ₹436.50 is 4.0 percent below the close against a 12 percent hurdle, reward-to-risk is -0.16 to 1, and the price that would deliver 12 percent (about ₹397) is below the stop. Management's 19.5 percent FY26 operating margin (the profit from running the business as a share of sales) includes ₹34 crore of other income; on sales alone it is 17.4 percent. Our FY28 earnings are ₹17.5 per share against ₹19.90 for the street. The dossier shows that a September-quarter margin near 18.5 percent on sales up 15 percent or more is only a signal to rebuild the model (it gives about ₹18.2, not ₹19.90). Reopening needs a fresh chart gate and both the 12 percent return and a 2.0 reward-to-risk at the actual entry, which on the street's earnings means a price near ₹424 or lower.

ALICON: a sales rebound that has not reached the margin or the plant

Alicon casts aluminium engine parts, mostly cylinder heads. Sales rose 38 percent in the June 2026 quarter, but the EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) margin is falling and the return on capital is 10.6 percent, below the 15 percent compounder floor. The red lines pass, with interest cover thin at 2.10 times. The operating test is where the verdict turned. Plant use was 78 percent in FY26 against an 85 percent pass line, and FY26 spending was about 18 percent short of its ₹165 to ₹170 crore plan, so that row scores FAIL. With the margin row also failing, the score is 2.5 of 5.0 with two failing rows, below the 3.0 minimum. The first draft scored that row WATCH and returned WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT; the reviewer showed that the report's own bands give FAIL, and the verdict was changed. The share price jumped 20 percent in one day on 30 September on 14.8 times the usual volume, and the company told the exchange it does not know why. At ₹870.35 our weighted value gives a 9.5 percent gain, below the 12 percent hurdle. The name reopens only after the 12 November 2026 results, and only if at least one failing row improves to WATCH (a margin of 10 percent or more, plant use of 85 percent or more, or first-half spending of about ₹68 crore) and a written earnings and target refresh clears both the 12 percent hurdle and the 2.5 floor at the actual price. There is no analyst consensus, so every forecast and the 24 times multiple are ours.

HUHTAMAKI: a 20 percent jump with no offer behind it

Huhtamaki India makes printed pouches and labels, and the Finnish parent owns 67.73 percent. The stock fell 11 percent to ₹191.31 on 27 October after results showed flat operating profit, then rose 20.0 percent to ₹229.57 on 28 October on 5.3 times its usual volume. The company has not said why. The dossier searched the NSE announcements to 28 October at 18:11 IST, a takeover and delisting bulletin, and company and parent pages, and found no open offer, delisting or takeover, so it treats the stock as an ordinary listed company. The seed ratios (ROCE, return on capital employed, of 17.6 percent, 31 percent three-year sales growth) came from a stale consolidated Screener page; the standalone page gives ROCE 12.0 percent, three-year sales growth of -6 percent and a price-to-earnings ratio, which is the share price divided by a year's profit per share, of 12.7. All red lines pass (debt to equity 0.11, interest cover 17.0 times, no pledge, three-year cash conversion 1.06) and Layer 2 scores 3.5 of 5.0. ROCE is below 15 percent, so the archetype is TURNAROUND_VALUE_INFLECTION, and the bucket moves from core to tactical. Reward-to-risk is 1.00 at the close, so there is no market buy. A limit order between ₹192.00 and ₹198.70 needs refreshed earnings, target, scenario weights and stop with at least 2.5 to 1 at the actual fill. The ₹198.70 top is the highest price that still clears 2.5 if the full-year margin is 6.5 percent instead of 7.2 percent, and a 5.0 percent margin would put the value near ₹190. The dossier also states that a gap to the bear value of ₹125 would lose more than the 0.35 percent risk budget, so the stop is a plan, not a cap.

MINDACORP: improving numbers, a one-off in the profit and a price above its buy zone

Minda Corporation sold ₹6,185 crore of locks, wiring, displays and cast parts in the year to March 2026. It passes every red line (borrowings 0.56 times equity, interest cover 4.2 times, no pledge, three-year cash conversion 1.79) and scores 3.5 of 5.0 with no failing row. Reported ROCE is 12.7 percent, because the ₹1,400 crore stake in Flash earns about 5 percent, so it qualifies as TURNAROUND_VALUE_INFLECTION, not as a compounder. The June 2026 profit of ₹206 crore includes a one-off ₹106 crore gain; without it the ledger's reverse DCF reading of 12.0 percent growth would be about 15.2 percent. Net debt, which is borrowings minus cash, is 1.2 times EBITDA on the company's measure, which counts 49 percent of Flash's EBITDA, and about 1.5 times on Minda's own. The chart is a Stage 3 pullback at 2.17 ATR above support, outside the 1.6 ATR no-buy line. Reward-to-risk is 1.20 at the close and 3.10 at the ₹596 zone top, and the highest price that still clears the 2.5 floor is ₹602.70. Limit orders between ₹580 and ₹596 go live only after the 3 November 2026 results, and only with no sign that the promoters will skip the ₹315.6 crore warrant payment due by 2 December 2026. The dossier also states that the 36 times multiple must be justified on its own at that point: at the 29.3 times of a close peer the value falls to about ₹583.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. A gate that the evidence could not fully meet is listed here instead of being hidden.

First, the stale-date exception. Each citation check ended PASS WITH EXCEPTIONS because many research records are older than 60 days: audited annual figures, rating letters, earlier management calls used to audit what management promised, and June-quarter filings. The counts of stale lines are 109 for NRBBEARING, 63 for ALICON, 48 for HUHTAMAKI and 85 for MINDACORP. NRBBEARING, ALICON and MINDACORP have not yet reported their September quarter (due mid-November, 12 November and 3 November 2026), so their earnings figures are for the June quarter. HUHTAMAKI filed its September-quarter results on 27 October 2026, but the newest balance sheet and cash-flow statement are for 30 June 2026. Every as-of date is true, and each dossier lists the stale records with the filing that cures them.

Second, the reviewer cycle. HUHTAMAKI is the only name approved by the reviewer: it returned REVISE in round 1 with two findings and APPROVE in round 2 after the writer fixed both. NRBBEARING, ALICON and MINDACORP did not reach approval. Each reviewer returned REVISE in round 1 (sixteen findings for NRBBEARING, nine REVISE findings for ALICON and six for MINDACORP) and again in round 2 (one REVISE finding each: the quarter named in a hinge claim, the reopen-condition wording, and an unrendered citation placeholder). Those findings were corrected after round 2 and no third review was run, so all three are marked BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. The red team ran on its configured model (ao-foundry-lc/gpt-6.1-sol) in every round it ran. It held the NRBBEARING verdict in round 1 with one finding on the reopen arithmetic and approved in round 2. For ALICON and HUHTAMAKI it asked for a stricter verdict in round 1: ALICON moved from WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT to REJECTED, and HUHTAMAKI kept its verdict but lowered the zone top from ₹203 to ₹198.70 and tightened its entry and add-on rules; both were approved in round 2. The MINDACORP red team approved in round 1 and was not rerun.

Third, tooling and data. The shared structural validator has no dossier mode, so the packet's code checks, the readability check, the citation check and the layout check were used instead. The DuckDuckGo search tool was blocked for some writers, who used the harness search and fetch tools instead, and several sources could not be read: Moneycontrol (403), Reuters (401), NSE bulk-deal and quote interfaces, BSE PDFs and scanned image filings. Company and exchange PDFs were converted to text and stored in the fetch cache with the text-file option, and one unreadable NRBBEARING call-transcript cache entry was replaced with its extracted text; no entry was edited by hand. A Nykaa call PDF that was cached under an Alicon source address is not cited anywhere. The MINDACORP dossier was rendered through a run-local wrapper that calls the shared builder and then inserts two lead-in paragraphs the builder has no slot for; the repository script was not changed. The ledger's reverse DCF uses profit as a stand-in for cash flow, so each dossier shows its own cross-check. Consensus is thin: three analysts for NRBBEARING, none for ALICON and one for HUHTAMAKI, and the ALICON forecasts and multiple are ours. The ALICON writer regenerated the round 1 packet files in place before the round 2 delta, leaving both round 1 review files untouched. The holdings log was assumed accurate and its freshness warning was not independently reconciled; none of the four names is held. Four writers worked in parallel on one shared fetch cache and scratch folder.
