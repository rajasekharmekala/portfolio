Business and new evidence

TDPS builds custom AC generators and electric motors. Its buyers include turbine and engine makers, factories, utilities and project firms. The company also sells spare parts and repair work. It does not report profit by product, service revenue, plant yield or use by site. That limits evidence on repeat income and returns on new capacity [1][11][12][13][14][15][16][17].

FY26 revenue from operations was ₹1,856.23 crore, PAT ₹238.77 crore, order inflow ₹2,238 crore and year-end backlog ₹1,973 crore. Exports and deemed exports were 79% of inflows. The ten largest customers made up 84% of revenue [1]. Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹640.05 crore, up 72% year over year; PAT was ₹86.29 crore, also up 72%. EBITDA margin was 19.0%; backlog was ₹2,207.3 crore [2][3][4].

Management raised its FY27 revenue guide to ₹2,600 crore and described about ₹3,200 crore of FY28 capacity. These are future targets, not delivered sales. TDPS beat its prior FY26 guide, which is positive; it does not prove the next capacity step or its return [1][4][18].

An October 1 filing disclosed an order of about ₹192 crore through TDPS USA for its own-design two-pole generators for US gas turbines. Delivery is due from August 2027 to January 2028. The buyer and margin were not disclosed [5]. On 12 October, TDPS allotted 7,741,935 shares at ₹775 and raised ₹5,999,999,625, or about ₹600 crore. Shares rose from 313,706,860 to 321,448,795, a 2.47% increase on the pre-issue base. The filing gives no use of proceeds or return target [6].

FY26 operating cash flow was ₹129.07 crore; receivables were ₹742.09 crore and inventory was ₹503.06 crore. Track these beside sales growth [1]. The annual report lists 68 service centers and 8,055 machines supplied; the home page lists 57 centers and 6,300-plus installations. Dates and definitions differ or are unclear, so the figures are not combined [1][10].

Filings support growth and order visibility. Customer mix, margins by order, service income and cash conversion remain open tests. The next section turns these gaps into explicit review triggers.
