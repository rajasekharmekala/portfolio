The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph states the rule behind the verdict, the number that triggered it and the level or event that would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier, with five-year tables, a peer comparison, growth scenarios and sources.

Astra Microwave: hold 200 shares, add none

Astra Microwave makes radio-frequency and radar electronics for defence and space programmes. Its red lines pass, but three-year cash conversion of 0.246 sits just under the 0.25 line and passes only through a turnaround clause. The operating scorecard scores 3.0 out of 5 with one fail, which is exactly the qualifying line. Reward-to-risk is 1.17 to 1 at the close and reaches the 2.5 floor only near ₹1,523. The plan is to sell all shares on a close below ₹1,493.31, and to sell 100 shares if half-year revenue to September 2026 is ₹392 crore or less. The 7.15 percent holding is above the 3.5 percent ceiling for a new position, and the dossier treats it as a disclosed exception.

EPACK Prefab: hold 1,050 shares, add none

EPACK Prefab builds pre-engineered steel buildings and insulated panels. All red lines pass, and three-year cash conversion is 1.35 as reported. The stock is 1.21 average daily ranges above support, and reward-to-risk is 1.70 to 1 against the 2.0 core floor. The dossier suspends the planned limit order to add shares, which is an order that fills at your price or better. Any add reopens only after the September-quarter results are filed, a support hold is observed, and the ratio is recalculated at 2.0 or better. The exit is a close below ₹201.82, and the dossier adds that a next-open sale is not a guaranteed loss cap.

Tanfac Industries: hold 55 shares, add none

Tanfac makes hydrofluoric acid and is building a refrigerant plant. It passes the red lines and qualifies as a compounder only through the 0.50 manufacturing relief on cash conversion. Reward-to-risk is 0.21 to 1 at the close and reaches 2.0 only in an entry zone of ₹2,951 to ₹2,985, the planned buying band. The thesis hinges on a refrigerant quota of about 13,000 tonnes a year from 2028, which is not yet allocated. The plan sells the entire holding on a close below ₹2,762, or on a quota below that level, or on a September-quarter margin below 15 percent.

Netweb Technologies: hold 40 shares, add none

Netweb designs servers for high-performance computing and artificial intelligence. Revenue and profit are growing very fast, backed by a large firm order book, but reward-to-risk is 0.32 to 1 and three-year cash conversion is 0.447. The board meets on 24 October on the company's own notice, and the call follows on 26 October. The plan sells all 40 shares at the next open after a verified close below ₹4,350. An unverified aggregator print on 23 October sits below that level, so the dossier asks you to check the official close before acting. It uses no 23 October price in any calculation.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. That means some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. Each exception below names what failed, why it cannot be cured now, how it lowers confidence and what would cure it. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS.

Data delay. The price feed had no 23 October 2026 bar for the Nifty 50 after one retry. All prices and technical levels therefore use the 22 October session. Some pages show 23 October prints, and the dossiers label those unverified and use none of them in a calculation.

Stale periodic filings. The latest periodic filing for each company is the quarter to 30 June 2026, and no September-quarter results exist yet. The citation check therefore lists many records as EXCEPTION AS-OF lines, 129 for Astra Microwave and 156 for EPACK Prefab, and grouped lists for Tanfac and Netweb. The matching dossier lists each group with its age and cure. The cure is to rerun the research once the September-quarter results are filed.

Ledger fields. For Astra Microwave and EPACK Prefab, the ledger's market value, price-to-earnings (share price divided by profit per share) and reverse-DCF fields follow a price other than the 22 October close. EPACK Prefab's dossier was rendered from a scratch copy of the ledger with its own reverse DCF, and the other dossiers state their own figures beside the ledger's. The cure is to rebuild the ledger fundamentals at the verified close.

Validator mismatch. The shared structural validator has only market-report and portfolio-audit modes. Run on a single-company dossier, it fails checks built for those formats, such as case notes for all 19 holdings. The review packet code skips it for dossiers, and no script was modified. The readability check also looks for estimate parts and a Chapter 7 that this summary does not carry, because the dossiers do. The cure is a dossier mode in the validator and a summary mode in the readability check.

Reviewer gate. Netweb and Tanfac received reviewer approval in round 2. Astra Microwave and EPACK Prefab received REVISE in round 1 and REVISE in round 2, and their remaining findings were corrected afterwards without a third review, under the two-round cap. Their status is BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT and they did not receive reviewer approval. The red team held the verdict for all four, and the stricter add rule it asked for at EPACK Prefab was applied. This summary report was not reviewed separately.

Holding label. The holding verdict list has no hold-and-wait label, so the routing rule's WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is applied to current holdings and printed with its plain meaning. The orchestrating agent could not start nested subagents, so the four company agents and the reviewers ran as separate sessions, one per company.
