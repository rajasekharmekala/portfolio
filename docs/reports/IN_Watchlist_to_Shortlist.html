The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph states the rule behind the verdict, the number that triggered it and the level or event that would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier, with five-year tables, a peer comparison, growth scenarios and sources.

Transformers and Rectifiers: sell all 400 shares

Transformers and Rectifiers makes large power transformers and has a record order book. It fails two hard red lines at once. A promoter director has pledged 42,200,000 shares, 21.84 percent of the promoter holding, against a 20 percent limit, and consolidated operating cash over three years is 0.150 of profit against 0.25. Its chart reads as an early turn, but the price is 3.18 daily ranges above the structural floor, and the reward-to-risk is 1.43 to 1 against the 2.5 floor on a central value of ₹394.00. The plan sells all shares at the next open, as a rule-based exclusion and not a forecast that it will lag a replacement. A return needs a pledge release of at least 3,560,341 shares, audited FY27 cash flow that lifts the three-year ratio to 0.25, a passing Test A or B on a fresh session, and a limit entry between ₹281 and ₹297.

Oriana Power: sell all 375 shares, on a stated judgment

Oriana Power builds solar and battery-storage projects and earns a return on capital near 40 percent on reported figures. Its shares fell sharply after a five-for-one split in September, and the price feed did not adjust for it, so the dossier rebuilt the averages. On the corrected series the 50-day average is down 1.0 percent over ten sessions but flat over 21, so the Stage 4 label is borderline. The stage code has no tolerance, the close is below the 20-day average and the dossier keeps the sale as its own judgment, with an override stop at ₹232.87 and a return test that needs a non-Stage-4 chart, the half-year filings and a split of the credit arrangement liabilities. The 4.59 to 1 ratio is a stop-based figure. Against the full bear case the ratio is about 0.61 to 1.

Laurus Labs: hold 31 shares, add none

Laurus Labs makes active ingredients and contract-manufactured drugs, and it passes every red line with its chart in Stage 2 (a confirmed uptrend). The reward-to-risk at the close is 0.86 to 1 against the 2.0 floor, so no purchase is enabled. Any add needs a written re-underwriting after the 29 October results, a day order at ₹1,941 to ₹1,955, and a fresh gate. Account cash covers one share, so the full 37-share add would need a sale elsewhere. The target uses the dossier's own five-year median multiple of 70.8 times, which is stated as an assumption. The stop is ₹1,888.35.

Manorama Industries: hold 55 shares, add none, as a time-limited exception

Manorama makes specialty fats for chocolate and cosmetics. Its three-year cash ratio on audited figures is 0.12, which fails the line, and the hold rests on the dossier's own judgment that FY26 working capital improved enough to excuse it. The exception ends at the FY27 annual results. If FY27 operating cash is not positive or is below 0.40 of profit, about ₹124 crore at central profit, all shares are sold. Adds need six conditions and a chart that confirms. The stop is ₹1,635.75, and the dossier shows that on the stricter stop of ₹1,489.95, set at the 63-day low, the ratio falls to 1.91.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. That means some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. Each exception below names what failed, why it cannot be cured now, how it lowers confidence and what would cure it. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS.

Data delay. The 23 October 2026 price bars were missing or stale for a large share of the market after retries and rate limits, and two names in this batch had no 23 October bar. All prices and technical levels therefore use the 22 October session. Some pages show 23 October prints, and the dossiers label those unverified and use none of them in a calculation.

Stale periodic filings. The latest periodic filing for each company is the quarter to 30 June 2026, and no September-quarter results exist yet. The citation check therefore lists many records as EXCEPTION AS-OF lines, from 76 to 138 per dossier. Each dossier lists each group with its age and cure. The cure is to rerun the research once the September-quarter results are filed.

Ledger fields. The ledger has no reverse-DCF or fundamentals for some of these names, and its price history for Oriana Power is not adjusted for the September split, so its averages, RSI (relative strength index) (a 0 to 100 momentum reading), daily range and support distance are wrong for that name. The dossier rebuilt them on an adjusted series and states its own figures beside the ledger's. The cure is an adjusted price feed.

Validator mismatch. The shared structural validator has only market-report and portfolio-audit modes. Run on a single-company dossier, it fails a check built for those formats, which asks for case notes for all holdings. The review packet code skips it for dossiers, and no script was modified. The readability check also looks for estimate parts and a Chapter 7 that this summary does not carry, because the dossiers do. The cure is a dossier mode in the validator and a summary mode in the readability check.

Reviewer gate. Laurus Labs and Transformers and Rectifiers were approved in round 2. Manorama Industries and Oriana Power received REVISE in round 2 on consistency points, which were corrected afterwards without a third review under the two-round cap, so their status is BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT. The red team held the verdict for all four. This summary report was not reviewed separately.

Holding labels. The holding verdict list has no hold-and-wait label, so the routing rule's WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT is applied to current holdings and printed with its plain meaning. Manorama Industries holds on a failed red line only through the writer's own judgment, which its dossier states in every section. The orchestrating agent could not start nested subagents, so the four company agents and the reviewers ran as separate sessions, one per company.

Cache note. The shared page cache keys each entry without the query string, so exchange announcement links that differ only by query string share one entry. This run cited company-hosted documents where possible, and other runs that cite those links may hit the same collision.
