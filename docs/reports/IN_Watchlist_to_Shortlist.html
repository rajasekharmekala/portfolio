Before you read

This report sums up one batch of four India names, each with a full research dossier. Three of them, Godfrey Phillips, Jyothy Labs and Fiem Industries, are rejected because their charts are in a Stage 4 downtrend that has not yet shown a recovery, and Godfrey also fails a cash-conversion rule. Tamilnad Mercantile Bank is a small, very well capitalised bank that earns a place in the core shortlist, but only a pullback to between ₹770.30 and ₹794.67 would give it a reward-to-risk ratio that clears the 2.0 floor on both of its valuation tests. Each dossier states the price or event that would change its verdict. Nothing here is a buy today.

What the batch found

Each row gives the verdict, the close used, the entry zone (the price range where we would place a buy order, shown for the rejected names only as a reference), the stop, our central target and the reward-to-risk ratio. That ratio is the gain to the target divided by the loss to the stop. A stop is the price at which the idea is wrong. The scoring file asks for at least 2.0 for a core name. Read the ratio at the close together with the ratio at the top of the entry zone. The first is what a buyer gets today. The second is what a limit order would get after a pullback.

The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph below says what the dossier concluded, why, and the level or event that would change it. The dossiers carry the full working, including five-year tables, estimate sections and a numbered source list.

TMB: a bank with very high capital and clean loans whose price asks for patience

Tamilnad Mercantile Bank reported a return on equity of 16.92 percent (annualised from the September quarter), a return on assets of 2.18 percent, gross bad loans of 0.61 percent, net bad loans of 0.14 percent and a capital ratio of 32.39 percent. Debt-to-equity, cash conversion and return on capital employed do not describe a bank, so the dossier uses return on equity, asset quality and capital ratios in their place. Our own forward estimate of sustainable return on equity is 14.3 percent, just under the 15 percent floor, so that test is a conditional pass, and the COMPOUNDER label holds because the rule tests the reported figure. The operating score is 4.0 of 5.0, with three passes (asset quality, capital and the source of profit growth) and two watch rows (margin and funding, and concentration in gold loans, which are 48 percent of advances, and in Tamil Nadu, which holds 80.9 percent). Profit in the September quarter rose 42.09 percent, and the dossier shows that this came from core lending income and not from tax or recoveries. But management guides the margin down and advances growth to about 7.3 percent in the second half, so our FY28E earnings per share is ₹112, only 3 percent above our FY27E figure. The share price is in Stage 3, 1.36 ATR (average true range, the typical daily price swing) above support, and Test C fails only on its short-term turn. Our base value is ₹1,010 against bull ₹1,350 and bear ₹600. A pending Enforcement Directorate penalty of ₹1,037 crore on the bank's bonus shares is the largest single risk. A close below ₹739.84 ends the idea, and a gap from the zone to the bear value would lose about 24.5 percent, so the size is capped at the 3.5 percent tier maximum.

FIEMIND: a sound lighting supplier whose chart has not turned

Fiem Industries makes lighting and mirrors for two-wheelers. Every money-based floor passes: return on capital 29.3 percent, debt-to-equity 0.05, three-year cash conversion 0.84 and no promoter pledge. The share fell about 36 percent from its August level, but profit did not fall. The price-to-earnings multiple dropped from about 27 times to 17 times after the August results and call, when the operating margin came in at 13.52 percent and the four-wheeler target moved to FY28. Three customers account for 69.2 percent of revenue. Our central value is ₹2,156 (FY28E earnings per share ₹125.50 at 17.18 times), the probability-weighted value is ₹2,129.77 (+24.1 percent), and an adverse mix of the three cases still gives +16.0 percent, about 10.4 percent a year, which is under the 12 percent hurdle. None of that overrides the chart rule: the close is below a falling 50-day average that sits below a falling 200-day average, and Test C fails only because the close is ₹61.12 under the 20-day average. The case reopens when a refreshed bar passes Test C, the 12 November 2026 results show revenue growth of at least 15 percent and an operating margin of at least 13.8 percent, reward-to-risk is recomputed at 2.0 or more, and the weighted value clears the hurdle.

JYOTHYLAB: a debt-free household-products maker whose profit is falling

Jyothy Labs has almost no debt (debt-to-equity 0.03, interest cover 90 times), no pledge and three-year cash conversion of 1.10, so the safety rules pass and the chart rule fails: Stage 4, with Test C failing only because the close is 4.6 percent below the 20-day average. The operating score is 3.0 of 5.0, with one failing row: gross margin fell to 38.5 percent from 48 percent a year earlier as crude-linked costs rose. Henkel did not renew the Pril and Fa licences, which were 7 to 8 percent of revenue, and sales under them stopped on 1 June 2026. Consensus earnings per share for FY27 fell 23.3 percent in 90 days. The watchlist seed was wrong on all three of its numbers: return on capital is 28.7 percent (not 24.6), the price-to-earnings multiple is 22.6 (not 17.5), and the three-year profit growth of 32 percent counts from a low in FY22 and is 11.5 percent from FY23 to FY26. Reward-to-risk is 4.01 to 1 at the close, but only 1.19 to 1 at a stressed multiple of 20 times. The probability-weighted value of ₹194.19 is +10.9 percent on price alone and about 12.9 percent only if the ₹3.50 dividend is repeated, so the return side is borderline. The case reopens on a refreshed Test C pass and FY28E earnings per share of about ₹9.60 or more, after the 4 November 2026 results show an operating margin of at least 8.4 percent.

GODFRYPHLP: a cigarette maker whose profit has not finished resetting

Godfrey Phillips has a strong balance sheet (return on capital 30.2 percent, debt-to-equity 0.04), but it fails two red lines. The chart is a literal Stage 4, with Test C failing only because the close is 3.0 percent below the 20-day average. And three-year cash from operations was ₹916 crore against profit of ₹3,482 crore, a ratio of 0.26 against the 0.40 line. The February 2026 tax change cut June-quarter profit by 44.3 percent on a volume fall of only 2 percent, and the headline price-to-earnings multiple of 19.9 sits on trailing profit that still holds three quarters from before the change. Using the June quarter times four, the multiple is about 34, and the reverse DCF rises from 6.59 percent a year to about 13.8. About 45 percent of standalone revenue is sales to Philip Morris entities on unpublished terms, and associate profit and fair-value gains are 22.5 percent of pre-tax profit. Our central value is ₹1,541 (FY28E earnings per share ₹77.40 at 19.9 times), which is 11.6 percent below the close, so reward-to-risk is negative. The seed was right on its figures and wrong to call sales excise-inflated, because the sales line is net of excise. The case reopens only when all four hold: a refreshed bar passes Test C, the 5 November 2026 results show volume of at least 1,800 million sticks a month and EBITDA of at least ₹205 crore, cash from operations is on track for about ₹784 crore in FY27, and reward-to-risk recomputes at 2.0 or more.

Both books were already at or above their limits (12 core, 10 tactical), so a new name must beat the weakest incumbent, which is chosen by a fixed order of tests: operating-test pass count first, then how far the price sits above support, then how near the next catalyst is, then reward-to-risk. Eleven of the twelve core incumbents have 3 passes of 5, and Tamilnad Mercantile Bank has 3 as well, so the first test ties. TMB sits 1.36 ATR above support, expects its next results about the same time, and has reward-to-risk of 2.75. It wins the posture and reward-to-risk tests and ties the catalyst test, so it takes the slot and CGPOWER becomes QUALIFIED_DEFERRED. The three rejected names did not qualify for the shortlist.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. A gate that the evidence could not fully meet is listed here instead of being hidden.

First, the stale-date exception. Each citation check ended PASS WITH EXCEPTIONS because many research records are older than 60 days: annual reports, earlier management calls used to audit what management promised, and June-quarter filings. The stale lines are 109 for Godfrey, 94 for Jyothy Labs, 95 for Fiem and 21 for Tamilnad Mercantile Bank. For Godfrey, the Q1 FY27 filing is 94 days old and Q2 is due on 5 November; for Jyothy Labs Q2 is due on 4 November; for Fiem the newest filing is 78 days old and Q2 is due on 12 November. For the bank, Q2 FY27 was filed on 26 October, and its stale lines are historical records. Every as-of date is true, and each dossier lists its own stale lines. The cure is the Q2 FY27 filings in the first half of November 2026.

Second, the data. The first ledger build for 29 October lost 190 instruments to a data-vendor rate limit, so TMB had no data. It was rebuilt once the limit cleared, with the four batch names added so that the ledger's reverse DCF and consensus fields were filled. The ledger's reverse DCF for the bank (negative 5.36 percent growth) is not meaningful for a bank, so the dossier replaces it with a price-to-book test and keeps the ledger value visible. Some items were not found and are shown as not found: consensus earnings for Godfrey and for the bank, the Philip Morris royalty terms, and the Godfrey Q1 FY27 call transcript. The fetch cache does not extract text from PDF files, so the writers extracted PDFs locally and stored the text through the cache's text-file option. Several exchange pages were blocked, so some figures come from aggregator pages and are labelled in the dossiers.

Third, the reviewer cycle. All four dossiers returned REVISE from the reviewer in round 1, and the red team returned verdict_holds for all four, with REVISE findings for Godfrey, Fiem and the bank. The writers fixed every finding. In round 2 the reviewer returned APPROVE for Godfrey and Fiem and REVISE for Jyothy Labs and Tamilnad Mercantile Bank, each with one finding: the wording of the REJECTED_BELOW_HURDLE label against the 12 percent hurdle for Jyothy Labs, and the 14.3 percent forward return on equity against the 15 percent floor for the bank. Both were fixed after the final round without a third review, so those two carry BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT and did not receive a reviewer APPROVE. The red team returned APPROVE in round 2 for the three dossiers that needed a second pass. Small NOTE-level edits to the Godfrey dossier were applied after its final round with no figures changed.

Fourth, tooling. The shared structural validator has no dossier mode, so the packet's code checks, the readability check, the citation check and the layout check were used instead. The first-use glossary tagging in the page builder skips text that sits directly inside a paragraph, so the writers applied the same rules in a post-render pass without editing the repository's scripts. The four writers shared one worktree and fetch cache and each kept scripts in its own prefix.
