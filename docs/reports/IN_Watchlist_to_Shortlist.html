Single-name underwriting

Laurus Labs sells APIs, finished medicines and custom development-to-commercial manufacturing services to global pharmaceutical and life-sciences customers. H1 FY27 CDMO revenue rose 71% to ₹1,774 crore, while Affordable Medicines grew 13% to ₹2,459 crore; growth was led by small molecules and FDF rather than the mature API base [2][4][5]. This mix supports earnings growth, but higher inventory and capex have kept cash flow behind EBITDA. The stock is a strong Stage 2 business with a weak entry at CMP, so the existing holding stays and additions wait for support and a fresh post-results review. If price holds near ₹2,049–₹2,149 and cash conversion improves, reassess; otherwise make no addition.

Business and results read-through

The company’s core operating advantage is its combination of process chemistry, qualified manufacturing sites and customer-specific production. Small-molecule CDMO work moves from route development and scale-up into validation and repeat campaigns; API work adds multi-step synthesis, purification, quality release and shipping; finished dosages add formulation, packaging and finished-goods delivery. The integrated platform can lower customer hand-offs, but repeat demand and batch execution must still be demonstrated project by project [5][6][12][13].

FY27 H1 shows both the opportunity and the gap. CDMO grew 71%, small molecules 77% and FDF 27%, while Bio declined 16%, API grew 2% and ARV revenue grew 2% [2]. Gross margin reached 62.9% and EBITDA margin reached 33.2%, compared with 59.6% and 25.4% in the prior half-year. However, operating cash flow fell to ₹805 crore from ₹1,093 crore, a 26% decline, as strategic inventory increased. The quarter delivered better earnings; cash conversion has not yet confirmed the same quality [2][4].

The CEO said the company continued to report “strong growth in CDMO business,” and CFO V.V. Ravi Kumar attributed profit growth to “improved operational leverage” [3][9]. The latest deck also reports more than 16,000 litres of peptide synthesis reactor volume, current API capacity above 9,000 kL and proposed acquisitions in oligonucleotides and peptide resins. These are evidence of capability investment, while customer concentration, plant utilization and contracted backlog remain unquantified in the reviewed filings [2][5][7].
