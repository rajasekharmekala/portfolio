How the underwriting gates were applied

Each layer answers a different question: is the business sound, are its operating KPIs healthy, and is the price close enough to support for a disciplined entry?

Layer 1 — hard floors

Layer 1 prevents a good story from overriding a broken price or weak economics. The financial test uses ROCE of at least 15%, debt-to-equity below 0.70 for non-financial companies, and three-year CFO/PAT of at least 0.70. The technical test requires a Stage 1 base breakout or Stage 2 markup with the relevant moving-average structure. A base is a price floor and a breakout is a move above it. The asymmetry test uses the scoring formula, not an arbitrary target: target minus entry divided by entry minus stop.

ADANIPORTS fails the standardized ROCE test at 14.1%, despite a higher company-defined measure, and its Stage 3 chart and 2.08:1 current asymmetry also fail. WABAG clears the financial floor but sits in Stage 3 below a falling or flat 50-DMA; its 3.15:1 base asymmetry misses the 3.5:1 tactical floor. CLEAN clears the prior-period financial floor but fails the current technical and momentum gate. TINNARUBBR clears its financial test, yet Stage 3, a falling 50-DMA and a failed momentum test block the allocation.

Layer 2 — operating proof

Layer 2 asks whether the business is earning the right to be reconsidered. WABAG has a large order book and net cash, but receivables, debtor days and ex-forex margin quality are watches. ADANIPORTS shows strong port margins and cargo scale, but rail volumes and consolidated ROCE definitions require caution. CLEAN’s cash conversion and new-product ramp are positive while margin evidence conflicts across sources. TINNARUBBR passes margin, capacity and value-added growth checks but remains watched on working capital and funding. None clears the full allocation case after Layer 1, so Layer 2 does not override the rejection.

What would change the decision

A technical reclaim must come with a stable or rising moving average and better relative strength. A bounce without those facts is not a pass.

Financial re-entry also needs cash conversion and margin quality to hold. The research flags receivables, foreign-exchange gains and working capital as key risks.

Use the exact triggers in each dossier. A close through its reclaim level, a result with the cited KPI improvement, or repaired cash flow can start a new review. A close below its stop moves the name farther away.

What we would do, and when

The portfolio action is to keep cash uncommitted rather than force a purchase. The price rule is simple: a rejected name must reclaim the dossier’s stated moving-average or support trigger, and its business KPIs must remain above the stated floor. A lower quote without that repair is not an entry signal.
