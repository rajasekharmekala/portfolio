Before you read

This report sums up one batch of four India names, each with a full research dossier. APL Apollo Tubes is a strong business that is priced too high for a safe entry today, so it is deferred with a limit-order plan and no purchase. Jindal Stainless and Styrenix pass the financial safety rules but sit in a long price downtrend, which the scoring rules treat as a hard stop. Suraj Limited is priced above our central value. Each dossier states the price or event that would reopen the case, and nothing here is a buy today.

What the batch found

Each row gives the verdict, the 27 October close, the stop, our central target and the reward-to-risk ratio, which is the gain to the target divided by the loss to the stop. A stop is the price at which the idea is wrong. The scoring file asks for at least 2.0 for a core name and 2.5 for a turnaround name. Read the ratio at the close together with the ratio at the top of the entry zone, because the first is what a buyer gets today and the second is what a limit order would get after a pullback.

Three rules decided these outcomes. The first is a set of hard red lines on debt, share pledge, cash conversion and a chart test called Stage 4. In Stage 4 the close sits below a falling 50-day average, and that average sits below a falling 200-day average. JSL and STYRENIX are in Stage 4 and fail the recovery test on one condition each, so the chart red line applies to them even though their finances pass. The second rule is the operating test, scored out of 5.0, where a name needs at least 3.0 and may have at most one failing row. SURAJLTD scores 2.5 and fails it. APLAPOLLO misses the 2.0 floor at the close and clears it only inside a lower price zone, SURAJLTD and STYRENIX are far below the floor, and JSL clears it only if its multiple rises from 17.5 times to 19 times earnings.

APLAPOLLO: a strong compounder, deferred because the price already pays for the good news

APL Apollo is India's largest structural steel tube maker, with about 55 to 60 percent of its segment, net cash of ₹1,628 crore, debt to equity of 0.17, a three-year cash conversion of 1.65 and a return on capital employed of 31.8 percent. The September 2026 quarter was a record, with 963,143 tonnes and ₹5,999 of operating profit per tonne. The operating test scores 3.5 of 5.0 and the one failing row is volume, which grew only 3.6 percent in the first half against 15 to 20 percent guided. The shares trade at 45.9 times trailing earnings, and the price already assumes 14.7 to 19.6 percent growth a year for ten years. The first draft said ACCUMULATE_ON_DIP because the central case clears the 2.0 floor by 0.11 at ₹2,172.82. The reviewer and the red team showed that the probability-weighted value gives 1.53, the margin one broker expects gives 1.10, and the broker multiple of 35 times gives −0.50, so the verdict was tightened to WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT. A limit order is allowed only in ₹2,055.61 to ₹2,095.00 and only after a fresh calculation passes 2.0 on all three views. The verdict changes if the December-quarter profit per tonne falls below ₹5,500 or the close falls below ₹1,927.98.

JSL: sound finances, rejected on the chart

Jindal Stainless is India's largest stainless steel maker with a return on capital employed of 19.3 percent, debt to equity of 0.36, a three-year cash conversion of 1.54 and a 0.60 percent pledge. The close of ₹709.80 sits below a falling 50-day average of ₹729.01 that sits below a falling 200-day average of ₹740.87, and the recovery test fails on one condition: the close is 1.9 percent under the 20-day average of ₹723.55. The operating test scores 3.0 of 5.0 with one failing row, the import and trade-policy shield, because a surge of Chinese imports has no duty yet. The central value of ₹859 needs a re-rating from 17.54 times to 19 times earnings, and the bear case of ₹491 is more than three times as far from the close as the stop. The rejection lifts to WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT if the price closes above ₹723.55 with a positive gain over 28 days or two weeks.

SURAJLTD: a shrinking business priced above our value

Suraj makes stainless seamless tubes and has no long-term debt, but sales fell 44 percent in three years and return on capital employed is 7.1 percent. At ₹214.70 the shares trade at 47.5 times trailing profit, and our central value of ₹155 (profit per share of ₹6.75 for FY28 at 23 times) is below the price. Even the bull case of ₹307 gives only 2.18 to 1 at the close. The operating test scores 2.5 of 5.0 with one failing row. Daily volume is about 2,100 shares, so a position could not be entered or exited cleanly. The dossier lists what would reopen the case: two straight quarters with sales of at least ₹50 crore and an operating margin of at least 14 percent, starting with the results due on 29 October 2026.

STYRENIX: a clean balance sheet, a broken chart and a collapsing quarter

Styrenix is the Indian leader in ABS, SAN and polystyrene plastics, with debt to equity of 0.40, interest cover of 12.6 times and no pledge. The close of ₹1,953.60 is in Stage 4 and the recovery test fails on one condition, so the chart red line rejects it. The central value of ₹1,972 (profit per share of ₹116 for FY28 at 17 times) gives a reward-to-risk of 0.12 at the close. After the ledger date the company reported September-quarter profit of ₹23 crore against ₹138 crore in June, and the shares traded at ₹1,802.10, below the ₹1,803.72 stop. That overlay is disclosed in the dossier and was not used for any ledger number. The case reopens only after two quarters with an operating margin above 11.5 percent and a chart base.
