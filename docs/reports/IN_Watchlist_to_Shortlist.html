The four decisions, one at a time

Each paragraph states the rule behind the verdict, the number that triggered it and the level or event that would change it. The full working is in the linked dossier. It has five-year tables, a peer comparison, growth scenarios and sources.

Interarch: replace

Interarch makes pre-engineered steel buildings. Its close of ₹1,508.80 is below a falling 50-day average of ₹1,683.77, which is below a falling 200-day average of ₹1,849.93. No base (a flat floor built before a new move) has formed. That fails the Stage 4 red line. Three-year cash conversion, which is operating cash divided by profit, is 0.35, because customers paid slowly in the last year. The probability-weighted value is only about 4 percent above the close, well under the 12 percent yearly hurdle. The plan is to sell 125 shares at a limit price of ₹1,560 or better, and the rest on a close below ₹1,447.44. The verdict reverses only on a close above ₹1,683.77, a passed chart test and half-year cash conversion of 70 percent or more.

RNIT AI Solutions: trim

RNIT AI sells face-recognition and conversational software to state governments. It has net cash and no pledged shares. At 23.35 percent of your net worth, the holding is above the 15 percent hard cap and the 22 percent ceiling for a winner you let run. Three-year cash conversion of 0.23 also fails the 0.25 red line, so the let-winners-run rule cannot apply. The base target of ₹98 is below the close, so the reward-to-risk ratio (the gain to the target divided by the loss to the stop) is negative. The plan sells 4,000 shares in two parcels, and a further 2,500 if half-year operating cash, excluding borrowings, is under ₹1 crore. The last three sessions appear to have closed at the lowest price allowed. That is inferred, not confirmed, so the sales are staged.

Sedemac Mechatronics: hold, add nothing

Sedemac makes motor-control units for two- and three-wheelers. It earns a 35 percent return on capital with little debt. But one customer is about 77 percent of sales, and the price implies about 30 percent yearly profit growth for ten years. Reward-to-risk is 0.19 to 1 at today's price, and the chart has under 200 days of history. The verdict is therefore WAIT_FOR_SUPPORT. The 14.39 percent holding is above the 6.5 percent sizing ceiling for new money. The dossier treats it as a disclosed exception. The exception ends on a close below ₹2,839, an operating margin (operating profit as a share of sales) below 17 percent, a second source for the main customer, or the first valid read of the 200-day average. New money belongs only in a limit order (an order that fills at your price or better) between ₹2,560 and ₹2,680.

Sanghvi Movers: hold, add nothing

Sanghvi Movers rents heavy-lift cranes. All hard red lines pass. Gross debt to equity is 0.51, and three-year cash conversion is 1.47 on its own accounts. The dossier scores the operating measures at 4.0 out of 5. But the chart is basing, every momentum test has failed, and the RSI (relative strength index, a 0 to 100 gauge where under 30 means a very fast fall) is 22.3. No purchase is allowed yet. The 890 shares are 6.78 percent of net worth, just above the 6.5 percent ceiling. The plan is to hold, to sell everything on a close below ₹302.16, and to consider limit orders only between ₹322.90 and ₹326.00 once the recovery tests pass.

Run notes and gate exceptions

This run published with gate exceptions. That means some checks could not be fully met with the evidence available. Each exception below names what failed, why it cannot be cured now, how it lowers confidence and what would cure it. The status of every dossier is PUBLISHED_WITH_GATE_EXCEPTIONS.

Data delay. The price feed had no 23 October 2026 bar for the Nifty 50 after one retry. All prices and technical levels therefore use the 22 October session. Some secondary pages show lower prices on 23 October, and the dossiers label those prints unverified. The cure is to rebuild the ledger once the 23 October bar posts.

Stale periodic filings. The latest periodic filing for each company is the quarter to 30 June 2026, and the latest audited year ends 31 March 2026. No September-quarter results exist yet. The citation check therefore lists many records as EXCEPTION AS-OF lines: 75 for RNIT AI, 79 for Sedemac, 33 for Interarch and 84 for Sanghvi Movers. The matching dossier lists each with its age and cure. The cure is to rerun the research once the September-quarter results and half-year cash flow are filed. They are expected by 14 November 2026, and no date is confirmed.

Validator mismatch. The shared structural validator has only market-report and portfolio-audit modes. Run on a single-company dossier, it fails ten checks built for those formats, such as case notes for all 19 holdings. The review packet code skips it for dossiers. The checks that apply to dossiers pass, and no script was modified. The readability check passes for this summary except two checks that exist only for company dossiers: the estimate working parts and a Chapter 7 with a trigger. This summary has neither, because the dossiers carry them. The cure is a dossier mode in the validator and a summary mode in the readability check.

Reviewer gate. RNIT AI received reviewer approval in round 2. Interarch, Sanghvi Movers and Sedemac received REVISE in round 1 and REVISE in round 2. Their remaining findings were corrected afterwards without a third review, under the two-round cap. Their status is BEST_EFFORT_AFTER_REVIEW_LIMIT, and they did not receive reviewer approval. The red team held the verdict for all four. This summary report was not reviewed separately, and it restates the dossier conclusions.
