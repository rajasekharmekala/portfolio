Business and results read-through

APAR makes conductors, transformer and specialty oils, and power and application-specific cables. H1 FY27 revenue grew 25.0%; EBITDA after open-period forex increased 60.2% to ₹1,602 crore and margin reached 11.8%. The ₹12,025 crore conductor order book and ₹3,012 crore cable backlog support demand. Yet H1 conductor volume fell 8.0%, Q2 conductor premium mix fell to 39.4% from 45.3%, and Q2 oil volume fell 20.0%. Higher earnings have not yet shown up in cash: H1 consolidated operating cash flow was -₹862.63 crore, while inventory rose to ₹5,055.38 crore from ₹4,152.79 crore at March. [1] [2]

The Q2 call pointed to U.S.-bound DDP shipments in transit and customer timing as reasons recognized sales lagged. That explanation can be right, but it remains a cash and delivery test. The next report should show shipments delivered, receivables collected, inventory stabilized and margins that do not depend on a short-lived commodity or mix benefit. Management’s FY27/FY28 optimism is forward guidance, not a delivered forecast. [2] [5] [6]
