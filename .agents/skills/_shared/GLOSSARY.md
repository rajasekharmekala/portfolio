# Anti-Fluff Policy: Glossaries and Report Meta-Explanations Abolished

Glossaries, `<dfn>` definition tags, and mandatory first-use definitions of standard financial or market terms (e.g. EBITDA, RSI, ATR, Moving Averages, Stage 2, etc.) are **permanently abolished and strictly prohibited** across all reports, dossiers, and briefings.

## Prohibited Patterns:
1. **No Glossary Sections:** Do NOT include a "Glossary", "Plain-English glossary", or definition list in any report, monograph, or dossier.
2. **No First-Use Definitions / `<dfn>` Wrapping:** Do NOT wrap terms in `<dfn>` or insert sentences explaining what basic financial acronyms stand for. Standard institutional terminology should be used naturally without condescending definitions.
3. **No Report Meta-Explanations / Beginner Primers:** Do NOT write primers explaining "how to read this report", everyday analogies (e.g. comparing moving averages to weather or rivers), or meta-commentary about the document structure.
4. **No Table Reading Guides:** Do NOT prepend data tables with `<p class="howto">` or "How to read this table" paragraphs.

## Mandatory Focus:
All reports and monographs must be high-signal, executive, and institutional. Jump immediately into the executive thesis, core metrics, operational analysis, financial trajectory, competitive moat, technical setups, and actionable decisions.
