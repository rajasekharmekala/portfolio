# Company-Specific KPI Selection Rules

Use 3-5 primary KPIs per company.

Separate leading indicators from lagging financial results.

Track traffic/volume separately from price when pricing can mask demand.

Track margin quality, not just earnings growth.

Prefer segment or operating metrics that map directly to the thesis.

Preserve prior-quarter values so trend is visible.

# Technical / Momentum Library

| Indicator family | Use in this system |
| --- | --- |
| Trend | 20/50/200-day moving-average structure; higher highs/lows; major trend breaks |
| Momentum | RSI, MACD, relative strength, acceleration/deceleration |
| Volume / breadth | Confirm breakouts, failures and market participation |
| Support / resistance | Define decision zones and invalidation rather than arbitrary stop levels |
| Relative strength | Compare a holding with its sector and benchmark to identify capital-efficiency deterioration |

## Support-Aware Entry Checklist

For every candidate or holding that may receive new capital, capture:

- nearest verified support and its type: base boundary, prior breakout/pivot,
  rising 20/50/200-DMA, or recent swing low;
- current price, support distance in `ATR(14)`, and whether price is
  `AT_SUPPORT`, `NEAR_SUPPORT`, `EXTENDED`, or `SUPPORT_BROKEN`;
- the conditional entry zone, stop below support plus the bucket-specific
  volatility buffer, supported target, and reward-to-risk;
- the evidence timestamp and source for every price/indicator.

Use `WATCH / WAIT FOR SUPPORT` when the business passes the research screen but
the price is extended. Use `NO TRADE / RESEARCH_REQUIRED` when support, ATR,
stop, target, or source freshness is unavailable. Support improves entry
location; it does not replace business-quality or thesis analysis.

# Decision Vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Strong / Add | Thesis, valuation and setup justify more capital |
| Healthy / Hold | Position remains attractive; no change required |
| Watch | Evidence is mixed or a decision catalyst is approaching |
| Trim / Protect Profit | Substantial gain with deteriorating trajectory or stretched risk/reward |
| Reduce | Thesis or setup weakened materially |
| Exit / Broken | Original thesis no longer supported |
