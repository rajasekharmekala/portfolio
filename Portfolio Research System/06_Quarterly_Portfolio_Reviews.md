## Entry-Discipline Review

At each quarterly review, audit whether new capital was deployed near the
planned support/MA zone or chased after extension. For every add, record the
support anchor, entry versus planned zone, stop distance, ATR buffer, and
whether the setup subsequently held or failed. Treat repeated support-chasing
or undefined downside as a process error even when the position was profitable.
