## Entry Timing Rule

Momentum strength is not permission to chase. New Tactical entries and adds
must use a verified pivot, breakout-retest, rising 20/50-DMA, or other
documented support confluence. Record the support anchor, entry zone,
`distance ÷ ATR(14)`, stop below support, target, and reward-to-risk. Prefer
entries within approximately `0.5 × ATR(14)` of support; if the move is
extended, wait for a controlled pullback or base. If support fails or any
input is unavailable, the setup is `WATCH / NO TRADE`, not an immediate buy.
