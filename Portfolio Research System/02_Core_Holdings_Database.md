## Core Entry and Add Standard

Core quality does not justify chasing price. For every initial purchase or
additional contribution, record the verified support anchor (prefer a base or
prior breakout level and the rising 50/200-DMA context), the entry zone, the
distance to support, the stop below support, and the supported target. Prefer
entries within approximately `0.5 × ATR(14)` of support. If the stock is
extended, wait; if support is broken or the required inputs are stale, use
`WATCH / NO TRADE`. A valid Core thesis can remain on `HOLD` while waiting for
an entry or add opportunity.
